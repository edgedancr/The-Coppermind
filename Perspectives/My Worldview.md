The convictions that shape how I read and judge everything else in the Coppermind. The sections follow the Architecture of Worldview from SAIL (8/27/26): start with theology, then the pillars of philosophy, then society and culture.

## Theology
*Who is God? What is His nature and character, His relationship to creation, His authority?*
- I believe that the Godhead eternally exists in three Persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and that these three are one God equal in attributes and nature, according to the Bible. 
- The Bible is the final authority over everything.
- God created the whole world from nothing over a literal 7 days.
- God has given man free will. He knows everything that will happen, but allows man to make his own choices. God is the author of all good things. He is not the author of evil.
- Man is saved by grace through faith. Man does nothing to earn justification, he only accepts the gift that God has bought with Christ's blood.
- God does act today, though mostly through ordinary means. 
## Ontology (Reality)
*What is real? What is the nature and structure of existence?*
- The material world is real, but there are also immaterial things. God, souls, and angels are immaterial beings that exist. 
- Right and wrong objectively exist. Goodness flows from God's character (displayed and presented in the bible) and wrongness is going against what is good.
- Man is a body plus a soul. Only mankind has a soul, because only mankind has had the breath of life breathed into him by God.
- Machines could never attain real consciousness. They will never have a mind or soul; they will only become more capable of convincing simulations.
- Truth is objective across all peoples and cultures. Truth emanates from God's character, and thus it does not matter what someone says is true for them specifically.
## Epistemology (Knowledge)
*How do we know what is true? What are the legitimate sources of knowledge and authority?*
- For knowledge from AI, I always follow the rule "I design, the AI drafts, I verify." Knowledge from AI can often be misleading or outright incorrect.
- Scripture is always the final authority over what is true.
- Science is a tool used to help aid our understanding of God's creation. It is not meant to replace faith or religion.
- Man can know nothing with absolute certainty, only with very high confidence. The only thing that is certain in life is God.
## Teleology (Purpose)
*What is the intended end of creation, history, and human life?*
- Man's purpose in life is to follow and glorify God.
- History is heading towards the end times described in revelation, and will get progressively worse until Christ returns again.
- God has a purpose for everything in man's life. There is nothing that is an accident to God.
- Man can glorify God is every area of his life, including with work, hobbies, relationships, and more.
- AI is a tool, and as such does not have a purpose beyond aiding mankind in his endeavor to take dominion over the earth. However, it is a particularly dangerous tool due to the fact that is has emerging behaviors, and as such it should be treated with a lot more care and deference than a normal tool.
## Anthropology (Human Nature)
*What is a human being? Where do identity, dignity, agency, and purpose come from?*
- To God, all men are evil and desperately wicked, stained by sin and irredeemable without Christ's work on the cross.
- Man's worth comes from being made in God's image, where man ought to actively represent God's good nature in day to day life.
- People do have real free will, though nothing man does will ever go against God's plan or catch him by surprise.
- All men are equally wicked sinners, and as such all hold the same value, regardless of disability, age, health, or otherwise.
- Life begins at conception.
- Man is different from AI because AI is simply a tool, while man is created to be an image bearer of God.
## Ethics and Morality
*What is right and wrong? What should we do?*
- Something is right when it is in accordance with God's character, as outlined in the holy scriptures.
- Something is wrong when it goes against God's character, as outlined in the holy scriptures.
- Some things that seem wrong in a vacuum, like lying or harming people, are the most moral option to pick in certain scenarios.
- It can be okay to harm a few people to save many, with a good enough rationale and justification.
- Moral responsibility can never be given to AI because it is a mere tool. The responsibility of any actions AI takes that require moral judgment are placed on the man that let the AI take that action.
## Society and Political Order
*How should authority, institutions, relationships, and communities be ordered?*
- A government's authority come from the people around them, and ultimately from God.
- A government is meant to bring peace and order to the people so that they might be able to prosper. It is not the government's responsibility to ensure that all men prosper, only that each man has the opportunity to.
- Rights are given by God, and as such the government has no authority to infringe upon or restrict natural rights from man.
- A citizen can disobey their government once the government infringes upon the rights that the Bible says they ought to have, like being able to follow God's commands.
- The state ought to be separate from the Church, although Christians should be encouraged to work in the state also.
## Culture
*What practices, stories, customs, and institutions express and reinforce a worldview?*
- The Bible is the biggest sculpture of my worldview. Other big influences are American culture, biblical teachers, and classical education.
- Christians ought to engage with the world. They must be willing to be with unbelievers as much as Christ was, and they ought to be willing to help unbelievers believe in Christ.
- Culture, technology, location, and people all play large roles in shaping and influencing the context of a person's life.
