##### Learning Methods (Deep Learning)
- Supervised learning
	- Key Idea
		- Learn a mapping (x ➔ y)
	- Typical tasks
- Unsupervised learning
	- Key Idea
		- Find a hidden structure in x
	- Typical tasks
		- Clustering
		- Dimensionality reduction
- Reinforcement Learning
	- Key Idea
		- Learn a policy π(a | s) that maximized long-term reward
	- Typical Tasks
		- Sequential decision making
		- Control problems
- **==Deep learning==**
	- Key Idea
		- Learns implicitly
	- Examples (Tasks)
		- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), (images)
		- RNNs / Transformers (text)
	- Timeline
		- ImageNet Competition — The Big Bang of Modern AI: 2012
			- Deep Learning unlocked by combined breakthrough in three areas
				- CNNs
					- Special ANNs that could be tailored to specific kinds of pattern recognition
				- GPUs
					- Made it computationally feasible to train on very large datasets
				- Reinforcement Learning
					- System learned through pure trial and error rather than trained datasets
		- AlphaGo — When AI Learned Strategy: 2016
			- Setting the Stage
				- Go is an ancient board game, with more positions than atoms in the universe (2.08 x 10^170)
			- The Plot Twist
				- 2016 — AlphaGo wins by combining DL (recognize patterns in board positions) with RL (improve through self-play and rewards)
			- Why it matters
				- Demonstrated AI could learn strategic intuition, not just rules
					- Wasn't real human "intuition," but the closest thing an algorithm could get.
					- It would adapt to a player's move
				- Showed power of **learning representation + learning actions**
	- Why "Deep" matters
		- Depth allows progressive abstraction
			- Simple Representations
				- edges in images
				- tones in audio
				- word fragments in text
			- Combinations
				- shapes
				- phonemes
				- phrases
			- High Level Concepts
				- Objects (faces, cars, etc...)
				- Words and meanings
				- Strategies or intents
	- Deep Learning shifted from Discriminative AI to Generative AI
##### 3Blue1Brown video — "Transformers, the tech behind LLMs"
- GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer). Nothing to do with Open AI specifically
	- LMMs are a text-driven GPT
		- They work by using their training data to work off of a prompt to find the most likely next word.
	- Transformers will grab tokens, make words, and map them in a vector space, and use vector math to find the correct answer.
		- ==Tokens==: The smallest chunk of text that carries meaning
			- An AI will read your sentences as tokens.
		- They will put your prompt through EVERYTHING in their database. With low effort models, that won't take as many tokens. With the most powerful ChatGPT model, that's against 140 billion pieces of data, thus costing more tokens and computational power.
	- Embedding
		- Taking a token and making a vector out of it.
		- 12,288 dimensions
