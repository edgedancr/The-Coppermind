##### Why AI Matters
>*For your job, your country, your world*
- What is AI?
	- *The application of mathematics and software code to teach computers how to understand, synthesize, and generate knowledge in ways similar to how people do it... A way to make everything we care about better. - Marc Andreessen* 
	- *What really matters is competence in completing a prescribed task, not consciousness of what that task happens to be. The machine may not be conscious in the same way we are, but it is programmed to respond cognitively in the ways that we do. In short, **==it acts like a human being, it does not think or feel like a human being.==** Therefore, intelligence can be thought of informally as the capacity to solve problems, whereas consciousness is the capacity to have subjective feelings and experiences (qualia). -Alan Turing*
- AGI and Predictions of AI
	- AGI is an AI that can do every human task better than every human can do it.
	- Sci-Fi vs Reality
		- The danger isn't that the AI "wants" something — It's that powerful systems pursue goals without sufficient human control.
	- The Singularity
		- AGI is developing faster than man can track it or stop it.
	- Existential Risk (x-risk)
		- When AGI decides to kill all humans because they aren't necessary.
- The PI-shaped Man
	- You know a little about a lot of things, making yourself able to connect in multiple contexts.
	- You also have to know a lot about something.
	- "A pi-shaped human is a high agency generalist who has a curiosity driven research mindset with a fearless entrepreneurial attitude."
	- You need to have the ability to get up and move towards your goal and make it real. 
	- ==**Scan wide, dig deep, build out.**==
- Human Centered AI
	- *"Human-Centered AI (HCAI) is an emerging discipline intent on creating AI systems that amplify and augment rather than displace human abilities. HCAI seeks to preserve human control in a way that ensures artificial intelligence meets our needs while also operating transparently, delivering equitable outcomes, and respecting privacy." -IBM, 2022*
	- Work as a human—AI team: augmentation, not abdication.
	- **Human** retains judgment, responsibility, context, ethics, and final decision authority, while **AI** expands speed, pattern-finding, drafting, coding, analysis, and access to information.
- Centaur professionals
	- A strong centaur professional typically develops five capabilities:
		1. Domain Master: Enough substantive expertise to recognize bas assumptions, missing constraints, unsafe recommendations, and fabricated claims.
		2. AI literacy: A practical understanding of what a model is good at, what it cannot verify, how prompting and context affect output, and where bias or hallucination can enter.
		3. Workflow Design: The ability to decide which parts of a task should remain human-led, which can be AI-assisted, and which should be automated with guardrails.
		4. Verification discipline: Source checking, independent calculation, test/validation, version control, provenance, and documented assumptions.
		5. Human judgement.

- **ODNI AI Ethics Framework for the Intelligence Community.**
	- Misconceptions about AI
		1. "AI is just a tool"
			- **Many people imagine AI the way they imagine a car or a calculator:** a useful, *predictable machine that does what we tell it to do.* But a car designer knows exactly how a steering wheel will behave. A calculator cannot mimic human emotion or produce false information with total conviction.
			- **It is a tool, but it is fundamentally different.** It learns patterns from enormous amounts of data. It can generate ideas, arguments, essays, and emotional language without *any human-like understanding.*
			- **The comparison to previous inventions is not accurate.** A car builder in 1900 understood the basic form an limits of what they were creating. Today's AI developers openly admit they do not fully understand how these models reach certain outputs, and they cannot predict all failure modes. That is not "just a tool." **==AI is a system with emerging behaviors.==**
			- No longer do you need to learn the language to converse with the machine. The machine has learned our language in order that we may converse with it easier.
		2. "AI is Neutral"
			- AI is trained on vast amounts of text created by humans and filled with human biases.
			- It's trained on the internet. The internet is stupid.
- Case Study: Mata v. Avianca (2023)
	- A lawyer used ChatGPT to help write a legal brief. The filing citated prior cases to support the argument, including Varghese v. China Southern Airlines. This case did not exist.
	- ChatGPT generated a hallucinated citation—a fabricated legal case that *looked* real:
		- A plausible plaintiff name: Varghese
		- A real airline: China Southern Airlines
		- Proper legal citation formatting
	- The model was not retrieving legal records—it was filling in the gaps of what it needed with fiction.
	- Why this matters
		- This case is not just about AI making a mistake, it's about over-trusting and mis-using generative AI.
		- Generative AI systems are powerful tools, not **authoritative sources.** they can produce fluent, confident output even when the content is false.
		- Using such tools in high-risk professional settings without verification exposes others to real harm. Using AI without understanding its limits is not innovation; it is risk transfer.
	- **Key Lesson: Capability vs. Reliability**
		- **Capability**: AI can generate professional-quality legal language.
		- **Reliability**: AI cannot be trusted to verify facts or sources on its own. Confusing capability for reliability leads to misuse.
		- Application: We must ALWAYS think, calculate, then verify.
		- We **MUST** become competent with our use of AI, especially because we are entering high-stakes fields. Laziness and negligence cannot be tolerated in the use of AI.
