##### The Tokenization of Everything
- Text, Images, and Audio are all converted into Vectors. To the model, a pixel is just another word. It doesn't have to be just language.
- Tokenization requires us to understand the meaning of the token.
	- Thus, tokenization is a gross approximation of meaning, because human's do not understand everything.
	- What would tokenizing a soul look like?
- It is theoretically possible to tokenize everything, but it would require omniscience.

##### Original Transformer Architecture
- Encoder-Decoder for Language Translation
	- Think English ➔ numbers + numbers ➔ Spanish

##### Attention in Transformers (3Blue1Brown video)
- Vectors for tokens are the same, regardless of the context. A token is plotted on a vector on its own.
- A single head of attention is when you only use one thing your comparing a token against. (e.g., adjectives). Each one you put it against is another attention head. 
- A query vector is the thing that go's and find all the things that match the attention head.

##### Hallucination
- It is a feature, not a bug
- Hallucination just means that it got the meaning of a word wrong. It goes and searches for meanings, and we cannot always know if the meaning it finds is the meaning we are looking for.
