##### SAIL Portfolio / LLM Wiki
- Usually called an LLM Wiki, but we are trying to do something slightly different.
- What is a portfolio?
	- A showcase and productivity tool: a growing, interlinked knowledge base that you develop and maintain using an AI agent, and keep using long after this course ends.
	- The whole wiki is your knowledge garden. It holds:
		- Your own work
		- Outside sources
		- Our perspective and worldview.
- Karpathy's LLM Wiki, with one fix
	- Karpathy's version
		- Instead of asking an AI the same questions over and over, it compiles what  you learn into linked pages that compound over time.
		- The AI does all the writing, modifying, and promoting to the baseline.
		- **Often criticized for compounding hallucinations.**
	- The SAIL version
		- Built on the course's think-calculate-verify loop.
		- **You design. The AI drafts. You Verify.**
		- Nothing becomes part of your wiki until you have reviewed it and approved it.
	- Why build it this way?
		1. A productivity tool for life
			- Build it to help you in later courses, apprenticeships, and your personal and professional life
		2. A showcase of your work and productivity
			- Employers, coworkers, and mentors should be able to see what you know and how you think.
		3. A record of your developing perspective
			- Your work, opinions, and worldview sit beside outside sources and show how your thinking grows.
- **Grading Criteria:** It will be graded mainly by watching us build and run the wiki.
- Parts of a Wiki
	- Sources: *You collect*
		- Your own work and outside material, kept in original form.
	- Wiki pages: *AI drafts; You verify*
		- Concept, topic, person, and summary pages, linked and cited.
	- ==Schema:== *You direct*
		- The rulebook your agent follows.
	- Index: *AI maintains; You check*
		- A map of every page for people and agents
	- Log: *AI maintains; you check*
		- A dated record of every action and decision
	- Perspectives: *You write*
		- Your reflections, opinions, and conclusions
- The most important thing you build
	- It turns a general -purpose AI into your research assistant, and it is the clearest evidence of how you think about organizing knowledge.
