##### Where we use AI: Business
- Narrow AI
	- Narrow AI is about doing a specific task, or set of tasks, really well.
	- Already everywhere: spam filters, recommendations, navigation, medical image screening.
	- On the near horizon: drug and protein design, self-driving logistics, AI agents at work.
	- Negative outlooks
		- Jobs: many claim AI is replacing "thinking" work, not just manual labor.
		- Education: essays, writing, and cognitive load. What happens when we outsource thinking?
		- Using AI as a therapist and dating AI: mistaking language for connection.
		- Autonomous weapons: who decides when a machine may take a life?
	- Big Brother meets Big Data
		- Surveillance capitalism uses day to predict and influence; state surveillance uses it to control.
		- Privacy is a condition of autonomy, not a minor consumer preference.
		- The West is not immune: data brokers and facial recognition are building similar tools.
		- Deepfakes: when video and audio can be forged, what still counts as evidence?
- Agentic AI
	- An AI that has access to external tools.
		- Access to external tools increases emergent behavior and risk.
- The people that will benefit from AI the most are those who can both engineer and design. Those who can do neither will be replaced by AI.
