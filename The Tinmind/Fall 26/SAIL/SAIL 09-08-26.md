##### Essays
- Definitions
	- Argumentative Essays
		- Defend a position with evidence
	- Descriptive Essays
		- Create a clear picture of a subject or experience
	- Expository Essays
		- Explain or inform
	- Narrative Essays
		- Tell a meaningful story
	- Analytical Essay
		- Examine how or why something works
		- This is the most common type of essay in SI, sometimes framed through a narrative lens. 
	- Comparative Essay
		- Identify meaningful similarities and differences
- The topic sentence for each paragraph should be able to be put together into a mini essay that explains your whole argument. 
##### Machine Learning
- What is AI?
	- What does it mean for AI to be artificial?
		- Created by man
		- Implies imitation or absence of naturalness
		- A human skill or craft made real
	- What does it mean for AI to be intelligent?
		- Intelligence is the capacity to solve problems.
- Statistics ➔ Machine Learning ➔ Deep Learning ➔ Reinforcement Learning ➔ Generative AI ➔ Agentic AI.
	1. Statistics
		- Putting data onto each other and finding correlations.
	2. Machine Learning
		- Markov Chains, Artificial Neuron (McCulloch & Pitts). Trying every path until one happens to be correct, then replicating it.
- AI uses both Example and Enthymeme (inductive and deductive) reasoning.
- Two types of problems in the world
	- Classification
		- What is it? What are its qualities and categories?
		- Decision trees, ChatGPT, Email spam detection, fraud detection
		- Old fashioned AI requires rules in order to classify. Lots of specific rules, so many more than is practical to replicate what modern LLMs can do.
	- Regression
		- How much? What are its quantities and measurements
		- Linear regression, neural networks, price forecasting, weather forecasting.
- AI Scaling:
	- Multiplicity, space bound 
		- Examples: data
			- More rows of data
		- Diversity: model parameters
			- Ensembles of models
	- Complexity, time bound
		- Breadth: more Features
			- More columns of data
		- Depth: bigger brains
	- We are already good at scaling Machine Learning now. The fundamental limitation for most ML techniques is good data. If you have the data, you can already extract so much value.
	- Learning Methods
		- Supervised learning
			- Learn to predict outputs based on labeled data.
		- Unsupervised learning
			- Learns without a labeled training data set.
		- Reinforcement Learning
			- Learns to behave in specific situation based on feedback (actions ➔ results)
		- Deep Learning
			- later
	- LLMs are just ML with a LOT of parameters. It was randomly created, with about 140 Billion parameters. We do not know why exactly this random collection of parameters work as well as they do. We cannot reverse engineer AI.
	- Effective systems match how much of the world they see with how richly they can describe it.
	- Minimizing the cost function
		- Gradient descent points to the fastest direction to minimize local error.
