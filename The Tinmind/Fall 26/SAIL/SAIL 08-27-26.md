- Three-Ps Risk Framework
	- Risk of technical failures, individual harm or bias, and societal impact.
	- AI technology scales very quickly, 
	- Multi-level risk assessment
		- Program
			- There is a failure at the program level; AI hallucinations are a program risk
		- Person
			- A failure at the personal level, like when it affects you as a person.
		- Population
			- When we start seeing a large group of people dealing with this issue; masses using AI as a therapist, romantic partner, etc...
	- Considers
		- Intended consequences vs. unintended consequences
			- If it works, what will happen? If it doesn't work, what will happen?
	- Complements
		- Multi-dimensional Frameworks (e.g. ODNI AI Ethics Principles)
	- Inherent in any assessment
		- Morality
		- Ethics
		- Values and Biases
			- Everyone has biases. As Christians, we know that our biases are rooted in reality and truth, since they come from God.
	- Definition
		- Population: The Societal Scale
			- Examines how broad deployment impacts groups, institutions, and existing social inequalities.
			- Vulnerability (the cause)
				- Regulatory gaps or power concentration
			- Risk (the outcome)
				- Systemic bias and economic disruption
		- Person: The Human Experience
			- Evaluates how individuals are affected by AI interactions, focusing on autonomy and privacy.
			- Vulnerability (the cause)
				- Low AI literacy or lack of consent
			- Risk (the outcome)
				- Discrimination and privacy loss
		- Program: The Technical Core
			- Focuses on system design, training data, and technical vulnerabilities like model opacity.
			- Vulnerability (the cause)
				- Biased data or model opacity
			- Risk (the outcome)
				- Hallucinations and manipulations
- The Architecture of Worldview
	- Theology
		- Who is God? What is his nature & character? What is His relationship to creation? How much authority does He have?
		- Theology is the invisible core around which all existence is ordered and animated.
	- Ontology (Reality)
		- What is real? 
		- The nature of structure of existence.
	- Epistemology (Knowledge)
		- How do we know what is true? 
		- Sources of knowledge and legitimate authority.
	- Teleology (Purpose)
		- What is the intended end? 
		- The trajectory of creation, history, and human life.
	- Anthropology (Human Nature)
		- What is a human being?
		- The source of identity, dignity, agency, and purpose.
	- Ethics and Morality
		- What is right and wrong? What should we do?
	- Societal and Political Organization
		- How should authority, institutions, relationships, and communities be ordered?
	- Cultural Norms and Symbols.
		- What practices, stories, customs, and institutions express and reinforce the underlying worldview?
- Imago Dei
	- What does it mean to be made in the image of God?
	- This matters because it is what we are protecting, or potentially violating in our line of work. We must understand what we are working with when talking about risking humanity, especially in the context of AI.
	- God is:
		- A personal being. 
			- Each person has a personal center of identity, often associated with the soul or mind. Personhood is the basis of consciousness, personal memory, self-consciousness, self-directedness, purpose and intentionality. 
		- A spiritual being
			- God is a spirit and likewise mankind is also created a spirit-being with an innate inclination to worship Him in spirit and truth.
		- A relational being
			- Humans are social beings. We are not just capable of relating; we need relationships. We find fulfillment only when we are properly related to God and to other humans, the tasks which Jesus described as the first and second greatest commandments.
		- A rational/creative being
			- God created us with minds and reason. When we exercise rationality and creativity, we are reflecting the nature of God.
		- A volitional/free being
			- God is free and sovereign, and He creates humans with freewill. this is one of the most precious but dangerous gifts to humankind, as it gives humans possibility of rebelling against God.
		- A moral being
		- A responsible being
		- An emotional being

- "Your Brain on ChatGPT"
	- We MUST learn how to think for ourselves.
		- LLMs help improve your cognitive abilities, but only after you already have given it your full thought.
		- Relying on LLMs first will give you a cognitive debt that will permanently reduce your capacity for thinking on your own.
- Fundamentals of Prompt Engineering
	- Explain exactly what you need and the context of the situation.
	- The AI will respond from the viewpoint that you give it, but if you don't give it one the answer will be very broad and largely unhelpful.
