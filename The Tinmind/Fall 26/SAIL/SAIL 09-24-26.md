##### What Our Tools Are 1
- The "Centaur" Analyst
	- The shift from Automation (replacing humans) to Augmentation (enhancing humans)
	- The "Big Three" tasks of the Analyst
		1. Summarization
		2. Explanation
		3. Synthesis
	- The roles of the analyst
		- The old role of the analyst
			1. Drafting
			2. Summarizing
			3. Synthesizing
		- The new role of the analyst
			1. Steering
			2. Integrating
			3. Verifying
		- The future role of the analyst (which is now)
			1. Orchestrating
			2. Verifying
			3. Evaluating
- Think Calculate Verify Loop
	- System 1 thinking
		- Fast, instinctive, error-prone (AI)
	- System 2 thinking
		- Slow, deliberate, rational (human)
		- Big models like Opus are trying to simulate system 2 thinking.
