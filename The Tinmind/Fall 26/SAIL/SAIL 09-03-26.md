##### Reality Check for AI
- AI will never attain consciousness. We know this because it is not made in the image of God. It was created in the image of man, and man cannot breathe life into it.
- However, AI is already operational, outpacing humans, and introducing risks from its scale and dependency. 
- Treating AI as if it is a human makes you reliant or dependent upon AI, and you won't care. It could become akin to a highly addictive drug. 
##### Prompt Engineering
- Four Levels of Prompt Engineering
	1. NPC
		- "Ask a simple question, get a simple answer."
	2. Mid
		- "Back and forth with the AI on format and style; not reading or challenging the content, just using the tools to make a better-looking product."
	3. Boss
		- Back and forth with AI on ideas; reading, comprehending, challenging, analyzing, refining.
	4. Legendary
		- Back and forth with AI on context, approach, and ideas; reading comprehending, challenging, organizing, analyzing, optimizing, and refining; fully using TCV Loop.
- You MUST feed AI the worldview you want it to have. Do not assume that by telling it "think with a Christian worldview" it will think like you do.
