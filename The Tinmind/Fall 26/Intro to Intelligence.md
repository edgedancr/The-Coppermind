## Assignment Notes
- Be early. To everything.
- ==Midterm==
	- The midterm will be the Intelligence Organizations--Missions/Functions sheet. Be able to reproduce it from memory.
	- Know the grammar terms for the midterm. Col. Middleton will give definitions for the terms a week or so before the midterm.
	- Learn the Military Rank and Insignias from the document on Canvas.
	- Open Book Portion
		- Use class notes, handouts, canvas materials, etc...
		- May also use open search like google, but not AI.
	- Closed Book Portion
		- 2.5 hours, take it at home.
	- Submit both portions together in the closed book submission.
- ==Final==
	- Student Teams will:
		1. Identify emerging intelligence challenges for countering cognitive/information threats involving surveillance data, social media, narrative intelligence, influence operations, and information operations. Students are ==required to use AI== as a bounded exploratory assistant for this part of the assignment.
		2. Assess the priority of the challenges of these future cognitive/information threats and with the final decisions made by applying human moral judgement.
		3. Identify ethical tensions associated with responding to those challenges due to Christian worldview and Constitutional constraints, limitations, or boundaries.
	- Cognitive/information threats
- You will need to make a profile on two of the directors of intelligence agencies. Use the format and the templates that are provided, do not try to be creative with it. This is a detailed, analytical analysis. 
- On Grammar questions, most of them are primarily looking for quotes from the Lowenthal book. Just repeat what the book said.
- Every other week you need to write an article review. It is not a team collaboration assignment, it is only assigned by teams. Do an individual assignment.
- Grammar and article reviews are graded on good faith effort.
- ==Look at the teaching resources, they are extremely helpful for assignments.==
