- The Republic
	- It took place within Athens
	- Primary topic: what is justice? What is a universal standard of justice?
	- Many sophists (wise people) who were very good at debating, but Socrates distinguished himself by calling himself a philosopher (Lover of Wisdom).
	- Thrasymachus is a sophist. (an arrow from here down to "Says justice is…")
	- What is Human reasoning? Do humans reason?
	- Says justice is whatever is advantageous for the stronger.
		- There would be no morality
		- Justice is only emotion, whatever the person feels, <u>subjective</u>
		- The most just person would be the most brutal.
	- Is ruling a craft? (Yes) The product must be cared for for it to be useful, so the ruler must care for his people.
	- Something is only something if it is that thing successfully.
	- Good people will not rule for money or honor. A city of Good men would fight not to rule.
	- Thrasymachus is forced to change his stance to saying <u>injustice is happier</u>. (348d, 343b-c)
