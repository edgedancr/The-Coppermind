**The Cave**
- The people are dragged by force out of the cave. They don't want to leave naturally
- The laws we have are shadows of justice, like the shadows in the cave
- The good is permanent, while everything in this world is subject to death & decay.
- Education is a turning around of the soul, from this world to the intelligible one (forms)
- <u>In</u> theory, the philosopher King is the least happy person of the city.
- There needs to be a common good agreed on by all, which requires a common mind.
- Love of wisdom means that there is harmony between the appetites, spirit, & reason. You cannot have any one of them rule on their own.
