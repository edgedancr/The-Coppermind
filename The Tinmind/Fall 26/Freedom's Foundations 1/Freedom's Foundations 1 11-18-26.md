Robespierre & Condorcet
- Totalitarianism is popular; the idea that everything can be overturned.
- Condorcet: Man is changing. A Democratic change will help make man more perfect and more moral.
- The more freedom of thought there is, the more man will be able to progress. The Church & State should not cover the mouths of the people.
- Equality of human nature; marxist view of human nature.
- Robespierre is acting as the voice of the people. He wants to remove all institutions in order to liberate the people. True justice & morality will shine forth once the institutions are abolished.
- Public morality must shine beyond private interests.
- Terror is okay when the ones holding the swords are "virtuous." It is justified as a means to an end.
- Must kill all those that oppress humanity
- Invents a "Supreme Being" that gives them the right to do everything they are doing
