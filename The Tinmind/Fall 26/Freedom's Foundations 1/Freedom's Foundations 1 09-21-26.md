- The person who is the worst friend to himself is the tyrant.
- The Tyrannical soul is the soul of the madness or irrationality. He is unordered and has lost his reason.
- (margin: 571-572) Everyone has lawless appetites, sometimes showing up in dreams.
- When there are many people like this in a city, the biggest among them will rise to power.
- The Tyrannical soul is governed by fear & unhappiness. He is scared of everyone because he has no friends. He is a slave to those he enslaves.
- (margin: 580a) The good life is that where one rules himself.
- All ~~good things and~~ parts of the soul have pleasure. The good life is when one gets the right amount of pleasure for each; no more, no less.
- (margin: 586?) True knowledge of the soul is how one finds the good life.
- (margin: 588a) Doing injustice does not profit the one who is injust
- There's an order to unlawful appetites.
