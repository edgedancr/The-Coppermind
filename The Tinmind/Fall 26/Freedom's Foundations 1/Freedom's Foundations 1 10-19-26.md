Hooker
- Reason gives us cause to choose the best rulers.
- Government is based on the natural sociability of man.
	- Created by societies for their benefit.
	- Nature provides the laws and statutes
- Societies have:
	- Natural inclination
	- agreed upon morals/laws
- Government is to procure peace, tranquility, and happiness.
- Monarch is the best security for a society if they consent to them.
- Positive law has a power that natural law does not.
	- By nature, man has no power over another man. That requires consent, which is through positive law. The consent can be tacet (?)
- The government has a forceful power that must be used to keep its people in check.
- The problem is that humans will get the government they deserve.
