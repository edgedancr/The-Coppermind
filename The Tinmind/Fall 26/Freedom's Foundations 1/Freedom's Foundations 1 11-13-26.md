- Review Quiz on Rousseau
	- Man has 3 natural faculties
		- Self-preservation
		- Pity
		- Free-will, which is what distinguishes man from animals.
	- Man naturally pre-moral, not good or evil.
	- Reason is the thing that engenders egocentrism & inequality
	- More free-will leads to more perfectability, → civilization, → evil, bad for man

Rousseau, The Social Contract
- The social contract thinkers need to define a state of nature for man
	- Because the city is different than nature, but reflects the soul of man
- Natural Right comes from man's nature, while natural law comes from God.
- (margin: Ch. 1) "Man is born free, and everywhere he is in chains"
- The social order gives man their rights.
- (margin: Ch. 2) All men are born free & equal.
- Family is important, but everyone chooses to be a part of it.
- ★ Force does not produce political right. People that are forced to obey are not giving the right to rule to the tyrant.

(a wavy line across the page)

- (margin: (Ch. 4?)) War comes from disagreements between states. If there were no property, there would be no war.
- (margin: Ch. 5) There must be a first, fundamental agreement in a state, like a constitution. A foundation that all agree on.
- Forming an association with others necessarily revokes personal liberty.
- (margin: Ch. 6 ★) You must set up a contract that defends and protects the good & liberty of its participants.
- By giving yourself to all, you are giving yourself to no one.
- The people have a general will, a common life. Everyone is both a citizen and a subject.
- All people are both
	- A member of the people under the sovereign
	- A member of the sovereign over the people.
- Each man can have a private will contrary to the general will, but the contract only cares about the general will.
