FF1 C2
- Ethics is the head of order & law.
- The individual & social order are linked. A bad individual can upset the social order, & vice versa.
- Any order, even a bad one, is better than no order.
- Ideology: means to lead people with very little political thought. It is following an idea without understanding much of the idea. It is a simplifier.
- Lots of the more ideological "isms" like Marxism, communism, etc..., upheave the social order and ideas like freedom & justice.
- Greeks sort of started the art of government, where they were writing constitutions and having more structured societies.
- Greeks were much more concerned about eternal, unknown things, which is a reason they don't progress well.
- Hubris was their downfall. They thought that they had it all.
- Their gods failed to teach them humility. Polytheism left them without social & moral order.
- They lost their prudence.
