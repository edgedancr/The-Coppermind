<u>Richard Hooker</u>
- A little after Calvin.
- the task of the conservitive is difficult, bc they must study the past.
- It's necessary to uncover the people's roots so that they understand <u>why</u> they are there.
- Law: what work each thing can do, how it is restrained, and how it can function
	- everything uses laws, even God himself.
- We cannot know God as He knows Himself. We only know that He is greater than we can know.
- Eternal laws:
	1. First Eternal law is the Holy Spirit.
	2. Providential governance of Creation. – First cause on which all of creation depends.
- God does only those things which left undone would not be good. Everything He does has a purpose.
- The freedom of God is not impaired; He binds us into being.
- Laws of nature by which natural agents work:
	- Eternal law is <u>natural law</u> when applied to nature
	- When Eternal law is applied to angels, it is <u>Celestial law</u>
	- When Eternal law is applied to rational beings, it is called <u>Rational Law</u>
	- When Eternal law is appied to rational beings about the divine beings (revelation), it is called <u>Divine law</u>
	- When Eternal law is applied to rational & divine law, it is <u>human law</u>
- Law & Reason: how do they fit together?
- (margin: Angels, with an arrow) Angels act in 3 ways
	- Mirroring God's love; acting in love bc of the love they see from God.
	- Seeing God more closely than we do
	- Knowing God more closely than we do.
- Angels can act in a corporation (like an army)
- Fallen angels sin bc they turn away from God and love themselves.
	- Doing the opposite of their calling.
