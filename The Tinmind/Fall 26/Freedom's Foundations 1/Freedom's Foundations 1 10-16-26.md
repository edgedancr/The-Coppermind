Hooker
- <u>All</u> things in the world seeks the highest, but man does so the most.
- Everything is designed by God to work in an order.
- We have a desire for perfection, bc we are under God.
- Having practical experience gives more knowledge than pure theory.
- Man's will → action → guides to moral action.
- Once we see what is good, we cannot help but want to do good. We could just have an observed idea of what is good.
- We must use the will to control the appetite. Reason bounds the will to what is possible (or reasonable).
- We sin bc we mistake false goods for true goods.
- Knowing what is good helps you know what is wrong. You don't have to be evil to know its bad. The path to righteousness is not naive.
- A law shows man how to act well.
- Self-evident laws, like greater good > lesser goods.
