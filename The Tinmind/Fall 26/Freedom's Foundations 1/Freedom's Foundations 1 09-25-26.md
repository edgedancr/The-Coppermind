Aristotle Ethics
- Legislation should attempt to bring friendship instead of focusing on laws & enforcement.
- Friendship is a virtue. Everyone needs friends.
	- The Tyrant has neither.
- (margin: 1155a 22) Friendship holds the city together
- the good, pleasent, & useful are good ends, loveable
- Do people love the good in itself or because it appears good for him?
- Different types of friendships (people you need things from):
	- (margin: useful) Utility: Doctors, lawyers, etc... The fundamental/bottom line friendship in a city.
	- (margin: Pleasent) Pleasure: Friend, Temporary, not used to achieve any means.
		- (margin: 1156b 1) Young people especially use this.
	- (margin: good.) Complete/Perfect: People who are similar in virtue. They are seeking the good of the other and themself.
- (margin: 1157b 7) Some people are good in charachter as a state, others in an activity.
- Friendly activities bridge the gap between goodwill & friendship (activity and state of living)
- (margin: book 10?) Friendships of inequality (Father → son, husband → wife, older → younger)
- (margin: 1160a 8) Political friendships work for the common advantage of justice
- (margin: 1160a 30) 3 different forms of political systems & deviations from them.
	- (margin: Best; son → father) Kingship, rule of 1. → Tyrany. Good when King is for others, bad when for his own interest. Worst deviation. No friendship.
	- (margin: better) Aristocracy, rule of best few. → Oligarchy (rule of the rich for themselves.)
	- (margin: Worst) Timocracy / Polity (Athens under Solon), rule of all/many. → Democracy (rule of the mob; Athens). Least bad of all the deviations, as they are so similar
- ★ Friendship & Justice are aligned.
	- The 3 types of friendships are descriptors of political communities.
