Burke, Reflections on the revolutions in France
- Rational Liberty. Manly, moral, and regulated liberty. Different than the liberty of the French revolutionists.
- Liberty is practical, not just abstract. It should be under law.
- (margin: p. 119) Rights came from Legal tradition. Good political systems are judged by experience.
- Magna Carta was the begining point of this legal tradition
- Liberty has been made an entailed inheritance – It cannot be sold.
- (margin: p. 122) Civil liberty follows nature, meaning that when there are equal parts young, middle-aged, and old, change is inevitable.
- Thinks the French Revolution will not end with a good government.
- Wants a constitution that will continue to exist over generations.
