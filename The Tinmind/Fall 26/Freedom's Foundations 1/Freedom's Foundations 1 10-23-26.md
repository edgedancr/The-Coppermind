The Prince
- (margin: Ch 12) Mercenaries are not secure.
- your army cannot hate you. Fearing you is okay, but trusting you is best.
- (margin: Ch 15) Thinks Plato's Republic is stupid bc it is not realistic at all, and not helpful.
- Skeptic of Christian morals
- The ruler's reputation of goodness matters more than actually being good.
- (margin: Ch 16) You cannot make your people hate you; you will lose your rulership. You can't control if they love you, so make sure they at least don't hate you.
- (margin: Ch 18) If it is beneficial, then it is good to break your word.
- (margin: Ch. 22) Don't have flatterers around you.
- Fortune determines half of your actions, so be prudent, knowing that things just happen some times.
- Religion is only a useful tool, not true. religious men are easier to lead.
