Hobbes, Leviathan (1588–1679)
- Scientific revolution. (Descartes, Galileo, etc...)
- Materialist, Empericist, Secular
- He's in a world where there are many religious disagreements, and no real unity in beliefs. So, he is aiming to create a modern, secular state.
- (margin: Intro) Believes in a sort of "clockmaker" god.
- Automata can have an artificial life.
- ~~People have the sovereign~~ the source (final power) of the state is the people for USA) Hobbes says the soul is that for man.
- The state is an artificial body. The language used here is not about what is good but about staying alive.
- Psychology is a source of wisdom. It is understanding human nature.
- (margin: Ch. 1) Motion is the begining of the senses, and thus sense knowledge. But our senses don't give us the thing itself. The eye is dependent on motion. What we see in the mind is not always true; only impressions of the thing as it was.
- You cannot have knowledge until you have scientifically tested it.
- (margin: Ch. 2) Imagination = decaying sense.
- Simple memory is what we percieve, compound memory is something that doesn't exist.
- Dreams are imaginations
- Phrophesy is an activated dream.
