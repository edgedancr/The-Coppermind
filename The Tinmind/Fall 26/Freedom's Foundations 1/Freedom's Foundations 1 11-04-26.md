Hobbes, Leviathan (Review/Overview)
- Seperation of religion & state, which is unique for this time.
- Good and evil are subjective. Only our desires and aversions are good and bad.
- Man is only a machine that processes memory & senses.
- Science is about the finding of names & terms using definitions.
- Sense experience is relative. There is no common sense experience in the world.
- Man is a machine that moves towards the things he wants, and away from what he hates.
- Scientific method is a true way to discover & prove ideas, but truth itself exists only in language. This must begin with certainty & end with certainty.
- Everyone in this time is in agreement that science is the way to find truth.
- The state will be made of individuals. All they need to found the state is a common protection from fear.
- Life is a constant endeavor of power. We are always afraid of losing what we have. We can never rest. Not truly.

Hobbes, ch. 13/14/15
- State, 3 stages (IDK)
	- Prudence to leave natural state
	- Reason to create laws
- Everyone has an equal want to stay alive
- Everyone will find a reason to be entitled to the things they need to live
- Even the weak will band together to attain what they think they are owed.

- Liberty is the absence of hinderences of doing what you want to do.
