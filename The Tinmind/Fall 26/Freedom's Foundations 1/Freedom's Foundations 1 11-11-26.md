Rousseau (1712–1778) – Discourse on the Origin & foundations of inequality among men
- 2 Principles of natural man, before he is rational.
	- Looking after himself
	- Pity
- Equality is natural to man. Civilization drives inequality.
- Part 1
	- Natural man does not have to fear illness
	- He is better off than man in civilization
	- Man should be a noble savage. He is happiest when he is pre-rational
	- Rational man has free will, often to his own detriment.
	- Only man has a faculty for self-perfection.
	- Language is only necessary because of civilization.
