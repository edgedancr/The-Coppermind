- We are stained by sin and as such we cannot attain perfect virtues.
- We become righteous only through faith.
- Theological virtues
	- Faith – gift of righteousness
	- Hope – gift of eternal life.
	- Love – God's love for us.
- Torture has the same idea as an oath over a bible: fear of the judge.
- (margin: Ch. 12) Peace & order are simply the things we must center our lives around.
- There cannot be a nature in which there is no good. It is a distortion of that good nature that makes someone evil.
- Goodness is closeness to God
- Slavery in this world is not as bad as slavery to sin
- (margin: Ch. 17) The Christian is a pilgrim in this world.
- Everyone belongs in the church, regardless of social class.
- Christians will look like any other good citizen, but our goals and reasons are different.
- (margin: Ch. 21) Republic is an affair of the people according to Scipio (Roman Philosopher).
- When not serving God, there can be no justice. Romans claimed a just Republic without God, but they were wrong.
- God's justice → Providential plan for the world.
