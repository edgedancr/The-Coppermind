Thomas Aquinas, Summa Theologica, Law
- Eternal law = Providence = God's governance of creation (arrows from Eternal law down to the next three)
	- Divine law (revelation), OT/NT
	- Natural law, moral law; written on the heart.
	- Human law
- (margin: 94 a1 ↘ a2) Natural law is a habit in the way that it is a disposition of reasoning.
- 2 Types of precepts in the soul:
	- Metaphysical: certain rational principles that are self-evident to us; like non-contradiction
	- (margin: Practical →) Practical reasoning is ordered to action; all things will chose what they think is good for them.
- First precept of natural law → good
- Natural law is part of virtue. With a sinful nature, this means that evil can be "good" that we chose for ourselves.
- Our reasoning has been perverted.
- Human law ought to restrict wickedness
- Human beings can be worse than animals if they have corrupted their natural law.
- Laws that encourage virtue help people, even if they aren't believers.
- Human law is under divine law, the common good of the people, and the rulers.
