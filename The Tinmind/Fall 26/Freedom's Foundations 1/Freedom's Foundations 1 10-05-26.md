City of God
- Christian political thought is based upon the fact that the city is not the only source of law & justice.
- Eternal laws = God's Law
- Says the Romans never had a republic, by their own definition
- (margin: Ch. 23) When God is the leader of the city, everyone becomes more just

On Kingship, Thomas Aquinas
- The King should be a christian who supports the state, which is also christian. They both ought to support the church.
- The difference between Augustine and Aquinas is that Augustine treats state & Christian justice as different, while Aquinas thinks they can come together.
