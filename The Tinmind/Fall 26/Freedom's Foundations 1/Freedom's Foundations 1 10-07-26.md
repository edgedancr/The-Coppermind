Summa Theologica
- Two books
	- Books of God's word. (OT, NT). Divine law
	- Book of God's works. (Psalm 19) "The heavens declare the glory of God." Eternal law.
- Law: an act of the reason...?
- Natural law: Knowledge of seeing God in nature.
- law belongs to those who rule and by participation to those who are ruled.
- ★ Law is an ordination of reason for the common good by one who has the care of the community, and promulgated.
- Natural laws
	- birds fly south, bees make honey, etc...
	- law of reason → human beings.
- We should be rational participants in the eternal law.
- Human law (or positive law): legislation; the laws we write for ourselves.
- Man has a general idea of right & wrong, but need to work out specifics.
