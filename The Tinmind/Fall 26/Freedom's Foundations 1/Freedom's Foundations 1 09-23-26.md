The Apology
- The unexamined life is not worth living.
- Not afraid of death bc either it is nothingness or to become one with The Good
- Man cannot live without knowing the right way to live (what is just)
- "The craftsman in the sky" looks to the form of a thing to create
	- We get our ideas from the divine.
- (margin: Republic ↓ 597) The role of our life is to be craftsmen of our souls.
- The soul can be harmed by the misuse of virtues, but not by sickness. It is different from the body.
- The soul cannot be damaged by bodily harm.
- (margin: 614) Myth of Ur: guy kinda dies, comes back, explains what he saw.
	- each soul spends 1000 years in heaven
	- unjust souls take 10x the wrongdoings they did in life.
	- The wiser a man is when he dies, the better off he is in heaven
