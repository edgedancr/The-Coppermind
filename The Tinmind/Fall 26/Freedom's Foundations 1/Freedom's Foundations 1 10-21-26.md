Machiaveli – The Prince (1469–1527)
- What did the ancient Kings do to stay in power? He answers with history, not philosophy.
- Man is the measure of all things
- (margin: Ch 1) Taking a place that has been free is hard bc they will fight back hard.
- "Virtu" = skill, strength. This is how good rulers are made.
- Fortune/luck are key parts of sucess
	- Moses = almost all fortune
	- Cyrus = skill and fortune
- Fortune is fickle, so one must rely on skill instead. Good fortune is unreliable.
- (margin: Ch 8) Coming to power through wicked actions can aquire power but not glory
