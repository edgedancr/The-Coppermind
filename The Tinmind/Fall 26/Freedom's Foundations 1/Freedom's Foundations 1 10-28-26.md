Hobbes, Leviathan
- (margin: Ch. 2) Scientific testing of sense knowledge is the means to truth
- (margin: Ch. 3) Two trains of thought: unguided and regulated. ~~Sort of like instinct vs. desire~~?
- God is infinite, and nothing infinite is understandable. Infinity is a placeholder for things that cannot be fully understood in reality.
- Religion and other similar ideas are tools used to control humans.
- (margin: Ch. 4) Every word has a "right" definition of language. Truth/Knowledge is found this way.
- No metaphors, lying
- True & false exist in speech only.
- Geometry is dependable: makes sense in the head, and in practicality. It is a sort of bottom line.
- Wants objective knowledge; no subjectivity.
- (margin: Ch. 5) Logic is dependable too.
- Science is the study of consequences.
- Human feelings are all scientifically definable.
