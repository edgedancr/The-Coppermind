<u>The Republic</u>
- A thriving economy & wealth invites war, necessitating soldiers.
- Education is necessary, but a special education is required for soldiers, because they have a sort of physical power.
- Education + practice + craft.
- 4 cardinal virtues:
	- <u>Wisdom</u>
	- Temperance
	- Justice
	- Courage/Fortitude.
- (margin: 376) A Military Education requires the teaching of the virtues. Guardians ought to be gentle & spirited.
- Musical education (poetical, exercise, reason)
- The question is not if we need an army, but of what type: Militia or professional. (It's always Professional).
- Children need education too, but by stories not reason.
- (margin: 378) "We need better stories about the gods that provide good examples for men".
- War is not a good in itself, & children can't tell what is good or not, so we ought to curate the content they consume.
- (margin: 380c) Law, making gods to creators of all good things, not all things.
