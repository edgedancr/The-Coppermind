	- Tones (shēngdiào)
		1. Flat tone, keep the tone steady: mā
		2. Rising tone, from low to high: Má
		3. Falling to a point, kinda gravelly: Mǎ
		4. Falling, but to a higher pitched fine, sharp point: Mà
		- There are 14 situations that the characters need to pronounce as a neutral tone. The 3 most common situations are:
			1. The second character of a double-character term. (bàba, māma)
			2. Question particle (ma, ne, ba)
			3. Structural auxiliary word (le, de, de, zhe)
		- if there are two 3rd tones together, treat it like a 2nd then 3rd tone
		- Practice with a poem:
			- bái rì yī shān jìn, huáng hé rù hǎi liú; yù qióng qiān lǐ mù, gèng shành yì céng lóu.
