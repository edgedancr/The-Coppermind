##### Family
- 家人 — family
- 我的 — my
- 加 — home, the place you live in
- 房子 — house
##### L2D1 Vocab
- 那 — that
- 的 — (A possessive or descriptive particle)
- 照片 — Picture, photo
- 这 — this
- 爸爸 — father
- 妈妈 — mama 
- 个 — (measure word for many common everyday objects)
- 女 — female
- 孩子 — child
- 谁 — (sheí) who, whom
- 她 — she, her
- 妹妹 — younger sister
- 姐姐 — older sister
- 男 — male
- 弟弟 — younger brother
- 他 — he, him
- 哥哥 — older brother
- 大哥 — oldest brother
- 儿子 — son
- 有— to have, to exist
- 女儿 — daughter

- 外婆 — maternal grandmother

- 奶奶 — paternal grandmother

##### Numbers
0. 零
1. 一
2. 二
3. 三
4. 四
5. 五
6. 六
7. 七
8. 八
9. 九
10. 十
- Numbers 11-19 are just 十 + the number, like 十一 is 11, etc...
- 20, 30, 40, etc... Are  二 十, 三十， 四十， etc...
- For numbers between 21 and 99, they are 二十八 = 28， 六十三 = 63， 七十九 = 79, etc...
