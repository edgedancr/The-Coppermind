##### Test L1D1 Vocab
1. 我 — I, me
2. 小姐 — miss, young lady
3. 什么 — what
4. 请问 — excuse me
5. 你 — you
6. 名字 — name
7. 贵姓 — what is your family name?
	1. 贵 is an honorific you don't give yourself, but only to other people.
8. 先生 — sir, husband
9. 呢 — open question.
10. 叫 — to call, to be called

##### Dialogue 2 Vocab
- 是 — is/are/am, to be
	- 他是我的老师 — he is my teacher
	- 你是日本人吗？ — are you Japanese? 
	- 我是 ___ — I am___ (from this place)
- 老师 — teacher
	- 老师好
	- 请问老师贵姓 — excuse me teacher, what is your family name
	- 老师不是美国人 — the teacher is not an american.
- 吗 — question particle, only yes or no
	- 你是学生吗
	- 你的名字是王妈 — is your last name 王？
	- 她姓王吗？— is his last name wang?
- 不 — No, not
	- It is 4th tone if it is followed by a 1st, 2nd, or 3rd tone, but it is 2nd tone if followed by a 4th tone.
	- 我不喜欢冰淇淋 — I do not like ice cream
	- 老师不姓李 — the teacher's last name is not Lee
- 学生 — student
	- 你是学生吗? — are you a student?
	- 我是___ 的学生。 — I am a student of ___
	- 好学生吗 — are you a good student?
- 也 — also
	- 我也是 — me too
	- 你也是美国人吗 — are you also an american?
	- 我也不是北京人？— I am also not from Beijing.
- 人 — Person, human
	- 国 — country
	- 他是个好人。— He is a good person.
	- 你是哪国人？ — What country are you from?
	- 那个人姓什么？— What is that person's last name?
- 中国 — China
	- 网线是中国人。— Mr. Wong is a Chinese person.
	- 中国的十五 —
	- 中国餐厅， 中国饭馆。— Chinese restaurant.
- 美国 — America
	- 美国的文化 — American culture
	- 美国人喜欢汉堡。— American people like hamburgers 
	- 你是美国哪里人？— Where in America are you from
		- 哪里— where
	- 我是滨州人 — I am from Virginia.
- 北京， 纽约 — Beijing, New York
	- 北京烤鸭 —  Beijing duck
	- 陈小姐住在北京 — Mrs. Chen lives in Beijing.
		- 住在 — living somewhere.
	- 我是纽约人。 — I am a New Yorker.
	- 我先去纽约 — I wan to go to New York.
		- 去 — to go
