##### Moon Festival
- 吃 — to eat
- 吃月饼 — eat moon cake
- 赏月 — watch the moon
- 家人 — family
- 烤肉 — have BBQ
- 祝贺亲友 — greet and send wishes to family and close friends
- 中秋节快乐！ — Happy Moon Festival!
	- 快乐 — happy
- 花好月圆， 幸福美满 。 — Wishing you happiness and harmony under the full moon.
- 月圆情圆， 心想事成 — Wishing your hearts desires come true under the full moon.
- 合家团圆 身体健康。 — Wishing you a happy family reunion and good health.
- 但愿人长久，清理工婵娟。— May we live long and share the beauty of the moon, even from afar.
