##### Imagination
- The IC must continuously challenge standards and norms in order to not get complacent.
- A dedicated Red Team helps get people to think about new and novel considerations.
	- Even when thinking of new ideas, it is important to be able to communicate those in a way that shows their significance to others so that they are taken seriously.
- Analysis Methods
	- Brainstorming
	- Outside-In. Making sure that you are looking at the whole picture first before zooming in on specifics
	- Red Team
	- Alternative Futures. Similar to Outside-In, but is focusing on the major dynamic elements that are affecting the situation, then determining whether or not each one is a high or low driving element for each future scenario.
- Curiosity is important for collection officers. It helps guide:
	- Where to look
	- What to look for
	- Helping you understand that you don't have the full picture.

##### Human-Computer Collaboration
- Technology is vast in volume, and must be properly handled if it is to be useful.
- It is important to integrate and work together well. Understanding what things should be allocated to machines and what things should be done by humans is important.
- Cyber defense is exceptionally complicated. Every new innovation becomes a new solution, but also introduces new threats, making every system more and more complicated.
