##### Thinking Systems, Fast and Slow
- System 1 (Fast)
	- Thinking which works automatically and quickly with little or no voluntary control.
	- Fast, effortless, involuntary (automatic), infinite capacity (parallel processing), for perception, intuition, and reflection.
- System 2, (Slow)
	- Allocates attention to the effortful mental activities that demand it. This includes complex computation, utilizing agency to make choices, and concentration.
	- Slow, effortful, voluntary (deliberate), limited capacity (serial processing), for reasoning, analysis, and choice.
- Humans often default to being "lazy controllers" and overusing System 1.
- WYSIATI (what you see is all there is)
	- This is being aware of what *we know*, what *we know we don't know*, and what *we don't know we don't know*.

- Basic Definitions
	- All Source Intelligence
		- Drawing from all types of collection as appropriate to the topic.
	- Key Judgements
		- The 30-second summary of the takeaways of your report. The "so what" part, which can be called the executive summary.
	- Analytic Confidence
		- How strongly an analytic conclusion is supported.
	- Mirror Imaging
		- Assuming your enemy will think and act in the same way you will.
	- Politicized intelligence
		- Giving intelligence you think the client wants, instead of what is really supported by the information.
	- Zero-day Vulnerability
		- A fault that could be exploited, but has not been advertised, fixed or exploited yet.
	- Serious vs. Grave Damage
		- Secret vs. Top Secret.
	- Two governing bodies over intel
		- HPSCI (House permanent select committee on intelligence)
		- SSCI (Senate select committee on Intelligence)
	- Christian Worldview
		- A belief that shapes every area of life (though this definition overlaps with other religions)
	- Just War Theory
		1. Just cause
		2. Just intentions
		3. Proper authority
		4. last resort
		5. probability of success
		6. Proportionality of means to objectives
		7. Discrimination of control.
	- Strong Topic Sentences
		- Creates a roadmap for the paragraph
		- Demonstrates causality
	- Executive Order 12333
	- IRTPA (Intelligence reform and terrorism prevention act)
	- Need to know
		- Giving only information that one needs to know to do their job, and nothing more.
	- Good Intel

		- Tailored
		- Concise
