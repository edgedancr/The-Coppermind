##### Just War
- The dual framework of just war analysis:
	- Jus ad bellum
		- The right to war. Evaluates the initiation of conflict.
	- Jus in bello
		- Right in war. Evaluates the conduct and tactics during the conflict.
- The strategic thresholds for initiation
	1. Legitimate authority
		- Can the acting body raise military forces and actually control/end the conflict? (excludes private citizens and banditry).
	2. Just Cause
		- Based on Aquinas two primary cancers: does the action rectify an existing injustice or prevent a wrongful attack?
	3. Right Intention
		-  Is the ultimate goal Augustine's peace, or is it masked egoism, revenge, and material gain?
- Finalizing the Authorization of Force
	4. Last Resort
		- Have diplomatic negotiations , economic sanctions, and mediation been exhausted? Has clear signaling been used to give the enemy time to reflect and back down?

###### The Proportionality Matrix


- Discrimination and the Boundaries of control
	- Legitimate Targets 
		- Armed forces, guerrillas, and civilians meeting the ICRC 2009 standard of "direct participation in hostilities."
	- The Gray Zone
		- Civilian infrastructure and the risk of "Double Effect"—unintentional harm to civilians resulting from a strike on a legitimate miliary target.
	- Protected Status
		- Women, children, the infirm, wounded POWs, and culturally/religiously valued objects.
##### The Polygraph Test
- The polygraph does not grant clearances; it is a specialized data collection tool designed to assist the adjudication process in the resolving each case fairly and consistently.
- The Trait of the Clearance Process
	- Polygraph Examinations
		- Eliciting unrecorded historical/behavioral data
	- Background Investigations
		- Verifying documented historical data
	- ➔ Adjudication
	- ➔ Access to the trusted workforce
- The Instrumentation: A Five -point Sensor Array
	1. Pneumograph 1 (chest)
	2. Pneumograph 2 (Stomach)
	3. Electro-dermal activity (fingers/palm)
	4. Cardiovascular sensor (arm)
	5. Seat sensor
- The instrument does not detect lies; it detects sympathetic nervous system arousal.
- Elicitation is the systematic process of "drawing out something hidden" by isolating the psychological stress of deception from normal baseline activity.
- Operational Scope
	- Background
		- Illegal Drug Involvement
		- Abuse of Legal Drugs
		- Serious Criminal Conduct
			- ==Deliberate Falsification of security forms==
				- This is one of the biggest red flags.
	- Counterintelligence (CI)
		- Espionage or Sabotage against the U.S.
		- Intentionally compromising classified information
		- ==Involvement in terrorist activity==
		- Secret foreign contacts
		- ==Deliberate damage to U.S. government IT systems==
- Anatomy of the 2-4 hour appointment
	1. Administrative intake
		- Consent from signed biographical worksheet completed.
	2. Responsibility fixing
		- Establishing the roles of examiner and examinee (audio/video recorded).
	3. Review Phase
		- Comprehensive walkthrough of the instrument, the SF-86 form, and *every specific examination question*
			- They will go through every single question that they will ask you when you are wired up. This is when you ask any questions and clarifications you need to so  you don't accidentally get flagged for something that isn't really a concern.
	4. Testing Phase
		- Sensors attached snugly; physiological data collection begins.
	5. Diagnostic/Resolution
		1. Clarification of any irregular responses.
