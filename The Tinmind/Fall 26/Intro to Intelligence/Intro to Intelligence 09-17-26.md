##### Denial & Deception
- Denial: trying to prevent people from "seeing" something that is present.
	- Encryption
	- Refusing to comment
	- Camouflage
- Deception: attempting to convey anything besides the whole truth.
	- Making up alibis
- Torture
	- There is no globally agreed upon definition of what constitutes torture.

##### Orbitology and Space
- LEO, 160 km to 2000 km
	- Imagery 
	- GPS
- HEO, elongated orbits above 35,768 km
	- Imagery over a specific place
- GEO, 36,000 km
	- Equatorial or inclined
- Sun-Earth Lagrange Points (L1, L2, L3, L4, L5)
	- Points of stable gravity in the Sun-Earth system, where satellite missions can "park" indefinitely. These are where things places there become stable relative to the gravity wells of the sun and earth
	- L1, the point between the earth and sun's gravitational pulls
		- ACE: monitor solar wind & interplanetary medium
		- Wind: study solar wind & Earth magnetosphere
		- SOHO: monitor solar activity & space weather
		- DSCOVR: monitor space weather & Earth's climate
		- Communication relays
	- L2, the point on the far side of the earth where gravity falls off
		- Gaia: catalogue stars for a 3d map of the Milky Way
		- James Webb Space Telescope. 
		- Scientific experiment platforms
	- L3, the point on the opposite side of the sun from the Earth.
		- Limited use, potentially useful for space observation.
