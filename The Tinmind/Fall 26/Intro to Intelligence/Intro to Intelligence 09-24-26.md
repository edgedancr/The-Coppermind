##### Pearl Harbor Case Study
- US intel 
	- Japan would not attack from the sea, only from the air.
	- US decided, with testing of captured Japanese munitions, that their torpedoes could not be used in Pearl Harbor due to the shallowness of the water. They also assumed that Japan could not innovate and figure out how to solve that issue.
- WW2 timeline up to Pearl Harbor
	- 1931 Japan invades Manchuria
	- 1933 Hitler seizes power in Germany 
	- 1936 Germany remilitarizes the Rhineland 
	- 1937 Japan invades China proper, most notably with the Nanking Massacre
	- 1938 Germany annexes Austria
	- 1939 Nazi-Soviet pact, invasion of Poland
	- 1940 Germany invades Denmark, Sweden and France, Battle of Britain
	- 1941 Operation Barbarossa with the invasion of the Soviet Union
	- December 7 1941: Pearl Harbor
