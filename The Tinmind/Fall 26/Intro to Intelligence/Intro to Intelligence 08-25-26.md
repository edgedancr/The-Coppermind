###### Information Model
| Information level   | Description                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wisdom/Intelligence | Predictive insight; time-sensitive knowledge; intelligence products                                   |
| Knowledge           | Information in context/comparison; analyzed information (models or other structure to elicit meaning) |
| Information         | Processed data into humanly "readable" form; collated or aggregated data                              |
| Data                | Signals in machine readable form (analog or digital)                                                  |
| Signal              | Electrons (RF), Photons (light), other communications                                                 |

- Being able to provide actionable intelligence is an invaluable skill in the IC.
- Orbital Altitudes for Intelligence Satellites
	- LEO (Low Earth) is for high-resolution imagery
	- MEO (Medium Earth) is for navigation and communication.
	- GEO (Geosynchronous) is at 22,000 miles and is for constant coverage of one area.
- Cyber and AI
	- CNE (Computer Network Exploitation) exploits networks to gather data.
	- CNA (Computer Network Attack) attacks networks to gather data.
