##### The Future Intelligence Analyst
- Roles of Future Intelligence Analysts
	- Connector
		- Use sophisticated digital to old to find, package, and deliver information to key users.
	- Curator 
		- Present the information so as to highlight decision-relevant insights and its provenance.
	- Collaborator
		- Work with the broad intelligence ecosystem and with machines.
	- Creator
		- Imagine, hypothesize, visualize, and design new or alternative perspectives.
		- Can AI be a creator? It's a current philosophical debate whether AI can create, and to what extent.
	- Critic
		- Challenge methods, reasoning, logic, and inferences in intelligence products.
		- It is important to be critical without being cynical.
	- Content-expert
		- Draw on a well of diverse subject-matter expertise.
- The IC needs specialists/analysts in:
	- Economics and financial intel
	- Industrial-capacity and supply chains
	- Energy, shipping, key minerals, logistics
	- Commercial-data, OSINT, privacy
	- International and business law
	- Cyber, AI, robotics, and semiconductors
	- Space exploration and defense industry
	- Mandarin and Chinese culture
	- Engineers and scientists
