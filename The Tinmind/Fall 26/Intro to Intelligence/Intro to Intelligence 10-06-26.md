##### The Collision of Two Cultures: The IC and Law Enforcement
- One Threat, Two Lenses
	- An police officer sees a crime to be punished. An intel officer sees a puzzle to be solved. Neither is wrong, their mandates demand different realities.
	- Admissible evidence & immediate threats vs. logistics hubs and financial networks.
- Prosecution vs. Prevention
	- Prosecution
		- Culture: Law Enforcement
		- Focus: Action-oriented. Identify, apprehend, and prosecute offenders.
		- Primary Goal: Public Safety and Justice
	- Prevention
		- Culture: Intelligence Community
		- Focus: Analysis-oriented. Anticipate, assess, and mitigate threats.
		- Primary Goal: National security and strategic foresight.
##### Guest Lecture 3: CDR. Blocker on the Open Skies Case Study
> *Rebuilding credibility and norms after operation spiderweb.*
- Framing the discussion
	- *For seventy years, the united states and Russia maintained a norm of transparency that helped prevent miscalculation and catastrophic escalation. Our question today: How should the U.S. respond now that the very principle of transparency may no longer be stabilizing?*
	- What is transparency in security?
	- The collapse of a norm — not a treaty — is the central issue.
