- 3 Types of Knowledge (Aristotle)
	- Techne: practical, usable skills with a craft.
	- Phronesis: Knowledge on how to navigate the world. (Socially, Politically)
	- Sophia: Knowledge of the first principles of things; wisdom.
- Induction is the way you get to forms; looking at the world around you.
	- Plato says we need to ignore the world and look upward for the forms.
- Matter is defined by the role it plays in the substance.
- Forms are immaterial, but connected to physical things.
	- forms are ontologically dependent on their matter.
	- forms die when their subject does.
- Aristotle doesn't really address universals, focuses on Particulars.
- On the Soul
	- Can matter think? Do animals think, or is it just stimulus response?
- ★ The 4 Causes:
	- Material (that out of which)
	- Efficient (That by which)
	- Formal (that into which)
	- Final (that for which), telos
