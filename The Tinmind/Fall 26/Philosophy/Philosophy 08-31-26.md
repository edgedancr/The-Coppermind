- Math & Philosophy are similar, both use reason to find ultimate reality. Numbers point to something that is eternal, transcendent
- (Heraclitus:) "If there was a substance the world was made of, it is fire." "The only thing that doesn't change is the theorem that everything changes." This idea of change is called Heraclitian Flux
- The Eleatics
	- Why is there something rather than nothing?
	- Parmenides (515–450), asked? argued: All that which is without cause is that which is without begining. Something in reality is that which is without cause. Therefore, something in reality is that which is without begining.
	- Zeno's paradox of motion. (490–430) (an arrow back up to Parmenides)
	- Believes that everything is permanent, & change is illusory. (above "change": **motion.**)
