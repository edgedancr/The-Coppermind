Blaise Pascal (1623–1662)
- Practical rationality
	- Trusting an argument for something you should do, even if you wouldn't believe it normally.
- <u>Pascal's Wager</u>
	- It is pragmatic to believe that God exists.

| | God exists | God doesn't exist | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Believe in God | +∞ | −1 | = **+∞** (circled) |
| Don't believe | −∞ | +1 | = −∞ |

- Objections
	- We don't choose our beliefs; doxastic voluntarism is false.
	- Would God be pleased with such belief?
	- How do you know which god to believe in?
