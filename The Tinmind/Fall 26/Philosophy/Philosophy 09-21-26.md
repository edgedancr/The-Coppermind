- Nicomachean Ethics (Aristotle)
	- What does it mean to be a good person?
- Aristotle's Value Theory: The good life & Normative ethics.
	- A person's charachter is the sum of their virtue & vices.
		- virtue is a disposition to think, act, or feel in the right way.
		- Vice is the same but in the wrong way.
- Says "The Good" is too abstract, not practical enough. He wants a individual telos for everything, not one telos, soulish (over "a": "individual"; under "telos,": "soulish")
- Happiness (Eudaimonia) = flourishing. A joy that can exist even in bad situations
- Intrinsic goods are always good, no matter what.
- Intrumental goods make you feel good.
- 3 candidates:
	- Pleasure
	- The political life (honor)
	- The contemplative life. ★ (circled)
