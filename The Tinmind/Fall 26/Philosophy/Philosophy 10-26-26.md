Gottfried Leibniz (1646–1716)
- Is everything that happens the will of God?
- Leibniz says everything that happens is the will of God.
- He is protestant, Gnostic, genius. Discovered calculus
- Philosophy of Religion
	- God's perfect rationality (584)
		- Amor fati? (584). Should we love our faith?
			- God's antecedent and consequent will. Pre-evil vs post-evil will.
		- Nothing arbitrary (583). God does everything for the best reason.
			- The principle of sufficient reason.
		- Necessary truths
			- God's nature logically preceds His will
		- Maximal creation
			- Diversity, harmony, order, simplicity.
	- The best possible world. Or perhaps, the world of best possibility.
		- Theodicy
			- God permits, but does not cause evil
			- Yet, Leibniz is a determinist
		- Is God free?
			- Is He free if He must make the world this way?
