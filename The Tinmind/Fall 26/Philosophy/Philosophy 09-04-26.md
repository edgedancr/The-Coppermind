- Phaedo & Meno dialogues
- Phaedo: Socrates is on trial. Main point: Does the soul exist seperately from the body? What happens when one dies?
	- Will vs Soul: Soul is all-encompassing essence of the immaterial parts of man, Will is a faculty of the soul.
	- How does the mind interact & communicate w/ the soul.
- Does the soul exist seperately from the body?
	- 2-fold strategy:
		- Prove the pre-bodily-existence of the soul.
		- Prove the post-bodily-existance of the soul.
- Meno's Paradox:
	- If you know what you're looking for (K), inquiry is unneccessary (U), and if you don't know (~K), Inquiry is impossible (I)
	- (K⊃U) & (~K⊃I)
	- K∨~K
	- U∨I
- ★ Plato's Forms: Souls know the forms of everything before being born, but the trauma of birth damages that knowledge. So, learning is instead recalling.
	- Proofs (p.47, 73b) "leading the Witness": "Doubling the square." Leading a slave through the geometric proof. This illustrates Forms
	- Equality (p.48, 74b). We have an innate, abstract idea of equality.
