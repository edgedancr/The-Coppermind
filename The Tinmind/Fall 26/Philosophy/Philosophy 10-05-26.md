Aquinas, Summa Theologica
- Does divine hiddeness count against the existence of God?
- Is the existence of God self-evident? Can it be proven?
	- 2 Types of self-evidence:
		- Self-evident, in itself, but not to us.
		- Self-evident in itself, and to us.
	- Epistemological vs. ontological priority
		- E: God's existence precedes His existence.
		- O: God's essence prededes/is identical to His existence.
- Can you prove God's existence?
	- Faith & natural knowledge: Faith depends on natural knowledge.
	- God's nature, existance, and effects
		- We reason from effect to cause, not cause to effect.
		- effects can demonstrate existence, but not nature (to the same extent)
