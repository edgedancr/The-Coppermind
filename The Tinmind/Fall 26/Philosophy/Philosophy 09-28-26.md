Augustine
- What is evil?
	- not a substance
	- Ontologically dependent on good.
	- a Privation; the absence of good.
	- a parasite. It harms that which is good.
	- Plato's chain of being applied to evil.
	- Human evil is bad choosing & disordered love
		- disordered love is effectively idolatry.
- The Will
	- Intellectualism & voluntarism.
- You have to exercise your will.
	- You are responsible for your actions
- The origin of evil
	- Lucifer non-being without explanation.
	- Is it inevitable that a finite being sins if given the opportunity? NO
- Theodicy (righteousness of God.)
	- Is God responsible for the fall, since He foreknew it?
	- We are dependant beings fundementally.
		- The ability for us to exercise our agency is more central & fundamental to God's character than stopping us.
	- ★ An evil man has more intrinsic good than an inanimate object.
- Divine foreknowledge and free will
	- Can human choice be free if God knows?
	- Just bc you observe, doesn't mean you cause it.
