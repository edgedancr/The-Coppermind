C3
- Metaphysics: The study of reality.
	- Nothing is emperical with metaphysics; the connection is invisable
	- Do people change over time? How do we explain that? Science cannot answer.
- Epistemology: The study of knowledge & how we know.
	- How do we know Math? Logic? How do we know that reason works?
- Philosophy of Religion: The study of things religion cares about.
	- Does God exist? Can faith lead to knowledge? What does it mean for God to be outside of time.
	- God is without parts; inseperable. A simple being.
	- Was creation an act of love?
- Value Theory/Ethics
	- What is the good life? Who is a good person?
- Other Branches of Philosophy
	- Logic
	- Philosophy of math, science, etc...
	- Political Philosophy.
- History of Philosophy
	- Presocratics
		- Looking for consistent, non-arbitrary explanations within nature.
	- 2 Metaphysical puzzles
		- The one and the many.
		- The nature of change/permanence.
- The Ionians
	- Thales (624–545), was first to claim that everything was made of water, to some degree. Also first Western Philosopher.
	- Pythagoras (570–490), Philosophy of math. Arithmatic, geometry.
	- Heraclitus (540–480) (an arrow carries this past the 8/31 date to "If there was a substance…")
