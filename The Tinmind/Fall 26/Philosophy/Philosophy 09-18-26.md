Aristotle Metaphysics
- Substance & Soul
	- 3 substance types
		- A particular sort of matter
		- Form (secondary substance). Particularly a universal one.
		- A Hylomorphic compound of both.
			- A "this-such"
			- Primary substances are neither "said of nor in"
	- Substance aligns with the form
- Souls & living organisms.
	- all living organisms are Primary substances
	- The soul is the actuality of the body.
		- Matter possesses potentiality vis-à-vis forms
	- Soul comes in a hierarchy
		- Nutritive, sensitive, appetitive, perceptual, motive, rational,
- Is the soul seperable from the body?
	- The rational part can due to the intentionality of the mind.
- What is Time?
	- Motion? The dimension that change is possible in
	- How is motion possible?
		- Motion requires something to put it in motion. Therefore, the thing that caused the first motion must be an eternally moving, unmoved mover.
	- Is motion only physical?
