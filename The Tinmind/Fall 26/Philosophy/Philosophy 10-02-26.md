- Definition of God: The greatest conceivable being, or the being than which none greater can be conceived.
	- not greatest possible because some people think God is impossible.
- The argument:
	1. God exists in the understanding.
	2. God is possible (might have existed in reality.)
	3. If something is both only in the understanding and is possible, then it might be greater bc it might be actual.
	4. By definition, God is not greater than it is.
	5. It is not the case that God exists only in the understanding and is possible (margin: 3,4 MT)
	6. If it is not the case that something exists only in the understanding and is possible, then it is actual.
	7. Therefore, God exists. MP 5,6
- Point 3 is the most contended.
- Gaunilo's Island. GCB could be replaced with greatest concievable thing.
	- Response: God is unique. He is greater than everything. An infinite argument for an infinite being.
- Begging the question? no...
- Equivocates God as an idea and as a concrete being.
