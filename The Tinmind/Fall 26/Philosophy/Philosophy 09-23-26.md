- Ethics (Aristotle)
- If you are functioning properly, the you are fulfilling your telos.
	- What is the proper function of man? to be virtuous
	- How does one be virtuous?
		- Enjoyment of being virtuous.
		- Two categories: Intellectual & moral virtue. Taught vs. habitual.
- We are not born with virtue. We must be habituated into virtue.
- Precision with Ethics is impossible.
- Virtues are intentional.
- Is emotion part of virtue? If so, then we are responsible for them.
