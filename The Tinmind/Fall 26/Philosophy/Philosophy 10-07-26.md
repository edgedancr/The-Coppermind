- We need to prove God via Post-Priori Knowledge.
- Can Humans, without knowledge of the Bible, conclude a creator, first being, perfect being?
- The 5 ways (proofs) for God's Existence.
	- Cosmological arguments:
		1. Motion – Unmoved mover
		2. Efficient Causation – Need something to be the first cause (for things other than motion) (over "for things": "like non-physical things")
		3. Contingency – How do you get anything without anything there? Therefore, God. (margin: "why something rather than nothing?", with an arrow to 3)
	4. Argument from Perfection – Hirarchy of creation; Hot things come from hotter things (margin: "another infinite regress →")
		- Relatives are predicated on superlatives
		- The maximum in any genus is the cause of all in that genus.
	5. Argument from Teleology (A Design Argument) – Things don't act with a purpose without a purpose-giver; and this we call God.
