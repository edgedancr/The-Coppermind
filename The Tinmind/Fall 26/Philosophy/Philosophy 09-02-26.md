- Christian Eternalist: The universe always existed, bc God willed it to, but nothing is created or destroyed. (above: "Idea comes from Parmenides, called "B series view of time"")
- Presentism: The past & future does not exist, only the present.
- Main Idea: There is a tension between what is seen & unseen.
- Christ came "in the fulness of time" Galatians 4:4
- The Euthyphro Dillema
	- Can God command the anti-10 commandments?
		- ultimately a question of God's nature.
	- What is piety (religious righteousness, holiness)?
	- Philosophical definitions
	- (margin: Piety:) 1). Prosecuting religious crimes. — Way too narrow. A-philosophical
	- 2) Whatever is pleasing to the gods. — The gods are immoral.
	- 3) Whatever ALL the gods love. — They might not agree
		- question: What is it that all the gods love first? (ontologically)
		- Which comes first: the gods' love for piety, or their definition of what they value? Which is the independent variable.
		- Piety would be arbitrary. (an arrow from here back up to 3)
		- Should we worship piety instead of the gods.
- God is the most rational one, and as such cannot act irationally (sin)
- Voluntarism: elevates God's will beyond logic. There is nothing God cannot do.
- God's Essential Nature: God has principles of His will that He cannot violate because of His nature.
- ★ If morality is rooted in God, then how?
