Rene Descartes (1596–1650)
- ★ Can you know you have/are a soul by reason alone?
	- You can introspect, but only yourself.
- Descartes historical context
	- Medieval Scholasticism
	- Scientific revolution: Galileo & Bacon.
- The father of "modern" (enlightenment) philosophy
	- 1600–1800
	- Emphasis on Human reason
	- Anti-traditional
	- Epistemology
		- Autonomous reason – Must reason on your own.
		- Certainty
		- Foundationalism – Everything you know rests on foundational truths you have.
		- Continental Rationalism
- Methodological Skepticism
	- Hyperbolic Skepticism: De omnibus dubitandum.
	- Possible vs. Reasonable doubt
	- Real skepticism? (No, Descartes isn't a skeptic)
	- Looking for something he could believe beyond all possible doubt to base everything on.
- Meditation 1: What can be called into doubt
	- Do we have knowledge
	- A grand taught experiment
	- Doubting the senses
	- Doubting mathematical knowledge.
- Meditation 2: The nature of the mind.
