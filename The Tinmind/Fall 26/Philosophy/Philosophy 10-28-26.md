- Metaphysics
	- Determinism. Everything has essentially already happened. The future is as certain as the past and present.
		- The future is certain, yet contingent
		- Certainty is a property of persons; necessity is a property of propositions.
			- Peter's denial of Christ is certain, but not necessary.
		- Compatiblism. Free will is still here; choice is discovering.
		- Miracles
			- Not a violation of natural law. Predetermined-irregular events.
	- Monads and individual essences. Sort of like molecules with personality.
		- Peter's denial is entailed by his essence.
		- Contra Aristotle: there are no accidental properties.
		- Windowless, non-causal, indivisible, imperishable, and infinite in number.
		- Monads are the fundamental component of reality. They are tiny pieces of mind.
	- Idealism
		- Panpsychism/Panorganicism
