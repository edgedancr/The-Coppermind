(margin) Republic bk 6, 7
- How many imaterial objects are there? Do invisible things have any physicality?
- How many different forms of a thing is there?
- Could be an infinite number of forms. Maybe negative forms?
- Well ordered human is ruled by his rational part of his soul, not spirit or appetite
- The Good. Can't define, but use analogies:
	- The sun : visible things :: The good : forms
	- Eye : Visible things :: mind : forms

> [!drawing] The divided line (p. 117)
> A vertical arrow labelled "Chain of being", split into intelligible (top) and visible (bottom), with "The Good" arched over the top. Left column, top to bottom: ontology – forms, math objects, (blank), images, shadows, reflections. Right column: Epistimology – knowledge, mathematical thinking, Belief/trust, imagination, opinion.
