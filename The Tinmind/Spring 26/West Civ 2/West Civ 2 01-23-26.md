- After killing Charles 1st, the people try a republic.
- Oliver Cromwell "The Protectorate" (1653–59). He abolished parliament, subdued Ireland (killed 30%), reconquered Scotland.
- 1st Navigation act (1651), people need to buy from England.
- Dutch invade Indonesia.
- Age of Mercantilism. Gold can buy anything, especially weapons.
- Salutary Neglect. "Americans" are left alone and ignore world politics. A national identity is rising.
- Glorious Revolution (1688).
- William of Orange marries into the royal family, becomes King, signs a bill of rights, makes it so monarchs must be Protestant.

- Tsar Alexei Romanov. He formalized social classes: Nobility & Property.
- 1683: Turks invade Vienna for Holy Roman Empire, they lose by biggest infantry charge ever. (15,000 horses vs. 200,000 troops).
