- late 1600s: Philosophes vs. Romanticists.
- Mapped out human understanding for fun.
- Average westerner is a "Moralistic, Therapeutic, Deist."
- Voltaire changes the game of historiography, satire, introducing physics, etc...
- Jean-Jacques Rousseau: Emile & Social Contract. "Authentic self vs. General will."
- "Noble Savage". He does what is right in his own eyes. It is natural...
- Social Contract protects. The General Will is scientific, rational, correct.
- "We will force you to be free." → Communist, Marxism, etc...
- The power of virtue & terror to make people free.
- British flavor of the Enlightenment.
	- Okay with Monarchy.
- Pax Britannica: "If the whole world thought like us, it would be better."
