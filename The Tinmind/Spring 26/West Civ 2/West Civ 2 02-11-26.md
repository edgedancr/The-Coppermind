(margin) 1815–1824
- Concert of Europe: Agreement between UK, Prussia, France, Austria, Russia to crush radical, revolutionary rebellions in all the other countries.
- Luis XVIII has French throne & wants peace to help crush Jacobins.
- Monroe Doctrine
- Ultras (conservative monarchists) support Kings, but don't love Luis XVIII bc he is really physically weak/sick.
- Democracy is entering the world more; an era of both democracy & conservatism.
- Charles X takes French Kingdom over from Luis

- Transcendentalism rises with it. "Great Spirit."
- Fight nature & win, or die trying. Authenticity is key.
