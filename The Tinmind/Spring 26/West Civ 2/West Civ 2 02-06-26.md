#### 1789
- America is becoming somewhere people can flee to and start a new life.
- Sans Culottes still can't vote, some of them are rallying together.
- Mary Wollstonecraft, is advocating for Women's Suffrage.
- 1000 Women march on Versailles, King tells his men to kill them, they refuse. They stop and let him live, but force him out of Versailles
- Jacobins: Atheists? Political party almost. They take over
	- Girondists: less radical of the radicals
	- Montagnards: More radical, end up winning. Hates royalty.
- Robespierre comes to power.
