Unit 2: Industrial Revolutions & Revolutions of 1848

- 1770–1820 ish: 1st Industrial Revolution: Steam Power, canals, rivers. (F1R)
- 1880 + WW1: 2nd IR: Combustion engines, steel, oil (S1R)
- 1800s, S1R: Diversity of food; better nutrition. Cheap indoor heating/lighting, also AC & refridgeration. People are going to work in factories now. People are being identified by their career.
- Malthus (economics). Says people grow exponentially, while food tech doesn't, thus overpopulation. He's wrong about food.
- Money is growing; credit cards, checks, disposable income.
- 1848: Nationalist Revolutions: All ultimately failed. Paris, Spain, Rome
- Telegraphs
- Mazzini, journalist, starts revolt in Rome to bring back Roman Republic.
- Romanticist Literature is stirring people up to die for something.
- Paris Revolt works for 3 years...
