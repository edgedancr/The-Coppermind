New Imperialism
- Old Imperialism: Cortez, taking over places and forcing stuff onto them. Blatant conquest.
- New Imperialism: Economical/Political treaties & agreements.
(margin) 1790ish to 1800+
- Chaka Zulu: Zulu who conquers south Africa. He dies, & Brits come in & get rights to railroads, mining, pumps, etc.. But leaves the ruler in charge.
- Western Way of War: citizen armies, flat plane combat, go for the head, technological superiority.
- A scramble for Africa: Everyone wants their resources.
(margin) British →
- West ends bad cultural practices & bring QoL updates like medicine & pumps,
- Canada, Australia, New Zealand are part of New Imperialism. Europe sends a lot of citizens to these colonies. They let them become independent, but had them fight with them/support them in war.
- 1650, British starts caring about Cape Town, & cuts off the Dutch there from the common wealth.
- British go to war with the Boers in South Africa around 1800. They end up setting up concentration camps.
