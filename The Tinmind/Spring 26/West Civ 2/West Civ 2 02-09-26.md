#### 1790's, France
- Thermidorean Counter-Revolution. Some Jacobins think they have gone too far and try to stop them. They want less violence & a more normal government.
- Killed the priests, robbed the churches to fund the revolution.
- Cult of the Supreme Being: Jacobins didn't believe in the Christian God, it was too close to being a Catholic.
- Though they were inspired by people like Washington, for a while, people are being burned out and want to stop being so radical. Leads to directory: 5 executives, Council of 500 legislatives.
- Total War: Every dollar & every citizen is needed & used for the war effort.
- Napoleon Bonaparte comes to power, reinstates Catholic Church, gives them their stuff back, and gets credibility in return.
- Conquers Europe for the first time since the Roman Empire.
- France is on the fence about Napoleon, so no one can kill him because they would make him a martyr. They send him to an island to die.
#### 1815
- Congress of Vienna; discussing what to do w/ France. English is a big power.
- Russia has fought & bled a lot against Napoleon. Their harsh winters protect them.
- Prussia: Most powerful state in Germany.
- Austria: Chancellor Klemens von Metternich wants less radicalism, invades France to stop revolution.
