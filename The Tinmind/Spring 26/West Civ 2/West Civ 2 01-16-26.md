West Civ 2 1.2
(margin) 1600s
- People are getting better diversity of food/vitamins. This allowed people to invent more.
- West is developing tech much more than the east now.
- Printing press + disposable income leads to lots of books. Theology, politics, farming, novels, puritan how-tos, etc...
- A Jansenist is a Catholic who believes as a Protestant, but doesn't want to break away from the Catholic church.
- Pascal was a great Christian thinker. (Mathematical reasoning to follow Christ)
- Rembrandt paintings. More individualistic. Almost Hellenistic.
- Caravaggio. Again, individualistic paintings. Baroque styles
- Global era. Trade around the world. Global geography opening up.
- People are pushing what you can get away with in art.
- 1617: Defenestration of Prague. (People throwing others out of windows)
- 1618: 30 years war. Now, in European minds, politics is a bigger deal than religion.
- Peace of Westphalia
