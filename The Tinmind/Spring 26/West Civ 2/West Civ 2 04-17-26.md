**Cold War & Decolonization**
(margin) Terminology 1950s
- 1st World: Alligned w/ USA & some level of Capitalism
- 2nd World: Alligned w/ Soviets
- 3rd World: Not willing to be alligned w/ USA or Soviets.
- Ideological Clash
- Soviets want to destroy Europe, but gets nuclear bomb by 49.
- Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD).
- Command Economy
- Martial Plan: USA is going to rebuild Japan into a Western Democracy. It works
- Russia tries to increase efficiency; doesn't tell their citizens how bad their standard of living is.
- Russia blocks off Germany, tries to starve them, America air-drops supplies to the people. First "act of War."
- Russia is losing engineers, musicians, etc...
- Russia blocks off Russian sector of Berlin to keep Russians from leaving. Called Berlin Wall.
- Stalin dies, no-one wants to replace him. Too dangerous. [?Sight?] Civil war
- Khrushchev replaces him, tries to pull back on some of Stalin's ideas.
- Lots of Colonies declaring independence.
