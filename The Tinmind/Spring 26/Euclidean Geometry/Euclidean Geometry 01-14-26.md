- Doxa vs. Alethia:
	- Doxa: What appears to work. — Opinion
	- Alethia: Truth — How things truly are.
- Before Greek Mathmaticians, people only cared how to do math, not why. It wasn't its own field of study, but used for other fields like astronomy, construction, taxes, etc...
- Pythagoras (570–495 BC). He did not invent the pythagorean theorem, but he is credited with it. He also is known for his tuning system for chords that we still use.
- He believed that numbers have a mystical value.
- Greek numerology:
	- 1: Monad the origin of all things
	- 2: Dyad the created matter.
	- 7: Apollo's birthday
	- 10: perfect number.
	- 2, 3, 5: Female, male, Marrige.
- Parmenides, the father of Ontology. 5th century BC.
- He brings Greek math to deductive reasoning. Removes the mystical roll of math.
- One of the only people to stump Socrates in Plato's dialogues.
- Plato (427–348 BC). Values geometry as something to form people. It was something that the guardians need to become better people.
- Eudoxus of Cnida, "Best Mathmatician of School of Athens"
	- looking at thing in proportions & ratios. Set the groundwork for stuff like Calculus.
- Euclid (c300 BC). He systemetized proofs, not exactly coming up with them.
- Archimedes (287–212 BC). Figured out Pi, used an early form of Calculus.
