Euclid's starting points:
1) a point is that which has no weight.
2) a line is breadthless with infinite lenght
3) The extremeties of a line are points
4) A straight line is a line which lies evenly with the points on itself.
5) A surface is that which has length & breadth only.
6) The extremities of a surface are lines.
7) A plane surface lies evenly with the straight lines on itself
8) A plane angle is the inclination to one another of two lines in a plane which meet one another and do not lie in a straight line.
9) When the lines containing the angle are straight, they are rectalinear.
10) right angles & Perpendicular angles
11) Obtuse angle is greater than right angle, acute is less.
12)
13) A boundry is an extremity of anything.
14) A figure is contained by boundrys or planes
15) A circle is a plane figure
16) The point where all lines meet is the center.
17) Diameter of a circle
18) Semicircles.
19) Rectalinear figures are contained by straight lines.
20) Equilateral, isoceles, scalene triangles.
21) Right, obtuse, acute triangles.
22) Square, oblong (rectangle), Rhombus, Rhomboid, Trapeza
23) Parallel lines.

Postulates:
1) A straight line can be made from any two points
2) lines extend infinitely.
3) A circle can have any center or distance.
4) Right angles all equal each other.
5) lines, if not parallel, will meet at some point.
