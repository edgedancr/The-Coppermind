- Deductive: If Premises are true, then conclusion will be hypothetically true. Formal.
- Induction: If Premises are true, then conclusion is probably true. Not Formal.
- Abductive: If premises are simple/intuitive, then conclusion might be true. It appeals to more than senses & reason, but to feeling & intuition.
- Argument: tries to prove something to be True when its certainty is disputed.
- Explanation: tries to explain why something is True when something is already assumed true.
