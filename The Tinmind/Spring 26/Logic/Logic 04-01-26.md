S only if P = P ⊃ S
S if & only if P = S ≡ P

4 rules of Proofs:
- Modus Ponens (MP) = affirming the antecedent
- Modus Tollens (MT) = Denying the consequent
- Disjunctive Syllogism (DS) = Denying either side of the disjunction.
- Hypothetical Syllogism (HS) = Chain argument, sharing a middle term.
