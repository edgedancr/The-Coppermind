- contraposing → obvert, convert, obvert. A & O
- Imediate Inferences:
	- Conversion: E & I can swap subject/predicate.
	No S is P → No P is S, Some S is P → Some P is S.
	- Never convert an O
	- Obversion: Change Quality, flip to complement (negation). Works with all (a,e,i,o)
	A→E, I→O, etc... Double negative.
- Per-accidents conversion. If A is true, you can make it an I and then convert it.
- Per-accidents contraposition: Obvert the E to an A, then to I, then O. Works only if True.

Valid Conversions: E→E, I→I, A→I, E→O
Valid Obversions: A→E, E→A, I→O, O→I
Contrapose: obvert, convert, obvert.
