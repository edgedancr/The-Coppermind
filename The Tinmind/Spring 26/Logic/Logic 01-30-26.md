- 3 acts of the mind:
	1: Simple aprehension — Terms
	2: Judging — Propositions
	3: Reasoning — Arguments
- First act of the mind:
	- Concepts vs. terms vs. words:
		- Concepts are unique objects, unique meanings. ideas
		- Terms are abstract objects, shared meanings.
		- Words symbolize terms.
	- Extension: More specific term of a term. Clarification
	- Intension: The idea or meaning of a term.
	More specific Intension → Smaller Extension.
- Classifying terms: (Kreeft ch. 2)
	- Unambiguous or Ambiguous
	- Exact or vague.
	- Univocal, analogical, or equivocal
