- 5 predicables:
	- Species: What a thing is; its essence
	- genus: One part of the meaning of a thing. Noun
	- Specific Difference: adjective part of the meaning of a thing.
	- Property: More specific, follows from the term logically.
	- Accident: like a property, but not essential.
