- Performative is when the action is being carried out by the sentence.
	"I dub thee..." or "I baptise thee..." etc...
- Connectives: (for compound sentences)
	Monadic (operates on one part): Not ← Negation
	Dyadic (operates on more than one part): And, Or, If.
	(margin) Conjunction → And, Disjunction → Or, Conditional → If
