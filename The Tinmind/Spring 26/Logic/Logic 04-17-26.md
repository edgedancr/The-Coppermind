- Inference to the Best Explanation (abductive)
	- What makes one explanation better than another?
		- Internally consistant.
		- Externally consistant.
		- Explanatory scope is wide.
		- Testable.
		- Simpler.
- This is valid in inductive reasoning:

Confirmation:
1. H ⊃ P.
2. P.
3. Probably H.

Disconfirmation:
1. H ⊃ P.
2. ~P.
3. Probably ~H.
