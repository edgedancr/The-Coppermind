Salvation + Predestination
- Salvation from: Death, sin, & the Devil.
	- all → Called the common curse (arrows from Death, sin and the Devil; under "the Devil": "Presence of sin")
- Views of Salvation:
	- Universalism: All will be saved
	- (margin: very similar, a bracket joining these two) Unlimited atonement: Christ died for all, so all have opportunity
	- Limited atonement: Christ died only for the elect.
- Ordo Salutus (Order of Salvation): (in a box; in the margin: "not necessarily in order", with brackets from it to the first and second groups)
	1) Foreknowledge
	2) Predestination
	3) Calling/Election
		→ 1–3: Before conversion.
	4) Regeneration
	5) Repentence & Faith
	6) Justification
	7) Adoption
		→ 4–7: During Conversion.
	8) Sanctification
	9) Perseverence
	10) Glorification
		→ 8–10: after conversion.
- Predestination:
	- (margin: Two answers: C, A) Calvin: We should emphesize God's will. God chooses some to be saved & others not.
	- Armenian: We should emphesize God's Knowledge. God chooses some, based on His knowledge of who would be saved, & vice versa.
	- Provideant Grace: when the gospel is being preached, God restrains our hearts enough that we have the choice to accept Christ. (an arrow runs from here up to Armenian)
- Foreknowledge. (above: "Boethius", "Molina", "Medieval Philosophers", with arrows to the two views)
	- (margin: Ⓐ) Eternal Present: God is outside of time. He can see all moments as present.
	- (margin: Ⓐ) Middle Knowledge: Before creating the world, God knew everything that would happen. From all the possible worlds to create, He chose this one. We still make our own decisions.
- (margin: Ⓒ) Moral Necessity: We aren't pushed around by outside forces; We are predictible, & it come about because of God's will. God's foreknowledge is not seperate from His will.
- Freedom:
	- (margin: Ⓐ) Libertarian Freedom/noncombatibilist: If something must happen, then it isn't free will.
	- (margin: Ⓒ) Compatibilist free will: Freedom & moral necessity can be intertwined.
	- (margin: objection to →) Every decision we make is because of who we are. We don't really have total control over our character or circumstances.
	- (margin: response to →) I still have the choice to respond how I want, despite what I am predisposed to do
