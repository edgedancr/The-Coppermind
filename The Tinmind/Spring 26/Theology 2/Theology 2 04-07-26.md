Hebrews & Moral Motivation
- Don't know the author; it was written as a sermon to a church the author knows. It's about resisting persecution.
(margin) (Beale 372)
- Some Hebrews want to return to old system to lie low & avoid persecution.
- Paul could've wrote it, but Hebrews says it's not by an apostle. Maybe Apollos? or at least someone like him.
- Why we are Morally Motivated: (Other Ideas:)
	- Duty: Deontology — Duty goes against happiness; shouldn't take pleasure in Morality (a line from here to "Kant")
	- Fear: People don't love this idea, but Bible treats fear as a valid path to wisdom.
	- Happiness: Eudaimonism — Whatever is Morally right will make you happiest.
		- Summum Bonum: Highest Good – all actions have a higher, final end.
- Hebrews' answer:
	- <u>Christ's</u> Supremecy:
	- (margin: Beale 369) OT is the shadow; Christ is the antitype. Jews can't go back.
	- Christ has the authority to forgive us; He is King of all nature.
	- (margin: Beale 380) <u>Christ</u> is our high priest; turning back to Moses will do nothing.
	- There is no other way to salvation.
	- There is no difference between duty & happiness; our joy comes from serving. (added above: "our duty to be")
