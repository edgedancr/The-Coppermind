Christocentric Soteriology – Dr. John Fredenick
- Justification happens in an instant; the point at which one is declared justified or "in the right." Not a moral statement, but a legal one
- Sanctification:
	- Can you lose salvation? Ⓒ No, never saved/backsliding. Others would say yes.
	- Bible talks as if we are already sanctified.
- Sanctification:
	- Progressive Sanctification: The journey to becoming more like Christ.
	- Completed Sanctification: More like justification, imputed to us from Christ.
- We need to keep theology Christological & not Anthropological.
	- Our faith does not contribute to salvation. We trust in Christ, & that is enough.
