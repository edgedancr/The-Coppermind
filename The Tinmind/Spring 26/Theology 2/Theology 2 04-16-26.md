**Revelation Approaches & Millennial Views**
- Revelation:
	- Prophecy
	- Apocalyptic.
- 4 approaches:
	- Historicism: Rev is a chronological history from age of apostles onward
	- Futurism: Also chronological, but almost everything is still future to us.
	- Preterism: Mostly happens in early years of the church.
	- (margin: Recapitulation, a bracket joining these two) Full-Preterism: Everything in Rev happened by AD 70.
	- Partial-P: Rev happens by the fall of Rome, but everything after ch. 20 is future.
	- Idealism: Lots of symbolism & patterns, uses it to say there will be an escalation of fulfillments until final fulfillment.

**Millennial Views**
- Pre-Mill: Usually futurist, believes Christ will come before Millennium.
	- Pre-trib, Pre-Mil, says believers don't go through the tribulation
- Post-Mill: Christ comes after Millenium. Usually Pretorist or historicist.
	- Sometimes non-literal 1,000 years.
- A-Mill: Usually Idealist; Christ returns after Millenium, which is non literal, and we are in it right now. Mill happens during church age, and will be a hard time.
