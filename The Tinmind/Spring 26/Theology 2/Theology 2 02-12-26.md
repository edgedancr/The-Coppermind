- <u>Objections to satisfaction</u>: — Calvin said this (an arrow points to the first answer)
	- Divine Child abuse. → Jesus is not a son in the way we are. Jesus was also willing
	- Nonviolent atonement. → He must be violent. He cannot let sin go without violence, and still be a God of law & justice. Sinners condemn themselves to death.
	- (margin: Kant. ★) Legal Fiction: sin isn't something that can be covered by someone else. → Penalty for sin is death, Christ died for us, & we are one with Christ. This is why, though Christ died for everyone, only those who are in Him are saved.
	- (margin: Allen) Does not address our situation; not useful for life on earth. → Sin is the cause of suffering, so by delivering us from Satan, Christ has given us a chance to be better. Also, our spiritual condidition is more important.
