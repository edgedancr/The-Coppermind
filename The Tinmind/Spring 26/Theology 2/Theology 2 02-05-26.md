(margin) John: Prologue:
- Non-trancendent god vs. trancendent God: Our God is a person; someone we can know. He has made the world with an order and purpose, unlike the pagan thought at the time.
- Believing in a "oneness" for everything in the world being connected was popular. John was fighting against that with light vs. darkness.
- Epistemalical Function of Miracles; to help us believe, & knowledge.
- Divine Irruption. We need God to rend the heavens & come down.
- Christ is the "I AM" who is all good things.
(margin) Work of HS
- Union w/ Christ, intellectual/moral guidance, evangelistic leadership.
- Holy Spirit is it's own part of the Trinity; its own divinity. It is not just an extention of God's will. It acts in conjunction with the Father.
- It is not an un-knowable force. It is Its own person.
