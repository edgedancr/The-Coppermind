- Jesus is Fully God & Fully Man. A Hypostatic Union. 2 natures in 1 person; not at odds against each other.
(margin) Christ's Deity:
- Jesus claims the angels, to be able to cleanse sins, the sabbath, to rule over life & death.
	- He knows God in a Godly way.
	- He existed before time.
	- He accepts all claims of deity.
- Apostles witness His deity also. They call Him Lord & God.
- Arianism denied His deity, kinda. He is the "greatest of the angels."
- Ebionism denies that Christ has anything to do with being God.
- Granville Sharp Rule "our God **and** Savior Christ Jesus"
- Christ has a physical & psycological human nature.
- Christ is free to sin or not to sin. He went through temptation, but not Evil desire.
- Docetism denies the humanity of Christ. Only appeared as human.
- Apolinarianism denies Christ's psycological humanity, but not His physical nature.
- Jesus prefers Son of Man over Son of God.
- Athanatius: "God cannot redeem what He has not assumed!"
- Anselm: "Christ's work must have been done by a human, but deity also for it to work for everyone."
- Nestorianism claims that Jesus is two distinct persons.
- Euticianism claims that Christ has 2 incomplete natures
- ★ Temporary functional limitation: By taking on humanity, Christ chooses not to exercise His power/knowledge.
