Paul's Doctrine of Sin
- **Nature of Sin**: "Active or Passive lack of conformity to the moral law"
	- Can be an act, thought, or disposition.
	- Pride & Idolatry are the chief root of all sin. It can be both at the same way.
	- "The flesh" (Sarx): Human beings in our totality have a corrupted, desire to sin.
(margin) Romans 1
- Corruption/Suppression of the truth → Idolatry.
- **Results of Sin**
	- (margin: [Con]) Consequences: Things that arise from the sin itself bc of the very nature of the sin.
	- (margin: [Pun]) Punishment: Something that can happen as a result of the sin. Example; consequence of staying up late is being tired, while punishment could be missing something bc you're tired.
	- [Con]: God "Gave them up" to the [Con] of their sin. He gave them up to impurity, unrighteousness.
	- [Pun]: They "Deserve" to die. It is a rational punishment, even from a human perspective.
	- Penalty of Death: Sin's penalty is death, & we can know that through general revelation. We have "conditional immortality" (Erickson 217)
	- We are held accountable bc we have natural law and are not fully ignorant.
	- Ignorance breeds more ignorance, & you don't feel guilty after a while (Erik. [?219?])
	- Weakness → Bondage of the will → Misery.
	- Noetic effect
	- <u>Promulgation of natural law</u>: how do we know it?
- **Magnitude of Sin**
	- Extent of sin:
		- Universal
		- Total depravity: "Sin is a matter of the entire person. (Erik. 227). We are always self-centered in our actions, & unable to extracate ourselves from our actions.
- **Original Sin**
	- Pelagianism: "We inherit no guilt or corruption from Adam."
	- Armenian: "We inherit corruption, but not guilt from Adam."
	- Calvinism: "We inherit both corruption & guilt from Adam."
		- Explained by federal or natural headship. Adam is our federal head, our representitive. He could also be our natural headship; we were all there in his sin. <u>Ericsson</u> agrees with natural headship, saying that we opt into having Adam as our head when we sin.
