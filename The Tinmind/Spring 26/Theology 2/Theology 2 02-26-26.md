Acts: The Church
- Unity of the Church:
	- Invisible: We are in the temple of the Holy Spirit. We are the branches to His Vine. The HS Creates & unifies the Church. Has a spiritual, organic unity.
	- Visible: We are part of the body of Christ. Has a unity of word/sacrament, not necessarily institutional authority.
	- Some issues are worth agreeing to disagree & going seperate ways.
- Church Government:
	- Episcopal: Believes in positions above elder that oversees multiple churches.
	- Presbyterian: Multiple levels of representive assemblies of elders.
	- Congregational: Church represents itself w/ its own people.
	- Non-governmental: No structure.
	- Acts could support a few different positions, but there isn't a straight answer, so a few <u>could</u> be correct.
	- Trelis (church) supports the Vine (life). You need both, but the Trelis doesn't need to be perfect. Balance is key.
- Functions of the Church:
	- Evangelism: Sharing with unbelievers.
	- Discipleship: Building up believers.
	- Worship.
	- Social Concern: The Church has the duty of the strong to the weak. (Upside-down K)
		- Property: Church is one of the biggest property right advocates.
		- Church & State conflict has been very important historically. One places limits on the other.
