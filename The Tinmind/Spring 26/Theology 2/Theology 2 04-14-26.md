**Holy History**
- Final events: Persecution in rome, destruction of the temple.
- Jews wanted to revolt and establish an earthly Kingdom, which God told them not to do.

**Public Ethics**
- In the world but not of it.
	- (margin: 1 Peter 2) We should recognize legitimate authorities in worldly government.
	- Seek to do good, regardless of circumstances.
	- We are elect exiles.
	- Demonic Pattern: Oppressive political power against the church that is supported by corrupt religion.
	- Right response to persecution: Suffer if necessary & avoid corruption.
	- Universalism is to be avoided; the world is sick & anything in it will not lead to Christ.

**Assurance of Salvation**
- Key question: do you have the Son? Have you been born again?
	- Do you show your love for God via sincere attempts at obedience?
	- Do you show your love for the church by loving God's people?
	- Do you confess your sins & failures?
	- The solution is here.
