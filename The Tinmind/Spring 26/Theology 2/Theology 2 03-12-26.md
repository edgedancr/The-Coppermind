- **Baptism:**
	- Passing through death to new life.
	- (margin: Views:) Pedo: (Erickson 412)
		- Catholic: Baptismal Regeneration; People are connected to God through church (over "church": "The institution")

		- A sign of regeneration that has already happened.

- **The Lord's Supper**
	- A symbol of union & Substitution
	- Transubstantiation (catholic): Elements become Christ's body & blood (over "Elements": "Physically")
	- (margin: Erickson 421) Consubstantiation (Lutheran): Physical presence of Christ, but without (an arrow points back up to "body & blood")
	- <u>Memorial</u>: Commemoration of Christ's death; just a symbol
	- Real spiritual Presence (Calvin): Christ has a spiritual presence only in communion.
