Matthew
- Early Ministry
- <u>Covenant Lawsuit</u> is continuing; Covenant Curses are coming.
- <u>Jesus' baptism</u>: Another sign that He is a cleansed, perfect, & true Israel.
- <u>Jesus</u> is the <u>Ladder</u> of Jacob, bringing us to Him and Him to us.
- 40 days like Israel. He is the better, perfect Adam. <u>Tempted</u> by Satan. He <u>refutes</u> him with only Deuteronomy 6-8.
- The Kingdom is at hand. Jesus is begining the process by which God's presence will fill the earth.
- <u>Sermon</u> on the Mount.
	- <u>Jesus</u> is uplifting the meek and humble, instead of the strong & mighty like <u>the rest</u> of the world.
	- <u>Jesus</u> is fulfilling the law. He set the <u>standard of</u> perfection. We can take part of that perfection via union w/ Jesus.
	- We must decide. Christ is bringing the Kingdom to us. We must accept Him or reject Him. But we cannot remain neutral.
	- His Kingdom will grow through the church over time.
