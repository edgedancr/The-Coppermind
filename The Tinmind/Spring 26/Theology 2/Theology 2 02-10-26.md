- <u>Cross & Atonement</u>
- Hypostatic Union:
	- Athanatius: Must be a union of both God & Man for it to work.
	- Anselm: Must be fully God & fully man.
- Theories of the Atonement
	- Moral Example – (Socinus) Jesus died to show us what a true act of love looks like.
	- Demonstration of Love – (Peter Abelard) Jesus showed us His love for us.
	- (margin: Allen →) Christus Victor – Defeats sin and Satan on the cross.
	- Satisfaction – (Anselm, Calvin). Jesus satisfied God's Justice.
	- (margin) All are correct. (a bracket joins the four theories)
- Details on Satisfaction:
	- <u>Substitutionary Penal Atonement.</u>
	- <u>Judicial Wrath</u>: God's wrath is justified, we broke His laws as the Imago Dei.
	- <u>Accountability</u>: God must see His justice carried out, because the moral order is objective
	- <u>Total Depravity</u>: We will always end with more sin than good; sin penetrates us in all areas of our life. This is handled by
	- Christ has been cut in two for us. He is the anti-typological Passover Lamb.
	- The sacrificial animals being spotless indicates a step towards equivalence.
	- Christ was intentional in His sacrifice. He has a much higher value that is more than equivalent for all human life. He takes on the judgement that we deserve.
	- <u>Propitiation</u>: There are consequences for sin that must be carried out.
	- <u>Expiation</u>: We need to be forgiven and "cleansed" of our sin. Cannot stand w/o (an arrow curls back up to Propitiation)
