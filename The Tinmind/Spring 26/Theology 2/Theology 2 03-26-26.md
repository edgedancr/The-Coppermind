<u>Justification</u>
- Protestant theology is birthed from Luther's realization of justification.
	- Sola Fide, Sola Gratia, Sola Scriptura, Solus Christus, Soli Deo gloria.
	- → About faith over works (from Sola Fide)
	- → About Grace over merrit. (from Sola Gratia)
(margin) Catholics (a bracket down the next three lines)
- Need to track your "merrit balance" in order to not be in purgatory too long.
- Christ's merrit covers eternal consequences of sin, while works cover the temporal punishments.
- C. Church is the "minister of redemption." They are the key to accessing Christ's gift.
- Alien Righteousness: We have a righteousness that is not our own. It is granted by grace through faith.
- <u>Adoption/union</u> (Erickson 360)
	- When we are saved, we inherit adoption.
	- We have a spiritual union with Christ. It is also judicial & vital.
