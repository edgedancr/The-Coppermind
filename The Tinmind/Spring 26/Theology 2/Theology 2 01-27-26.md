(margin) Themes in Mark:
- Second Exodus
- Christus Victor → Divine Warrior.
- Upside-down Kingdom
- The Messenger:
	- Gospel in Isaiah, etc... What is happening here is the same as the OT (Exodus, Malachi, etc...)
- "Repent for the KOG is coming."
	- By nature. Rule over the whole world.
	- By covenant. Rule over His people.
- Daniel's 4 Kingdoms (Daniel 2,8)
- Holy Spirit → Climatic Redemptive Judgement.
- The Mission:
	- Gathering of the apostles → Fishers of Men.
	- Messianic Secret: Jesus needs to buy time. He hides His identity to not be overwhelmed by the crowds, so He can train His apostles.
	- 12 apostles → 12 tribes, → Jesus is restoring Israel.
	- Any sin can be forgiven, except the rejection of God's truth of Christ.
	- Binding of the strong man → Christ Binding sin before setting us free.
- Stilling of the storm → Power of the creator over nature.
- Upside-down Kingdom
- Somethings belong to the earthly-Sovereign, and some are just for the Heavenly Sovereign.
- The triumph: A turning point
- Upside-down triumph
- Brokeness of Man
