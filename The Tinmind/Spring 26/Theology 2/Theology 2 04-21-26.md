**The Message of Revelation**
(margin) Themes:
- Persecution
- Totality
- God's Plan
- Christology + Theophany
- Letters to the Churches
	- The church is struggling, and it needs to overcome.
		- False Teaching
		- Suffering
		- Complacency
- Throne in Heaven (Beale 473-474)
	- Daniel 7: Sabath rest, reigning. Rend the heavens. Authority: Creator's rights. The Scroll. The lamb
- Seven Seals: Disasters throughout the world
	- 144,000. Who are they? Dispensationals say Israel. Beale says the Church. We don't really know. We know that it means that they have overcome.
- Seven Trumpets.
	- Perfect & complete judgement "woe, woe, woe" & lack of repentence.
	- <u>Angel</u> of the Lord.
- Two Witnesses.
	- 42 Months, Beale says it is the time between Christ's leaving & return.
	- Are the Two the church? People?

**The Message of Revelation Pt. 2**
- Woman & Dragon: Beale says it is a revelation of deeper conflict. Resembles the persecution of the church when God comes to save us.
(margin) Beale 482
