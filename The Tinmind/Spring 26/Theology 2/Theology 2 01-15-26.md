	Creation, commission, failure/succeed, redemption.
(margin) Adam (a line runs from here down the margin to Israel)
- Adam, supposed to take dominion & make a dynasty, represent God.
- Tripartite structure of reality:
	- Invisible Heavens, Visible Heavens, Habitible Earth.

(margin) King →
- Man was to welcome God into their lives, and prepare the earth for His coming. He was to explore & build.
(margin) Prophet →
- To explain scripture & God's word's to the people.
(margin) Priest →
- was to help bring redemption.
(margin) Israel →
- was to be a Kingdom of priests. A holy nation guided by the prophets
- They are to be a light to the other nations and redefine power & leadership.
(margin) Christ →
- The antitypical Prophet, Priest, & King.
- The Church is restored Israel, while Christ is true Israel.
- Christ succeeds where everyone else fails.
- Inaugurated Eschatology: Christ starts the later days.
- Eschatology hasn't been consumated yet, in an "already but not yet" way.
- There is more work that Christ will do.
