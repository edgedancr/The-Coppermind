Calling, Conversion, Regeneration
- **Election of Israel**
	- "All Israel"
	- True Israel? Church is Israel
	- (margin: Dispensational) Ethnic Jews?
- **Calling:**
	- (margin: Ⓒ) Effectual Calling: When God calls us, we respond. (added above: "& Irresistible grace.")
	- (margin: Ⓐ) Provideant Grace: We decide whether to respond or not. (Erickson 420ish)
	- (margin: Ⓒ) Monergism: "I work." God enlightens our will to see Him, which we respond to because it is the best, most perfect option.
- **Conversion**
	- Repentence & Faith.
	- Faith as belief that... content
	- Faith as belief in... a person (Erickson 354)
	- Becoming a Christian requires believing in Christ as our Lord & savior.
	- Being a Christian requires believing everything in the Bible.
- **Regeneration**
	- New birth; born again.
	- New picture of life. It gives us a reordered love, which makes us want to change & be better.
