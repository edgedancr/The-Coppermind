**Final States**
(margin) Hell:
- Nature of Hell:
	- Eternal Conscience Punishment: Lake of fire
	- Annihilation: Not a lot of support, but people do believe it.
- Finality of Hell:
	- (margin: **Erickson 477**) Restorationism: People will eventually repent and be let out of Hell.
	- Finality: Those in Hell will never escape. It will be eternal.
- (margin: 478) Justice:
	- Raising a finite will against God's infinite one ends with infinite punishment.
	- By necessity, we are responsible creatures.
	- Freedom: God gives us the freedom to reject Him, & He gives us the consequences of that.

**Final States Pt. 2**
(margin) Erikson 479
- Degrees of punishment in Hell
	- Darkness
	- Seperation from God
	- Punishment
- Retrospective Power of Heaven & Hell: People will remember the days of earth dully compared to their suffering in Hell or joy in Heaven.
(margin) Heaven:
- Present Heaven, where God is with His angles right now.
	- Soul Sleep: We are non-existant or unconscience after death before general res.
	- General view is that it is instant to die & go to present Heaven
- New Heavens & Earth; The New Creation
	- We'll have "Resurection Bodies"
	- Communion; we are enjoying God's presence.
	- New Earth will have a certain amount of continuity & change.
	- (margin: Erikson 472) A purified world: no evil, suffering, or death. Work & activity will be restful. There will be no opposing forces.
	- Different levels of rewards in heaven. Some will have greater joy, but there will be no envy
- Beatific Vison: We will be perfectly satisfied by God.
- Argument from Desire: All natural desires have something to satisfy them, except for the "longing" people feel in life for something greater. It's a longing for God.
