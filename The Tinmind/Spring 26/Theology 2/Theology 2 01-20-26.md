- 22/27 of NT book are being used as scripture by the begining of 2nd Century. News travels slowly, and church readers were being persecuted, so it took a little longer than it should have to spread.
- Other gospels appeared around 300–400 years after Christ.
- NT has way more early manuscripts than anything else.
- Most questions on translation errors and on what is canon are not major issues.
- Use of OT in NT:
- 5 Convictions:
	- OT is The word of God.
	- Christ represents true Israel.
	- Church is living in the Later days.
	- Prophetic Correspondance w/ OT.
	- NT is the interpretive key to the OT.
- 3 tiers of correspondance: (between OT & NT)
	- Quotation.
	- Allusion.
	- Concept.
- 7 interpretive uses:
	- Direct fulfillment.
	- Typological fulfillment. (indirect)
	- Analogy.
	- Symbol.
	- Abiding Authority.
	- Prototype/Blueprint.
	- Irony.
- The Gospels:
	- The genre is about lives, not legend.
	- Their audience is open.
	- The Synoptic problem. (Matthew, Mark, Luke.)
	- Augustinian
	- Gospel.
