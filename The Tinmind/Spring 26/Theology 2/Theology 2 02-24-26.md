**UNIT 2**
Acts: Work of the Holy Spirit.
- Ascension & **Session**:
	- Final question of the disciples: Gives us the mission & Pilgrim Politics. Ps 110:1
	- Multiply & take dominion is being fulfilled through the word of God. **Beale 155**
	- Pentacost is an inversion of Tower of Babel. It brings people together under a beautiful unity. Everyone has their own culture/language, but are brought together in Christ.
- Work of the Holy Spirit:
	- In Salvation:
		- Uniting us to God.
		- Conversion: Conviction of guilt/repentence → Repentence & Faith.
		- Regeneration: Indwelling/union → Sanctification, Resurrection, & Glorification.
	- In Empowering:
		- Empowering, Gifting, Filling, Equiping.
		- OT has gifting for some. NT has gifting for all.
		- Lists of gifts: Positions, Talents, Abilities
		- God has called us to use our gift to serve the body of believers.
- ★ Cesationism vs. Continuationism
	- History: Montinism, about 100 years after close of the canon, says gifts remain. Unpopular, and dies out until it's return in 1700–1900s with carismatic Movement.
	- Arguments for continuationism: Bible doesn't say they end, & experiences.
	- Arguments for Cesationism: Almost no history of miracle gifts. "Tongues will cease" 1 Cor. 13:8, though it could be refering to only the end times. Argue that gifts were to identify apostles, not normal believers.
	- Either way, Fruit of the Spirit is more important.
