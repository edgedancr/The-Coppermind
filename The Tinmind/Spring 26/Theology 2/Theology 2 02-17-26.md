- <u>Evidence for the Resurection</u>
- 2 things that even non-Christians accept:
	- Jesus died
	- His followers wrote about Him.
- Explanation for those 2:
	1) Died, Rose → (Christianity)
	2) Died, Didn't rise → Myth, not literal, disciples meant it as metaphor or story.
	3) Died, Didn't rise → Hallucination, disciples misunderstood
	4) Died, Didn't rise → Conspiracy, disciples knew & decieved people.
	5) Didn't die, Didn't rise → Swoon theory.
- Arguments against:
	5) Romans were efficient, they wouldn't have let Him live. Who moved the stone?
	4) No one defects, all the disciples are persecuted & 11 are martyred. Why didn't they give up after Christ left? Why did they do it? they gained nothing. The Jews wouldn't accept it as a conspiracy, because the Jews weren't expecting it.
	3) Hallucinations aren't shared. Jesus proved Himself. People would've figured out.
	2) Gospels are concise, not written to be a fantastical story. Myths were not written that close to the event. Women wouldn't be eyewhitnesses. Peter says it's not a myth
