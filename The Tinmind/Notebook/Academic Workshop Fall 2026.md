### Freedom's Foundations

1. Dr. Bayer is far, far smarter than you, so don't feel like a fraud when you don't track with her side tangents.
2. Class is almost entirely lecture-based, when she mentions a page number, write it down!
3. Big fan of understanding terms as the original authors define them, and her questions reflect that.
4. KEY: Pay attention to big, overarching concepts.
5. Understand terms ***as the author describes them.***

- Homework
	- The only homework is the reading assignments
	- Q: "How much do I need to read?" A: "How much do you understand what the author is saying?"
	- The only thing that matters is that you understand people's opinions on the concepts talked about in class.
	- Pick an author to really engage with.
	- Two unexcused absences that are not for a college-sanctioned event. If you are sick, get an RA's signature.
	- If you use AI, you get flogged with the Constitution.
	- DO NOT GET CREATIVE WITH BAYER.
- Tests
	- Two bluebook essays, a midterm and a final.
	- KEY: She will ask questions on big pictures, ideologies, and their authors. Be prepared to compare and contrast between philosophers and political theorists.
	- Memorize quotes from the books if you can, it will help a lot.
	- The more details you put in, the better you do, but she still prioritizes overarching themes.
	- Be accurate, be real, do not exaggerate.
	- Make a dynamic observation.
	- She will send out study questions. The answers will not be directly on the test, but the themes will be very important.
	- Don't give historical context, don't give your commentary, give exact definitions of what the authors' believe.
- Paper
	- One 5-6 page essay.
	- She gives a list of topics to write on; any other topics must be approved by her.
	- The way to success is demonstrating your understanding of what the author is saying only engaging afterwards if necessary. 
- Strategies
	- She will break down the reading section by section in class. Anytime she spends more than ten minutes on a topic, idea, or keyword, write it down. It will most likely be a blue book question.
	- Again, her priority is for you to understand what the text is saying, not necessarily to engage it unless you are truly brilliant (she does changer her grading for the class depending on how good your paper is)
	- Group study can be helpful (with the right people) because you can tell if you are way off of what she wants.
- General Success Tips
	- Memorize quotes for blue book essays
	- Be exhaustive in your essays; she penalizes you for missing things and will not always give you feedback or explain your grade.
	- Ask questions after class.
	- If you are drowning in work and papers, pick a couple authors and really study up on those If at all possible, hit all of the reading. Be a philosopher.
