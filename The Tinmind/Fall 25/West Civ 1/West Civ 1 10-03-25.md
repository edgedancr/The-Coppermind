- The Regal Period of Rome: 753–509 B.C.
- The Romans were fighters & lovers (Roma, amor)
- Livy likes the Republic, Augustus liked the empire.
- Scholars don't like Livy as much, because he has a lot of events that seem unlikely.
- Senate (means "group of all guys"), but still has Kings
- The Republic (509–30 B.C.)
- Tarquin Superbus "proud Tarquin" Rich, "noble", tries to take over stuff
- Brutus leads the senate to overthrow the monarchy, then becomes the first ruler.
- The revolution starts with an outrage over Sextus making a penelope-like girl, Lucretia, kill herself. Sets the standard for women killing themselves for honor. (Dido)
(margin) Rome
- Huge city, first city with 1 million people, not farmers anymore, need 3M slaves a year. Identity Crisis.
- Greece lost to Rome politically, but won culturally.
- Catullus: wrote Greek-style poetry.
- They like their vices, but need a cure.
