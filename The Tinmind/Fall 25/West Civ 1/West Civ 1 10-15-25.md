- Rome persecutes Christians, starting with Steven.
(margin) early church (an arrow runs up and down beside the next lines)
- Variations in severity across Rome. They thought Christians were atheists.
- Domitian tries to boil John, sends him to patmos
- Flavia gets her husband (who is a consular) to AHS so that Christians can escape.
- Tertius wrote Romans for Paul.
- Eulalia destroys a statue's nose, chooses to be a martyr.
- Decius in 250 AD says to hunt all the Christians down.
- The church is wondering if people who recant and said can truly be Christians.
- Cyprian says that Christians can only be saved by bishops, which is the start of bishops having power and Apostolic Succession.
- Porphyry & Celsus write books on why people shouldn't be Christians
