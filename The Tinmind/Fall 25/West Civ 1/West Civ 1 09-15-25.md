- Golden age Athens: 479 B.C. – 338 B.C.
- <u>Read the written lectures.</u>
- Pericles voted strategos (general) 29 years in a row. He had radical democracy, striving for equality (1 vote, homeless, etc...)
- Isonomia
- Ekklesia: everyone gets one vote, everyone can draft a bill.
- They elected people as magistrates by random
- Athenians have an unwritten rule that assumes base level education
- Athenians cared about loyalty. If you weren't willing to die in battle then you couldn't vote.
- The rich had to do nice things for the poor (Evergetism)
- Ostracism: they would vote 1 person to leave for 10 years, once a year.
- Acropolis (citadel), Parthenon (to Athena), important buildings.
