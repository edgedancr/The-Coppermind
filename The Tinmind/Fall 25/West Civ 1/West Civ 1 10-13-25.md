(margin) The way
- Jesus was called Ioshua Bar Ioshef.
- Intertestamental Period: 400 years up to AD.
- 168 BC. Macc. Rebellion, The Jews start splitting into pro-hellenistic and anti-hellenistic. Some stop following old laws and participate in Greek culture; speaking Greek, writing plays, etc...
- Sadducees were pro-greek, Pharisees were traditional, basically started a civil war
- Romans were nice to the Jews until around 30 AD.
- Essenes were Jews who made remote communities, maybe dead sea scrolls from them
- Sicarii: "dudes with knives" thought killing romans would make Jesus come.
