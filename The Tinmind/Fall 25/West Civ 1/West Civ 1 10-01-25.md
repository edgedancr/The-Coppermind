- Rome is the cultural ancestor of the west. We use similar letters, food, government, etc...
- Cato 1 and 2, upheld republican values, roman family values.
- Cicero: Best lawyer ever, great speaker, founding fathers love him.
- Res nova = new thing, means weird.
- Brutus & Cassius, helped overthrow Ceasar? (an arrow from here up to Cicero)
- Cincinnatus, retired from soldier, became a farmer, they wanted him back, made him "dictator," or "he who talks the most," then retires for good
- Fasces, weapon, unity of sticks, fascist comes from here
- Rome becomes an empire. At it's height, it had 25% of the world population
- Bronze Age (1500–1200 BC?) indo-europeans become Romans in Italy.
- Etruscans, weird language, unknown, not indo-european, have a slightly positive view of the afterlife, also had a better view of women.
- (margin: Roman Values:) Natio: "Follow our laws and be part of the culture, and you're in". This makes them a melting pot of people
- Hannibal, bad guy, killed lots (50,000 in 1 hour)
