- Bring laptop for midterm. REVIEW ESSAY PROMPTS
- Ideas: Pericles
(margin) Peloponnesian War
- The Socrates – Pericles generation "died" in the war, and the Athenians turned into power-hungry "might makes right" people.
- Alcibiades: seduced Socrates, betrayed Athens, convinced them to invade Sicily
- Sparta takes over Athens, chooses 30 Athenian tyrants to be backed by Spartan soldiers.
- Daimon – Spirit, like an internal spirit.
(margin) The Hellenistic Era
- Era of Greek dominance in culture. (3-400 BC). They throw away the ideas of past generations. They give up on Arete
- Macedon: Alexander's father, which the Greeks considered barbarians, and uncultured, like hillbillys.
