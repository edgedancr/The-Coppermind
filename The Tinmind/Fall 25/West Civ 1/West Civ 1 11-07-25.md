- Early medieval is 600–1000. They loved Roman-esque archetecture. Low, squat.
- High-Medieval liked Gothic Art (French-style): Thin walls, soaring heights, stained glass. (Notre Dame), Gargoyles.
- Revolution of Love. The Romans sort of sanitized erotic love, which meant there was little love poetry in the E.M.A.
- Troubadours: travelling French poets.
- Court of Love, Laws of Love, by Andreas Capellanus
- Scholasticism: Thomas Aquinas → Faith & Reason combining. Aristotle gets re-discovered.
