- Hebrews think in tribes. They lean rural/authoritarian.
- Greeks think in political citizenship. They are functional theists.
- Greek character: Humanism – Man is the measure of all things
	- Paideia "culture", "Man is a political animal". They teach how to be a Greek
	- Eleutheria: Freedom to be virtuous
	- Parrhesia: Boldness, "Freedom of speech"
	- Logos: Natural law
	- Kritis: Judgement
- Greeks refine Lyric poetry (to be played with a Lyre) in 600 B.C.
	- Archilochus & Sappho for epics, in dactylic hexameter. Very short poems. Sappho was the most famous female poet
