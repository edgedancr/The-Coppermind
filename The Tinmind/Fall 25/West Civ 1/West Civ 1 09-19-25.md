- Thucydides said that the war between Athens & Sparta was caused by Paranoia. "<u>Thucydides Trap</u>" is the term used for war based on fear of competition
- Herodotus <u>wants to know causes</u> of the wars. He asks "are my sources accurate?"
- Pact with the dead – We owe the dead for their contributions to society
- (margin: West Civ:) We are a free people, and we have the power.
- Unwritten laws of society for honor.
- Scholae: Scholarship which brings rest, honor, and Arete
- Krypteia: secret police for Sparta, not for "civilized" west (an arrow from the "West Civ" margin note points here)
- Delian League: A confederacy meant for protecting, but it became "Pay Athens so your windows don't get broken."
- (margin: 470 B.C. ish) Sparta teams up with Corinth and Thebes to form their own league
(margin) Peloponnesian War 431–404 B.C.
- Spartan Paranoia
- Athens build huge walls, stores everyone inside, then a huge plague breaks out
- Athens invade Sicilia to plunder for money, lead by Alcibiades.
- Melian Dialogue, Natural law, but add "Might makes right"
