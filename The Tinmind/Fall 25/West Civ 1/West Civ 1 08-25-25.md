- Contrapposto: a pose for a statue made by the Greeks.
- No dates on tests. :)
- God is "To exist"
- ★ 4 Periods: Creation, Fall, **Redemption**, Restoration. There is one point of redemption, Christ on the cross, but also micro redemption with all of our good works. We make the world a little less ugly.
- ★ Four views of history:
	1. Colony of heaven. the world is bad, so we should stay away. Examples: Amish, Menenites
	2. 2 kingdoms. Focus on individuals. Don't worry about the big picture, because we can't change it anyway.
	3. Transformationalist: We will transform the world. They will do what we want.
	4. Compatibleist: We can all get along. You do you, and I do me. It'll work out.
- Memento Mori: Remember you will die. What comes after?
- ★ Civilization is man applying order to life.
- ★ West Civ: The <u>legacy</u> of Greece, Rome, and Judeo-Christianity.
- America stole A LOT from Greece and Rome.
