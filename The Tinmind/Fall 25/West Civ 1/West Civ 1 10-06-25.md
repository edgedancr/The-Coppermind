- (margin: 1st Triumvirate) Pompey invades Israel and never leaves. Paul's grandpa is an Israelite who fights for Rome, and gains citizenship?
- Roman civil war (a long arrow runs from here down the margin to the Pompey & Crassus line)
- Gracchi brothers: Tiberius & Gaius. Tiberius trying to figure out Republic with farms
- Yeoman (homesteader, small farm) vs. Latifundia (Big farm)
- Populares (common people) vs. Optimates (smart people) ruling
- Mos maiorum: "The way of our ancestors". The Romans have no written constitution and use verbal tradition.
- Virtus: like Arete, but more family/communal focussed.
- Tiberius proposes a bill to give everyone land, but is killed by the sennate, and the same happends to his brother a few years later.
- Volkswanderung: the mass migration of the german peoples 1100 BC–700 BC. A bunch of tribes, which is why their name is different across languages.
- Gaius Marius saves Rome from invading Germans (104–102)
- Creates a professional army with 20 year enlistments, but becomes the consul for 6 years in a row.
- Marius has an apprentice, Sulla, who starts a civil war with him. Sulla marches on Rome, and Marius has no army in Rome, so he frees the slaves and gives them weapons, but that fails and he runs and dies.
- Pompey & Crassus need a 3rd, and invites a random dude Julius Caeser.
- Quid pro quo: "what for what" or "what can I do for you to do for me"
- Gravitas: like the weighty-ness of a political figure.
