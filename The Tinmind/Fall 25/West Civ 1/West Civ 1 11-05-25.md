- (margin: High Middle Ages – 1000–1300) Castles start appearing: state power starts growing, papal power is fading.
- The great Schism I (1054): Pope & Archbishop excommunicate each other. then, all the east is excommunicated. People get skeptical and take any chance they can to leave the Catholic power.
- Investiture conflict: who gets to make a bishop? Political/theological authority (an arrow from the Schism line)
- King Henry denounces the pope. The pope puts a bounty on the king
- Henry finesses the pope, who dies, then Henry gets power.
- (margin: 1095–1099) 1st Crusade: Turks start attacking and killing the messengers etc.. people fought back as a valid military response. (Battle of Manzikert)
- However, the Crusaders were kinda bad, killed a lot, brutal.
- Indulgentia: Pope Urban II preemptively forgives all their sins in the crusade.
