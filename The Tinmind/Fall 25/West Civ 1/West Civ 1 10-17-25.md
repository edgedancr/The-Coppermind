- There were a bunch of bogus gospels (Thomas, Philip), but the early church knows they are fake.
- Justin Martyr dies after going to caeser defending Christianity.
- Christians would meet in the catacombs, the Romans didn't care much bc they hated death, so they left them alone.
(margin) Heresy: (an arrow joins the next five lines)
- Gnosticism: Christians should mortify the body.
- Ebionites: Jesus was fully God, not at all man.
- Marcionites: OT God liked Jews, NT God hates Jews.
- Montanists: Christians must have a charismatic experience when worshiping.
- Donatus: Said people who recant are not/cannot be Christians.
- Constantine calls himself the 13th apostle, He passes the Edict of Milan, making it legal to be a Christian.
- Christianity grows from a couple hundred at the night of Jesus' death to 3 million in 313 AD.
