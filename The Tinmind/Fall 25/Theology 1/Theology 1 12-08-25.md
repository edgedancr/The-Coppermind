(margin) Study Session
- Unconditional = Land grant, conditional = suzeran-vassal treaty
- Themes:
	- Mountain of God: Place of assembly for redemptive judgement (above: "↙ Eden")
	- Battle of the Seeds; Woman vs serpent. Who will rule?
	- Typology; Anti-Typological Kingdom. – Exodus as examples of salvation
	- Redemptive judgement
	- Har Magedon
	- Dominion & Dynasty
	- Prophet, Priest, & King
- Covenants: 1) Inter-trinitarian. 2) Adamnic 3) Proto-Gospel 4) Common Grace (Cain) 5) Noaic 6) Abrahamic 7) Moses 8) Davidic 9) New covenant
- Promises to Abraham:
	- Seed
	- Land
	- Name
- Triparte-structure of creation: Invisible heavens, Visable heavens, visable earth (over "Invisible heavens": "most holy place, eden")
- Endoxation of the spirit: The spirit has physical form with Christ, forms the glory cloud, the invisable heavens.
- City of God/Man. Jews are in pilgrim politics, living under city of man for city of God
- 722 Assyria takes Israel. 587 Babylon takes Judah.
- Judges: covenant lawsuit. Created desire for a King (human).
- Upside down Kingdom: Christ's Kingdom is about humbling people and serving others, instead of exalting and serving themselves. Hannah's song talks about this concept.
