(margin) Isaiah
- Very much like a gospel.
- The "remnant" of Israel is left, from which Christ will come. He is "true Israel"
- Christ is the Victor, champion. The gospel is "your God reigns".
- He is the perfect substitutionary attonement, sprinkling His own blood. He was part of the corporate Adam, and thus partook in the curses.
- Salvation of OT people is like paying a credit card. God knew that Christ would pay their debt.
- Jesus will come back, and He will make the world glorious again.
