- (margin: Theology Proper) God's Trancendance. God is "other" from the world.
- God is also immanent. He is "God with us"!
- (margin: Negative Theo) Not setting limits on God by placing Him with the things of the world.
- Economic Trinity: God's work for our Salvation. This reveals → Imminent Trinity: God in Himself
- God is rational. He is logic.
(margin) Heresy: (a bracket joins the next two lines)
- Modalism: The 3 persons are actually three different modes
- Tri-theism: The 3 persons are actually three different Gods.
- Psycological Trinity: Finding the 3 starting with the 1
- Attributes of God: Neccesary – Trancendent – Not contingent – He is one, not made of many things. – Constant & Immutable – He has life & being. – Personal – Infinite; without limits – He has all power – Rational & Logical
