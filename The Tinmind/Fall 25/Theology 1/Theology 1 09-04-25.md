- Typology. A sign of something to come. David is a type of Jesus.
- Suzerain-Vassal treaty. If we do good things, we are blessed, and vice-versa
- Land grant. An unconditional covenant. "I give to you, sign on the dotted line"
- Creation ex nihilo – creation from nothing
- If God didn't create the world Himself, then the world would be seperate from Him.
- Upper Register – Invisible heavens
- Lower Register – Earth and space.
- Indexation of the Spirit – Heaven is quite litterally the spiritual body of the Holy Spirit (Spiritual realm)
- The Earth was "good" not perfect. the human project was for the purpose of developing the Earth
- God created everything according to it's kind. Micro-evolution (Humans adapting to cold environments, etc...) is accepted, not Macro (dog to fish, etc...)
