(margin) Ezekiel
- The presence of God is leaving Israel bc Israel has become an abomination. He won't return until He comes as a baby.
- God promises that He will atone for Israel, despite them breaking His covenant.
- God is going to unite Israel's enemies against Israel in order to ennact judgement against them.
- God will be their Shepherd, and will give them a rebirth.
