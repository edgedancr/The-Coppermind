(margin) Exodus:
- The corporate Adam:
	- Presence of divine glory.
- Contest w/ Pharoah: each plague mocks a god.
- God hardens Pharoahs heart; withholding common grace? He might be enacting judgement. He gave him time to repent, and Pharoah refused.
- Passover points to Christ; God's firstborn instead of ours.
- If you are willing to accept the covenant you are a native of God's people. They have communion with God.
- Kingdom of God by nature: He is King of all things regardless.
- K.o.G. by covenant: He wants His people to willingly follow Him, and consent.
- Suzerin–Vassal treaty
- Israel doesn't want to obey, but still wants God's blessings.
- They have a covenant of conditions where if they follow God He will bless them, and vice-versa.
- They break it, and Moses has to appeal to the unconditional covenant of success made with Abraham
