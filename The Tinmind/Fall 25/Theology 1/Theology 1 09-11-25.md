> [!drawing] Creation, sin, exile, restoration
> A box: "Creation" with an arrow up to "Sin"; beside it an X of arrows between "Sin", "Exile" (below) and "Restoration" (upper right).
> scan: IMG_0836.jpg

- Cain kills his brother, that can make him "the seed of the serpent", founds the "City of Man"
- Spiral of desire: like an addiction
- Sin creates a struggle for power in family where the wife wants to lead and the husband dominated the position. (from "position": "God?")
- Common grace: the force that keeps humans from doing the most evil in every action.
- Spiral of desire ends with the flood generation
- Seperation from God leads to the boasting of and thirst for power.
- City of God: (People trying to follow Christ from seed of the woman)
	- Pilgrim politics: We have to work together even though we are dissevant. Israel did this from the patriarchs – Moses
	- Typological Kingdom: Israel between Moses and Babylonian exile.
	- Anti-Typological Kingdom: Christ's Kingdom at the end of time.
	- Exile brought Israel back to (an arrow loops back up to Pilgrim politics), up until the second coming of Jesus.
- God gave a hint to substitutionary attonement when He killed a sheep to clothe Adam & Eve
- Noah is a type of Christ. He is a savior of the people; someone who will restore the land.
