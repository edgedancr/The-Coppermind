(margin) The Law: (arrows run from this label down to the end of the Leviticus lines)
- Civil: Laws about specific punishments for sin.
- Ceremonial: The way Israel is to engage in worship and respect for God.
- Moral: What is right and wrong as humans made in God's image.
(margin) Leviticus:
- Christ fulfilled the laws. We still have laws that He gave, but not all. Moral law still applies, but the penalty has been fulfilled.
- We were imprisoned under the law → Shows that we need a savior.
- The law restrains sin and acts as a guardian.
- OT Jews were saved by trusting in God that it would somehow work out if they honestly tried to honor God and follow His word.
(margin) Numbers:
- Israel fails so miserably at keeping the law.
- God ensures that Israel will succeed through their King, Jesus.
(margin) Deuteronomy:
- Israel should not be **IN** or **OF** the world. The people should be different, not like the rest of the world. They should not manipulate or go outside of the rules and laws.
- Israel is to destroy their enemies, not enslave. They are to be put to death. Just like how the Jews from Egypt had to die out before taking the promised land.
