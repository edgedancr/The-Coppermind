- God has divine authority, meaning that He can define reality, command it,
- (margin: Calvin) Dependence: God has "maker's right." He has every right to control us, and we have complete dependence on Him.
- (margin: Scotus) Analytic truth: Using definitions, context, and testing to understand meaning.
- Wisdom: Moral obligations arise from our circumstances. (Social requirement theory) God is the only one who can know what is required at a certain time, because only He knows everything and all context. So, He is allowed to rule us (?) because of His great wisdom.
- (margin: Locke) Power: Law has to be backed by power, so that punishment and reward can be carried out through the law
