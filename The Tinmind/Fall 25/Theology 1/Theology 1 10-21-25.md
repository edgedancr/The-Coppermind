(margin) Judges:
- Covenant Lawsuit: God laying out the rules and using them to ennact His judgement on His people, since they violated His covenant.
- Israel has fraternized with the enemies of God, which leads to idols and immorality
- The cycle of sin highlights a need for change. Judges suggest a King.
(margin) Samuel:
- Hannah's song shows the upside-down Kingdom.
- Israel is corrupted (shocker). They don't want God as King, so, He gives them what they ask for, King Saul. He's a bad king (shocker again)
- David vs Goliath is a microcosm of harmagedon.
- David comes up with the temple; God never asks him to build it.
- Everytime someone has a mountaintop, they fall. David falls w/ Bathsheeba which points to the need for Christ as our King.
