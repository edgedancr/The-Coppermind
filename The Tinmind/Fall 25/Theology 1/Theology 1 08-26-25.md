- Exams are about our ability to understand the discussions.
- Term Paper is the biggest, most important assignment.
- Use things like Google Scholar, not AI
- Follow what you are interested in.
- Prolegomena
- Systematic theology: start with a logical question, then see what the Bible says.
- Biblical theology: start with reading, then find themes
(margin) Theology is:
1. Living. It's ongoing and active
2. Rational.
3. Communal. People have been reflecting on the Bible for thousands of years.
4. Practical. It changes how we live.
5. Dependent. We must use the Bible, and God's revealing of Himself to us.
- General Revelation: What we can know about God without Him speaking to us. Natural Law is what we know about morality
- Special Revelation: What we know with what God tells us.
(margin) Styles:
- Personal. We are made in His image, and can participate as children of God
- Anthropic. Special Revelation uses human language. We <u>can</u> understand.
- Analogical. He helps us understand with analogies.
- Progressive. It moves from seed to fruit
- Public. Everyone can see what the Bible says. There is no private revelation.
- Authoritative. He has all power. No one has more.
