- The goodness of God.
- Intellectualism: God commands something because it is right. (arrows join this line and the next two)
- Voluntarism: Things are right because God commands them.
- Middle ground: God is a law unto Himself.
- (margin: Moral goodness and value:) God is the most valuable thing. Everything else is worth as much as it relates to God. Chain of being: Realness, life, desire, human beings, angels, God (the only neccesary being) (above "life": "plants"; above "desire": "Animals")
- Heart: The aspect of a person by their moral direction.
- We ought to be united in heart to the right extent with everything we encounter. This is why God's purpose is to glorify Himself.
- (margin: Moral evil:) Something with love doing evil. (People)
- (margin: Natural evil:) Something with no love doing evil. (huricane, etc...)
- God is perfectly good and love. He needs nothing.
- He has perfect judgement, yet wonderful mercy & grace.
