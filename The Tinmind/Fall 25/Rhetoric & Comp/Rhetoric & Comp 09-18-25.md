- ★ <u>Just start writing</u>. Ideas will flow once they start.
- When first researching your project topic, use dictionaries & encyclopedias
- Be aware of your audiences. Are they scholarly or general?
- Evaluate your sources: C.A.R.S.
	- Current: Is it up to date?
	- Authoritative: Does the author have the credentials?
	- Relevancy: Is the subject relevant?
	- Scholarly: Is the work professional?
- ★ Beware of copyright laws
