**Intelligence in National Security: Definition in 7 Points**

**1 “Information about those who would do us harm.”** (*Maureen Baginski*)

- *What*: Plans, intentions, capabilities especially about Existential Threats

- *Types*: Puzzles, Mysteries Reduce uncertainty, estimate proximate reality to avoid surprise & help decision makers at all levels (Strategic, operational, tactical, tech) make informed decisions

- *Progression*: Based on data (machine readable), info (human readable and collated or aggregated), knowledge (contextualized, analyzed, modeled to obtain meaning), wisdom (predictive, time-sensitive, actionable; in relentless *pursuit of* truth)

**2 Products:** Need to be tailored to scope, mission and user perspectives (*Mark Lowenthal*)

- *Scope*: Strategic (Largest scope, long-term), Operational (Regional, mid-term), Tactical (line-of-sight, immediate), Technical (threat capabilities/performance/vulnerabilities, engineering data)

- “*Good*”: Timely; Tailored to the user; Digestible-succinct and clear; Stating known and unknown

- *Missions*: Counter-intelligence; Denial & Deception; Allied or collaboration; Covert ops

**3 Process:** (*Mark Lowenthal*)

- *Cycle*: **T**asking, **C**ollection (HUMINT, OSINT, MASINT, FININT, SIGINT-COMINT-ELINT-FIS, ADTECH/ABI-POL), **P**rocessing, **E**xploitation/Analysis, **D**issemination

- *Classification*. SECRET, TOP SECRET, SCI Compartments; Need to Know; ORCON, LE Sensititive

**4 Organization: US Intel Community** formalized in EO12333 (*Mark Lowenthal*)

- 17 Agencies and DNI; missions and expertise –\> Functional managers (HUMINT, GEOINT, MASINT, SIGINT); All-source Producers: CIA, DIA, State Dept

- 6 Program Managers; 5 Departmental organizations; and **6 Military** Service intel organizations

**5 Provide information and decision support to wide range of intelligence Users**

- Policymakers, Law Enforcement, Diplomats, Military activities

- National, State, local, Tribal, and international organizations

**6 Intelligence Themes from history are persistent issues** (*Gordon Middleton*)

- *Categories*:
  *- Organization* (Role of culture; Collection synergy; Redundant analytic structure),
  *- People* (Strat v Tactical; Policy maker’s use of intel; Politization),
  *- Process* (Consumer-Producer Relations; Secrecy v Openness; D&D; Mirror Imaging),
  *- Technology* (Reliance for Collection; Reliance for Anal/Dissem; Encryption issues)

- *Moral/Ethical Principles* required in intel: Due to need for powerful tools & authority; and
  - Inclination of human nature to use power for selfish ends: Scripture-Love Neighbor & Enemy;
  - *Malevolent empathy* (*Dell Tacket*)-Just War; IC Directives/Agency Regs; Const/US law & International conventions

**7 Currently, intel is in *INFORMATION* business AND in *SECRETS* business** (*Greg Treverton/Rich Haver*)

- Requires new thinking, tools, process, and restructure vs leftovers from the Cold War

- New actors among those “who would do us harm”

- New tools and technologies for implementing “new harms”
