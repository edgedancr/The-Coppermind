## Slide 1

The Future Intelligence Analyst

Connector – use sophisticated digital tools to find, package, and deliver information to key users.
Curator – present the information so as to highlight decision-relevant insights.
Collaborator – work with the broad intelligence ecosystem and with machines.
Creator – imagine, hypothesize, visualize, and design new perspectives.
Critic – challenge methods, reasoning, logic and inferences in intelligence products.
Content-expert – draw on a well of diverse subject-matter expertise.

Adapted from, The Future of National Intel. How Emerging Technologies Will Reshape Intel Communities. By Shay Hershkovitz, 2019.
