Welcome to the Arena

Arena is building the definitive platform for AI evaluation. Our goal is to advance AI development by rigorously measuring model performance and delivering tangible real-world value.

Our Mission

To measure and advance the frontier of AI for real-world use.

Our Vision

To build the foundation for everyone to understand, shape, and benefit from AI.

Our Story

Created by researchers from UC Berkeley, Arena (formerly LMArena) was founded to help understand AI performance in the real world. The platform, previously known as Chatbot Arena, quickly gained notoriety in the AI industry as the gold standard of evaluation. In 2025, we formed Arena Intelligence Inc. and raised funding from a16z and The University of California. Since then, we have been on a mission to obsessively measure everything and advance AI research for the benefit of humanity.

Backed by world class investors

With $250 million in venture funding from leading investors, we’re advancing our mission to make AI evaluation transparent, rigorous, and trusted across the industry.
Funding led by Felicis and UC Investments (University of California), with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, The House Fund, LDVP, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Laude Ventures.
