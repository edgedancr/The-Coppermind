# Step 6 — Report

## Objective

Produce the final ACH analytic product with explicit confidence levels, caveats, and forward-looking indicators.

## Prompt

```
Draft the final ACH report using the following structure. This is an analytic product — write in a professional, concise intelligence style.

**ANALYTIC QUESTION**
Restate the question clearly in one sentence.

**BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF)**
State the leading hypothesis in 1-2 sentences. Include an explicit confidence level: High, Moderate, or Low. In one sentence, justify the confidence level by referencing the strength of diagnostic evidence and the fragility (or robustness) identified in the sensitivity analysis.

**HYPOTHESIS RANKINGS**
List all hypotheses from most likely to least likely. For each, provide:
- A 2-3 sentence summary of why it was ranked where it was
- Reference to the specific diagnostic evidence items that most influenced its position
- The I/II count from the matrix

**KEY DIAGNOSTIC EVIDENCE**
List the 3-5 evidence items that most influenced the conclusion. For each, note which hypotheses it distinguished between and its verification status.

**LINCHPINS AND VULNERABILITIES**
List the assumptions and evidence items on which the conclusion depends. For each linchpin:
- What it is and why the conclusion depends on it
- Current confidence in the linchpin (High / Medium / Low)
- What would happen to the conclusion if the linchpin were removed or reassessed

**INFORMATION GAPS AND COLLECTION REQUIREMENTS**
List what is unknown and what specific additional information would most improve the analysis. Frame these as actionable collection or research tasks where possible.

**MILESTONES FOR FUTURE OBSERVATION**
List 3-5 specific, observable events or indicators that should trigger a reassessment. Frame each as:
"If [observable event], then [hypothesis X] becomes more/less likely because [reason]."
These should be concrete and monitorable, not vague.

**DISSENTING VIEW (if applicable)**
If the devil's advocate analysis in Step 5 revealed a credible alternative that was not fully resolved, present it here as a minority view with its supporting logic and the conditions under which it would become the leading hypothesis.

**METHODOLOGY NOTE**
State that this analysis was conducted using the Analysis of Competing Hypotheses methodology (Heuer, 1999) with AI assistance. Note the evidence mode used (analyst-provided, AI-researched, or hybrid) and flag that all AI-researched evidence items were subject to analyst verification.
```

## Analyst Checkpoint (Final)

This is your last review. The output of this step is the deliverable.

- **Hallucination check:** Read every factual claim in the report. Is it real? Is it sourced? If any AI-researched evidence made it into the final product without verification, remove it or flag it now.
- **Confidence calibration:** Is the stated confidence level appropriate given the number, quality, and fragility of linchpins? A conclusion with two single-source, unverified linchpins should not be presented at High confidence.
- **Milestones check:** Are the milestones specific and observable? "The situation gets worse" is not a milestone. "Country X deploys additional naval assets to the contested area within 90 days" is a milestone.
- **Dissent check:** If the devil's advocate step surfaced a real concern, is it represented fairly in the dissenting view section? Omitting credible dissent undermines the integrity of the product.
- **Edit as you would any analytic product.** This is your report, not the AI's. Rewrite, restructure, and refine before dissemination.
