## What It Is
C. S. Lewis's essay "On Living in an Atomic Age," first published in 1948, as reprinted in *Present Concerns: Essays by C.S. Lewis*, edited by Walter Hooper (New York: Harcourt Brace Jovanovich, 1986), pp. 73–80. It is assigned in SAIL Module 5. Lewis argues that the atomic bomb should not dominate our minds, that the real question it raises is whether Nature is all that exists, and that naturalism cannot account for human reason or values. The file is a five-page retyped PDF; citations use its page numbers (pp. 1–5), not the book's. The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.
