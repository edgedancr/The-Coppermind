## What It Is
An Intro to Intelligence spreadsheet, "Intel Themes – Summary of Texts," that defines the course's 13 themes in U.S. intelligence, grouped as Organization, People, Process, and Technology. Each theme's definition is built mostly from quotations of two textbooks, with page numbers: Mark Lowenthal's *Intelligence: From Secrets to Policy* (5th edition, marked "L") and Gregory Treverton's *Reshaping National Intelligence* (2003, marked "T"); one passage is marked "(GM)," the instructor's own. Each row also lists related topics and further readings. The original is in `Sources/Readings/`; Obsidian can't open Excel files, so wiki pages cite this card by theme number (e.g. "Themes summary, theme 10"), and give the textbook page in the text as the spreadsheet does ("L: 8").

## Reliability Notes
**Proposed B-2** (usually reliable; probably true). The definitions are quotations of two standard intelligence textbooks, compiled by the instructor; the textbooks themselves are not in the vault, so the quotations can't be checked against them here. Harrison sets the final rating.
