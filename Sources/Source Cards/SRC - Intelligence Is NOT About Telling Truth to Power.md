## What It Is
A four-page "Perspective" article, "Intelligence is NOT About 'Telling Truth to Power'," published online in the *International Journal of Intelligence and CounterIntelligence* in June 2021. Lowenthal, a former Assistant Director of Central Intelligence for Analysis and Production and the author of the course textbook, argues that the phrase misrepresents intelligence, which deals in uncertainty.
