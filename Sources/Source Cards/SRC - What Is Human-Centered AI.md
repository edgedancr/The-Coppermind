## What It Is
"What is human-centered AI?," a six-minute explainer on the IBM Research blog, dated 31 March 2022. It defines human-centered AI (HCAI) and describes IBM's research under three themes. It is the source of the HCAI definition SAIL quoted as "IBM, 2022" ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]). It is saved as a print of the web page, so citations use its section headings: the untitled "opening," "Human-AI collaboration and co-creation," "Responsible and human-compatible AI," "Natural language interaction," and "The next frontiers for HCAI." The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.
