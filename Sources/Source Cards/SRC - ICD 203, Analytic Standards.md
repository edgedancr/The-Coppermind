## What It Is
Intelligence Community Directive 203, *Analytic Standards*, dated 2 January 2015: the directive that sets the IC Analytic Standards, the nine analytic tradecraft standards, and the role of the ODNI Analytic Ombuds. It supersedes the 2007 version. The course copy is a six-page scan with no text layer; the agent ran text recognition (OCR) on it and checked the section headings and the likelihood table against the page images. Wiki pages cite it by section, linked to the PDF page (e.g. "ICD 203, §D.6.e(2)").
