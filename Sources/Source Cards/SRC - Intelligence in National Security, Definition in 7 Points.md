## What It Is
A one-page Intro to Intelligence handout, "Intelligence in National Security: Definition in 7 Points" (October 2026 version), that sums up the course's definition of intelligence in seven numbered points, crediting Maureen Baginski, Mark Lowenthal, Gordon Middleton, and Greg Treverton and Rich Haver for individual points. The original is in `Sources/Readings/` as a Word file, which Obsidian can't open, so wiki pages cite this card by point number (e.g. "7 Points, pt. 3"). An older November 2025 version in the course folder differs only in point 4, where this version adds the functional managers' disciplines and the all-source producers.
