## What It Is
A RAND Expert Insights paper (document PE-A4012-1, revised 2025-06-27) by Kyle Chan and four co-authors, assessing what Beijing is doing to support its AI industry across the whole technology stack and whether it will work. It is saved as a print of the RAND web page, so the PDF is one long page; citations use its section headings: the untitled "opening," "Beijing's AI Policy Goals and Tools," "U.S. Export Controls to Constrain China's Compute," "Will China's AI Policies Work?," and the appendix "China's Layered State Support for AI" with its subsections (Energy, Chips, Computing Infrastructure, Research and Talent, State-Backed AI Labs, AI-Specific Funding, Local Government Support, Promoting Open Source, Data, Applications). The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.
