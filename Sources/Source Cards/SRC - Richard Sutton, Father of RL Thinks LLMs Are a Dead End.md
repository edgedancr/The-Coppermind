## What It Is
The Substack page for "Richard Sutton – Father of RL thinks LLMs are a dead end," a 66-minute episode of the Dwarkesh Podcast posted on 2025-09-26 (https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/richard-sutton), assigned in SAIL Module 5. It holds Dwarkesh Patel's short introduction and the full edited transcript of his interview with Richard Sutton, recorded at the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute in Edmonton. Sutton argues that large language models are a dead end because they imitate people instead of learning from experience; Patel argues the other side. Wiki pages keep the two voices separate: Sutton's claims are attributed to Sutton, Patel's to Patel.

Obsidian can't open HTML, so wiki pages cite this card with the transcript's chapter heading, e.g. "Sutton interview, 'The Era of Experience'." Patel's text above the transcript is cited as "introduction." The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.
