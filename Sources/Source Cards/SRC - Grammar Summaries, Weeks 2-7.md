## What It Is
A Word document from the Intro to Intelligence course resources, "I2I Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7": a week-by-week list of terms with short definitions, grouped by reading, with discussion questions and answers at the end of most weeks. Most entries paraphrase Mark Lowenthal's *Intelligence: From Secrets to Policy* by chapter, with page numbers. Many give two page numbers, the second in parentheses; the second matches the edition the [[SRC - Themes in US Intelligence, Summary of Texts|Themes summary]] cites (mirror imaging is "163 (129)" here and L: 129–30 there), so wiki pages write them as "L: 163/129". Other entries summarize the 9/11 Commission Report with its page numbers, Kaiser and Pherson's guide to analytic writing, the 2018 HPSCI interview with Mike Rogers, and week 7's readings on cyber, the Internet of Things, and AI. The original is in `Sources/Readings/`; Obsidian can't open Word files, so wiki pages cite this card by week (e.g. "Grammar, wk 3").
