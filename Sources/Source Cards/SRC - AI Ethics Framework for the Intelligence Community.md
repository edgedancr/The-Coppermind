## What It Is
*Artificial Intelligence Ethics Framework for the Intelligence Community*, version 1.0, as of June 2020: a six-page ODNI ethics guide for Intelligence Community personnel on how to procure, design, build, use, protect, consume, and manage AI and related data. Page 1 gives its purpose and ten things AI "should" do; pages 2–6 set out ten groups of questions across the AI lifecycle. It is a companion to the separate *Principles of AI Ethics for the Intelligence Community*, which it cites but which is not in the vault. The PDF has its own text layer. Wiki pages cite it by PDF page ("ODNI framework, p. 3").
