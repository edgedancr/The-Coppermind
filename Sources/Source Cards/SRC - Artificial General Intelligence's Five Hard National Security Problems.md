## What It Is
A RAND Expert Insights paper (document PE-A3691-4) by Jim Mitre and Joel B. Predd, proposing five hard problems that the possible emergence of artificial general intelligence poses for U.S. national security, and recommending how U.S. strategy should prepare. It is saved as a print of the RAND web page, so the PDF is one long page; citations use its section headings: the untitled "opening," "About This Paper," "Endemic Uncertainty," "The Five Problems," and "Toward a Robust Strategy." The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.
