## What It Is
Excerpts from Louis M. Kaiser and Randolph H. Pherson's *Analytic Writing Guide* (Pherson Associates), a spiral-bound guide to writing analytic papers, scanned for Intro to Intelligence as four PDFs in `Sources/Readings/` (the `file` property names the first): ch. 3, "Develop an Analytic Line of March" (pp. 13–19); ch. 4, "Prepare a Detailed Outline" (pp. 20–26); Part III, ch. 5–7, on topic sentences, answering readers' questions, and ordering supporting information (pp. 27–55); and Part IV, ch. 9–11, on reviewing paragraphs, economizing on words, and the final review (pp. 61–75). Every page was scanned upside down and has no text layer; the agent rotated the pages, ran text recognition (OCR), and checked page numbers against the scans. Wiki pages cite the book's printed page numbers, linked to the matching PDF page (e.g. "Kaiser & Pherson, p. 29").

## Reliability Notes
**Proposed B-2** (usually reliable; probably true). It is a published practitioners' guide; Randolph Pherson's other books, such as *Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis* with Richards Heuer, are cited in its footnotes. Its advice is method, not factual claims, and it agrees with the course's lectures and handouts. Harrison sets the final rating.
