## What It Is
The transcript of "The Catastrophic Risks of AI — and a Safer Path," a TED talk by computer scientist Yoshua Bengio (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qe9QSCF-d88), assigned in SAIL Module 5. Bengio traces deep learning's progress, describes studies of AI deception and self-preservation, argues that growing AI agency is the main danger, and proposes a non-agentic "Scientist AI" as a guardrail. It ends with a short Q&A with TED's Chris Anderson (ch. 11). The original is in `Sources/Readings/`, saved as a `.md` file with its text unchanged. Citations use the transcript's chapter headings (ch. 1–11), not its timestamps.
