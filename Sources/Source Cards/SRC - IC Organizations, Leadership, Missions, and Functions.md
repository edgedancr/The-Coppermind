## What It Is
An Intro to Intelligence handout, "Intelligence Organizations—Missions/Functions" (September 2026), that lists the Office of the Director of National Intelligence and the 17 other members of the U.S. Intelligence Community in three groups (program managers, departmental organizations, and military service organizations), with each one's director and core mission. Its introduction quotes Executive Order 12333 on the role of functional managers, cites Mark Lowenthal (*Intelligence: From Secrets to Policy*, 8th edition, p. 168) on the all-source analytic groups, and cites Thomas Fingar on status differences between collectors and analysts. The original is in `Sources/Readings/` as a Word file; wiki pages cite this card as "IC Organizations, intro" or "IC Organizations, table."
