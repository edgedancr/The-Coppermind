## What It Is
An Intro to Intelligence handout, "Clarifying with 'SEE-IT,'" that explains the SEE-IT method for making an idea clear (state, elaborate, exemplify, illustrate), works an example on critical thinking itself, and then lays out Gerald Nosich's elements of critical thinking and a table of standards for critical thinking, each with checking questions. A last section adapts the method to best-case and worst-case scenarios ("SEE-I"). The original is in `Sources/Readings/` as a Word file; wiki pages cite this card by section heading (e.g. "SEE-IT Checklist, 'Standards for Critical Thinking'").
