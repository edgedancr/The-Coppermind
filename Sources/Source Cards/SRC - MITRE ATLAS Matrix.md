## What It Is
A one-page print of the "ATLAS Matrix for AI Systems" from the MITRE ATLAS website: 16 tactics as columns with 111 techniques beneath them, filters for platform and maturity, and a mark for items adapted from MITRE ATT&CK. The page is copyrighted 2021–2026 by the MITRE Corporation and was printed with every platform filter on and the maturity slider at "Feasible." The original is in `Sources/Readings/`.

## Reliability Notes
**Proposed B-2** (usually reliable; probably true). MITRE is the established maintainer of ATT&CK, and the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications maps its risks to this knowledge base (content version v2026.06). But this print shows only names and counts, so its claims can't be checked against the technique descriptions. Harrison sets the final rating.
