> [!summary]
> SAIL distinguished **intelligence**, the capacity to solve problems, from **consciousness**, the capacity to have subjective feelings and experiences (qualia).[^1] In the quotation SAIL used, AI ==acts like a human being; it does not think or feel like a human being==.[^1]

## Key Points
- The quotation, attributed in my notes to Alan Turing: "What really matters is competence in completing a prescribed task, not consciousness of what that task happens to be. The machine may not be conscious in the same way we are, but it is programmed to respond cognitively in the ways that we do."[^1]
- SAIL's "Reality Check for AI": AI is already operational, outpacing humans, and introducing risks from its scale and our dependency on it. Treating AI as if it were human makes you reliant on it, and that dependence could become like a highly addictive drug.[^2]

**[[John Lennox]]'s view**
- He calls "artificial intelligence," "machine intelligence," and "machine learning" anthropomorphisms, and risky ones, because "these machines are not intelligent. They only simulate intelligence."[^3]
- **No full AGI without consciousness:** building a machine that does all that humans can do "runs up against the huge problem of consciousness." Unless we discover what consciousness is and recreate it, he says, "you're never going to get AGI in the fullest sense of the word."[^3] See [[Artificial General Intelligence]].
- **Nobody knows what it is:** he says he has talked to several leading neuroscientists and "nobody knows what consciousness is"; some people don't believe it exists at all, which he calls "a very easy way of escaping it."[^4]
- He quotes "Peter Norvig's big book," which he calls the AI community's Bible: "We are not trying to construct a conscious machine because we don't even know what that would mean." He calls this honest.[^4]
- **What transcends AI:** humans experience qualia, perceive beauty, speak, pray, worship God, and relate to God. "God has done something magnificent. He has coupled intelligence with consciousness."[^4] See [[Imago Dei]].

> [!caution] Sources disagree
> SAIL: intelligence is the capacity to solve problems, and AI acts like a human without thinking or feeling like one.[^1] vs. Lennox: "these machines are not intelligent. They only simulate intelligence."[^3]

> [!question] Whose view is this?
> "AI will never attain consciousness. We know this because it is not made in the image of God. It was created in the image of man, and man cannot breathe life into it."[^2]
