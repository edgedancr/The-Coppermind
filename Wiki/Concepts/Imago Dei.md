> [!summary]
> The *imago Dei* is the teaching that human beings are made in the image of God. SAIL taught it as a foundation for ethics in intelligence work and AI: ==it is what we are protecting, or potentially violating, in our line of work==, so we must understand it when weighing risks to humanity from AI ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/27/26|SAIL notes, 8/27/26]]).

## Key Points
SAIL described God, and humans made in His image, as ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/27/26|SAIL notes, 8/27/26]]):
- **Personal:** each person has a personal center of identity, often associated with the soul or mind. Personhood is the basis of consciousness, personal memory, self-consciousness, self-direction, purpose, and intentionality.
- **Spiritual:** God is a spirit, and humans are created as spirit-beings with an innate inclination to worship Him in spirit and truth.
- **Relational:** humans do not just relate; they need relationships, and find fulfillment only when properly related to God and to other people, the tasks Jesus described as the first and second greatest commandments.
- **Rational and creative:** exercising reason and creativity reflects the nature of God.
- **Volitional and free:** God is free and sovereign and created humans with free will, "one of the most precious but dangerous gifts," because it makes rebellion against God possible.
- **Moral, responsible, and emotional.**
