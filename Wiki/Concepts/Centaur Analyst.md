> [!summary]
> The centaur model describes professionals who work *with* AI rather than being replaced by it. SAIL framed it as the shift from automation (replacing humans) to augmentation (enhancing humans) ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/24/26|SAIL notes, 9/24/26]]).

## Key Points
**Five capabilities of a strong centaur professional** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]])
1. **Domain mastery:** enough substantive expertise to recognize bad assumptions, missing constraints, unsafe recommendations, and fabricated claims.
2. **AI literacy:** a practical understanding of what a model is good at, what it cannot verify, how prompting and context affect output, and where bias or hallucination can enter.
3. **Workflow design:** deciding which parts of a task should remain human-led, which can be AI-assisted, and which should be automated with guardrails.
4. **Verification discipline:** source checking, independent calculation, testing and validation, version control, provenance, and documented assumptions.
5. **Human judgment.**

**The changing role of the analyst** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/24/26|SAIL notes, 9/24/26]])
- The analyst's "big three" tasks: summarization, explanation, and synthesis.

| Old role | New role | Future role ("which is now") |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting | Steering | Orchestrating |
| Summarizing | Integrating | Verifying |
| Synthesizing | Verifying | Evaluating |

## Why It Matters
- ==Verification appears in both the new and the future role==, which is the job this wiki's review process practices ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/24/26|SAIL notes, 9/24/26]]).
- In an Intro to Intelligence guest lecture, Detective Mennen said AI is not taking analysts' jobs, but using it effectively is necessary; because AI will make mistakes or hallucinate, verifying its output is part of the job ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/1/26|Intro notes, 10/1/26]]). Compare CDR Blocker's [[Curiosity, Character, and Competence]] ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/27/26|Intro notes, 8/27/26]]).
