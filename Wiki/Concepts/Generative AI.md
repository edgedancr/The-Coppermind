> [!summary]
> Generative AI is AI that generates new content, such as text, audio, or video. IBM's explainer places it as the newest layer inside [[Deep Learning|deep learning]], built on [[Foundation Model|foundation models]] such as [[Large Language Model|large language models]], and calls it ==the area "that has really caused all of us to really pay attention to AI"== ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 4–5]]). SAIL taught that deep learning shifted AI from discriminative to generative ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/10/26|SAIL notes, 9/10/26]]).

## Key Points
- **Why "generative":** these technologies generate new content ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]).
- **Is it really new?** Some argue generative AI only regurgitates existing information in a new format. IBM's narrator answers with an analogy he admits is imperfect: every musical note already exists, yet no one says new music doesn't ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]).
- **Forms:** besides language models there are audio and video models, which can be used to create [[Deepfake|deepfakes]]; chatbots and deepfakes both come from this space ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]).
- **Reactive by design:** IBM's agentic AI video describes generative AI as waiting for a prompt, generating content from patterns learned in training, and stopping there: "It doesn't take further steps without your input" ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 1]]). Large language models power chatbots, while images and audio typically use diffusion models ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 3]]). See [[Generative vs. Agentic AI]].
- **Uses:** generating new content, or summarizing existing content into something "bite size and manageable" ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 6]]).
- **Example workflow:** Tao Prompts' tutorial makes a music video in a day using only AI tools: ChatGPT for the lyrics, an AI music generator for the song, Midjourney for still images kept consistent with a style reference, an image-to-video generator to animate them and lip-sync the singer, and CapCut to edit ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, intro]]), ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 2]]), ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 3]]), ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 4]]), ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 5]]), ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 6]]).
- **Adoption:** for decades AI seemed "about 5 to 10 years away"; machine learning and deep learning brought an uptick, then foundation models and generative AI sent adoption "straight to the moon" ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 6]]).
- **In SAIL's progression:** statistics → machine learning → deep learning → reinforcement learning → generative AI → agentic AI ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/8/26|SAIL notes, 9/8/26]]). See [[Artificial Intelligence]] and [[Agentic AI]].

## Why It Matters
- IBM notes deepfakes have legitimate uses, such as letting someone who is losing their voice keep speaking in it, but can also be abused ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]). SAIL asked what still counts as evidence when video and audio can be forged ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/15/26|SAIL notes, 10/15/26]]).
