SAIL's rule for where humans belong once AI enters a pipeline: ==AI belongs in the speed, volume, scale, and language work; humans belong in judgment, accountability, and policy== ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]).

## Key Points
**In the intelligence pipeline** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]])
- *AI:* collection, processing, and part of analysis.
- *Humans:* part of analysis, deciding, and acting.

**In the kill chain** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]])
- *AI:* classification, tracking, and collateral estimation (find, fix, track, target), and battle damage assessment algorithms (assess).
- *Human:* engage, because it is a legal, moral, and policy requirement.
- **The LOAC mandate:** the [[Law of Armed Conflict]] requires distinction and proportionality, and neither can be delegated to an algorithm.

## Why It Matters
- The [[Time Compression Trap]] and [[Automation Bias]] both threaten the human decision point ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]).
