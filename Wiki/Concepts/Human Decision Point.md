> [!summary]
> SAIL's rule for where humans belong once AI enters a pipeline: ==AI belongs in the speed, volume, scale, and language work; humans belong in judgment, accountability, and policy==.[^1]

## Key Points
**In the intelligence pipeline**[^1]
- *AI:* collection, processing, and part of analysis.
- *Humans:* part of analysis, deciding, and acting.

**In the kill chain**[^1]
- *AI:* classification, tracking, and collateral estimation (find, fix, track, target), and battle damage assessment algorithms (assess).
- *Human:* engage, because it is a legal, moral, and policy requirement.
- **The LOAC mandate:** the [[Law of Armed Conflict]] requires distinction and proportionality, and neither can be delegated to an algorithm.

**In police intelligence:** in an Intro to Intelligence guest lecture, Detective Mennen said that ==AI and link analysis speed the first cut, but vetting, law, and ethics still belong to people==.[^2] See [[Intelligence-Led Policing]].

## Why It Matters
- The [[Time Compression Trap]] and [[Automation Bias]] both threaten the human decision point.[^1]
