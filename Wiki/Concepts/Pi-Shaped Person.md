> A pi-shaped person knows a little about a lot of things, which lets them connect across many contexts, and also knows a lot about something ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]). SAIL quoted the definition: "A pi-shaped human is a high agency generalist who has a curiosity driven research mindset with a fearless entrepreneurial attitude." ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]])

## Key Points
- Knowledge isn't enough: you need the ability to get up, move toward your goal, and make it real ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]).
- The motto: ==scan wide, dig deep, build out== ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]).
