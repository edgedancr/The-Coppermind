An LLM wiki is a growing, interlinked knowledge base that a person develops and maintains with an AI agent. Instead of asking an AI the same questions over and over, the AI compiles what you learn into linked pages that compound over time ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]]). The Coppermind is one.

## Key Points
**Karpathy's version vs. the SAIL version** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]])

| | Karpathy's version | SAIL version |
|---|---|---|
| Who does the work | The AI does all the writing, modifying, and promoting to the baseline | You design, the AI drafts, you verify |
| Known weakness | Often criticized for compounding hallucinations | Built on the [[Think-Calculate-Verify Loop]] |
| The rule | | ==Nothing becomes part of the wiki until you have reviewed and approved it== |

**Why build it this way** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]])
1. **A productivity tool for life:** useful in later courses, apprenticeships, and personal and professional life.
2. **A showcase:** employers, coworkers, and mentors can see what you know and how you think.
3. **A record of developing perspective:** your work, opinions, and worldview sit beside outside sources and show how your thinking grows.

**The six parts** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]])

| Part | Who writes it | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Sources | You collect | Your own work and outside material, in original form |
| Wiki pages | AI drafts; you verify | Concept, topic, person, and summary pages, linked and cited |
| Schema | You direct | The rulebook the agent follows |
| Index | AI maintains; you check | A map of every page, for people and agents |
| Log | AI maintains; you check | A dated record of every action and decision |
| Perspectives | You write | Reflections, opinions, and conclusions |

- SAIL called the schema the most important thing you build: it turns a general-purpose AI into your research assistant and is the clearest evidence of how you think about organizing knowledge ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]]). This wiki's schema is [[SCHEMA]].
