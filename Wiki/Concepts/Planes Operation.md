> [!summary]
> The "planes operation" was [[Al-Qaeda|al-Qaeda]]'s name for the 9/11 plot.[^1] [[Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]] (KSM), whom the 9/11 Commission calls its principal architect, proposed to [[Usama Bin Ladin|Bin Ladin]] in 1996 an operation to train pilots who would crash planes into buildings in the United States; Bin Ladin approved it in late 1998 or early 1999.[^2]

## Key Points
- **Origins:** KSM and his nephew Ramzi Yousef reasoned that striking the U.S. economy would best influence U.S. policy, and began thinking of aircraft as weapons during their 1995 Manila air ("Bojinka") plot.[^3] KSM's original plan, ten hijacked aircraft with KSM landing the last to make a speech, struck the Commission as "theater".[^1]
- **Targets:** in spring 1999, Bin Ladin, Mohammed Atef, and KSM chose the White House, the Capitol, the Pentagon, and the World Trade Center.[^4]
- **Operatives:** Bin Ladin first picked four veterans, including Khalid al Mihdhar and Nawaf al Hazmi;[^4] an East Asia component using Yemenis was canceled in spring 2000.[^5] The [[Hamburg Cell]] then supplied pilots who were fluent in English and familiar with Western life,[^6] and 13 [[Muscle Hijackers|muscle hijackers]] joined later.[^7]
- **Tradecraft:** al-Qaeda's security committee altered passports and visas, and operatives were taught to forge documents and erase travel stamps.[^8]
- **Final steps:** in coded messages, "architecture" was the World Trade Center and "politics" the White House; all 19 tickets were bought between August 25 and September 5, 2001.[^9]

**Dissent at the top:** Bin Ladin pressed KSM to attack earlier, in May and again in June or July 2001; Mullah Omar and several senior al-Qaeda figures opposed a major attack on the United States that year, but Bin Ladin persisted.[^10]

## Why It Matters
- **A source caution:** much of what is known comes from detainee interrogation reports; the Commission could not question the detainees or their interrogators, and says it evaluated their statements carefully and tried to corroborate them.[^11]
- For what the plot required, see [[Requirements for a Complex Terrorist Operation]]; for how it was paid for, [[Al-Qaeda Financing]].
