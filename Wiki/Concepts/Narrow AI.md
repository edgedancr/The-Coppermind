> [!summary]
> Narrow AI is AI that does a specific task, or set of tasks, really well.[^1] SAIL noted it is already everywhere (spam filters, recommendations, navigation, medical image screening), with drug and protein design, self-driving logistics, and AI agents at work on the near horizon.[^1] [[John Lennox]] describes a narrow AI system as one that "does one thing that normally requires an intelligent human."[^2]

## Key Points
**Negative outlooks**[^1]
- **Jobs:** many claim AI is replacing "thinking" work, not just manual labor.
- **Education:** essays, writing, and cognitive load. What happens when we outsource thinking? See [[Cognitive Debt]].
- **AI as therapist or romantic partner:** ==mistaking language for connection.==
- **Autonomous weapons:** who decides when a machine may take a life? See [[AI in Warfighting]].
- **Surveillance:** see [[AI-Enabled Surveillance]].
