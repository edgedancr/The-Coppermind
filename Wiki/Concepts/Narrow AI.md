> [!summary]
> Narrow AI is AI that does a specific task, or set of tasks, really well ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/15/26|SAIL notes, 10/15/26]]). SAIL noted it is already everywhere (spam filters, recommendations, navigation, medical image screening), with drug and protein design, self-driving logistics, and AI agents at work on the near horizon ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/15/26|SAIL notes, 10/15/26]]).

## Key Points
**Negative outlooks** ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/15/26|SAIL notes, 10/15/26]])
- **Jobs:** many claim AI is replacing "thinking" work, not just manual labor.
- **Education:** essays, writing, and cognitive load. What happens when we outsource thinking? See [[Cognitive Debt]].
- **AI as therapist or romantic partner:** ==mistaking language for connection.==
- **Autonomous weapons:** who decides when a machine may take a life? See [[AI in Warfighting]].
- **Surveillance:** see [[AI-Enabled Surveillance]].
