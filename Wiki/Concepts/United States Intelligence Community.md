> [!summary]
> The U.S. Intelligence Community (IC) is made up of 18 organizations, headed by the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/25/26|Intro notes, 8/25/26]]). Intro to Intelligence grouped them by where they sit in government: independent agencies, Department of Defense (now Department of War) elements, and departmental elements whose main mission is not intelligence ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/25/26|Intro notes, 8/25/26]], [[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/10/26|Intro notes, 9/10/26]]).

## Key Points
**The groups under Executive Order 12333, Section 1.7** ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/10/26|Intro notes, 9/10/26]])
- **Independent:** ODNI and the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA).
- **Department of Defense:** the National Security Agency (NSA), National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), National Reconnaissance Office (NRO), Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA), and the intelligence elements of the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps.
- **Departmental:** FBI and DEA (Justice), INR (State), OIA (Treasury), I&C (Energy), and I&A and the Coast Guard (Homeland Security).

**How the course first described them** ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#8/25/26|Intro notes, 8/25/26]])
- ==Departmental organizations don't have intelligence as their primary goal, but use it as a backbone for everything else==: FBI, DHS, State, Energy, Treasury, and DEA.
- The defense elements are more military-oriented, and include the Space Force.
- The Coast Guard has both wartime and intelligence authorities, so it fits neither category.
- ODNI is the head of intelligence, and the CIA is independent.

**Functional managers:** the CIA for human intelligence and open-source intelligence, the NSA for signals intelligence, and the NGA for geospatial intelligence ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/10/26|Intro notes, 9/10/26]], [[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/15/26|Intro notes, 9/15/26]]). See [[Executive Order 12333]].

**Oversight:** [[Intelligence Oversight Committees]]. **Culture:** [[Seven Tribes of Intelligence]].
