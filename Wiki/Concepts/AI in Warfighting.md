> [!summary]
> SAIL taught that AI compresses the lethal engagement cycle, in three main ways.[^1]

## Key Points
1. **Battlefield awareness and sensor fusion:** AI fuses air, land, sea, space, and cyber feeds into a near-real-time common operating picture. This compresses Boyd's [[OODA Loop]], letting commanders act inside the adversary's decision cycle.[^1]
2. **Targeting and the kill chain:** AI is now active in finding, fixing, tracking, and targeting, but ==the decision to engage, legally and ethically, requires a human==.[^1] See [[Kill Chain]] and [[Human Decision Point]].
3. **Autonomous systems and adversarial threats:** peer competitors are removing humans from the engagement loop, while U.S. systems remain vulnerable to data poisoning, sensor spoofing, and model manipulation.[^1]
