> [!summary]
> Yoshua Bengio is a computer scientist whose research, by his account, was foundational to AI as we know it today; he and his colleagues earned top prizes in the field, and people call him a "godfather of AI" ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 2]]). He appears in the Coppermind for his TED talk warning of AI's catastrophic risks, assigned in SAIL Module 5.

## Key Ideas and Contributions
- **Early deep learning:** 15 or 20 years before the talk, he and his students were developing deep learning, which progressed from barely recognizing handwritten characters to recognizing objects and translating between major languages ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 3]]). See [[Deep Learning]].
- **Stayed in academia:** when tech companies saw the technology's commercial potential in 2012, he stayed at a university to develop AI "for good," working on medical diagnostics and carbon capture ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 3–4]]).
- **Turn to safety:** using ChatGPT in January 2023, he realized AI was arriving faster than he had expected, in "a few years" rather than decades or centuries ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 4]]).
- **Advocacy** ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 4–5]]):
	- A leading signatory of the "Pause" letter, in which he and 30,000 others asked AI labs to wait six months before building their next version. "As you can guess, nobody paused."
	- A signer, with leading AI-lab executives, of the statement: "Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority." See [[Existential Risk]].
	- Testified to the U.S. Senate on these risks. He calls himself the most cited computer scientist in the world.
- **Agency as the main risk:** he is most worried about AI's growing agency, which he sees as the main gap between current AI and human-level cognition ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 6]]). See [[Agentic AI]] and [[AI Deception]].
- **[[Scientist AI]]:** his team's proposed non-agentic AI, meant to act as a guardrail on untrusted agents ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 9]]).
- "I'm not a doomer. I'm a doer." ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 9]])
