> Richards Heuer developed [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]] (ACH) at the CIA in 1999 to counteract confirmation bias.[^1]

## Key Ideas and Contributions
- [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]]: generate all plausible hypotheses and use evidence to disprove them, rather than gathering evidence for a favored conclusion.[^1]
- *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999), which Joseph Gartin calls the benchmark work on [[Structured Analytic Technique|structured analytic techniques]]. Heuer urged analysts to "be self-conscious about their reasoning processes".[^2]
- Rob Johnston cites Heuer's work as evidence of how much research remains on how analysts think.[^3]
