> Richards Heuer developed [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]] (ACH) at the CIA in 1999 to counteract confirmation bias ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]).

## Key Ideas and Contributions
- [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]]: generate all plausible hypotheses and use evidence to disprove them, rather than gathering evidence for a favored conclusion ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]).
