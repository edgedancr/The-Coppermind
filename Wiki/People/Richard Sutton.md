> [!summary]
> Richard Sutton is one of the founding fathers of [[Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]] and inventor of many of its main techniques, including [[Temporal Difference Learning|temporal difference (TD) learning]] and policy gradient methods, for which he received the 2024 Turing Award ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, introduction]]), ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]). He wrote the 2019 essay "The Bitter Lesson" ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]). He appears in the Coppermind for his Dwarkesh Podcast interview arguing that large language models are a dead end, assigned in SAIL Module 5.

## Key Ideas and Contributions
- **Intelligence as goal achievement:** Sutton calls having a goal "the essence of intelligence" and endorses John McCarthy's definition: intelligence is "the computational part of the ability to achieve goals" ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]).
- **RL as "basic AI":** reinforcement learning is about understanding your world, while LLMs are about mimicking people ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]). See [[Critiques of Large Language Models]].
- **The [[Bitter Lesson]]:** methods that rely on human knowledge are eventually superseded by methods that learn from experience and computation ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]).
- **The experiential paradigm:** intelligence is taking the stream of sensation, action, and reward and altering one's actions to increase the rewards ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "The Era of Experience"]]). See [[Continual Learning]].
- **Humans as animals first:** "If we understood a squirrel, I think we'd be almost all the way there to understanding human intelligence"; language is "just a small veneer on the surface" ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Do humans do imitation learning?"]]).
- **[[AI Succession]]:** he considers succession to digital intelligence or augmented humans inevitable ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Succession to AI"]]).
- **A classicist, not a contrarian:** he is content to be out of sync with his field for decades, and measures his views against what thinkers about the mind have long held ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Surprises in the AI field"]]).
