SAIL distinguishes six kinds of essay by purpose, and identifies the ==analytical essay as the most common type in Strategic Intelligence (SI)== ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/8/26|SAIL notes, 9/8/26]]).

## Components

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Argumentative | Defend a position with evidence |
| Descriptive | Create a clear picture of a subject or experience |
| Expository | Explain or inform |
| Narrative | Tell a meaningful story |
| Analytical | Examine how or why something works; the most common type in SI, sometimes framed through a narrative lens |
| Comparative | Identify meaningful similarities and differences |

Source for the table: ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/8/26|SAIL notes, 9/8/26]])

## How to Use It
- **The topic sentence test:** the topic sentences of every paragraph, put together, should form a mini essay that explains the whole argument ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/8/26|SAIL notes, 9/8/26]]).
