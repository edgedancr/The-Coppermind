> [!summary]
> The think-calculate-verify (TCV) loop is SAIL's core method for working with AI. SAIL drew it as the lesson of [[Mata v. Avianca (2023)]]: ==we must always think, calculate, then verify==.[^1] In the Coppermind it takes the form "You design. The AI drafts. You verify."[^2]

## Components
- **Two kinds of thinking:** SAIL mapped *System 1* thinking (fast, instinctive, error-prone) to the AI, and *System 2* thinking (slow, deliberate, rational) to the human.[^3]
	- Large models such as Opus are trying to simulate System 2 thinking.[^3]
- **The rule:** think, calculate, then verify, every time; especially in high-stakes fields, where laziness and negligence in using AI cannot be tolerated.[^1]
