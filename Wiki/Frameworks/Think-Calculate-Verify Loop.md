The think-calculate-verify (TCV) loop is SAIL's core method for working with AI. SAIL drew it as the lesson of [[Mata v. Avianca (2023)]]: ==we must always think, calculate, then verify== ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]). In the Coppermind it takes the form "You design. The AI drafts. You verify." ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|SAIL notes, 10/13/26]])

## Components
- **Two kinds of thinking:** SAIL mapped *System 1* thinking (fast, instinctive, error-prone) to the AI, and *System 2* thinking (slow, deliberate, rational) to the human ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/24/26|SAIL notes, 9/24/26]]).
	- Large models such as Opus are trying to simulate System 2 thinking ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/24/26|SAIL notes, 9/24/26]]).
- **The rule:** think, calculate, then verify, every time; especially in high-stakes fields, where laziness and negligence in using AI cannot be tolerated ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]).
