## Executive Summary
An IBM Technology video explains how artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI relate, warning up front that it simplifies ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 1]]). It defines AI as trying to simulate with a computer something that would match or exceed human intelligence ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 1]]), and nests each field inside the last, like a Venn diagram: [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] learns patterns from data ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 3]]), [[Deep Learning|deep learning]] uses multi-layered [[Neural Network|neural networks]] ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 4]]), and [[Generative AI|generative AI]] builds on [[Foundation Model|foundation models]] such as [[Large Language Model|large language models]] ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]). The narrator argues that generative AI does create new content, comparing it to new music made from existing notes ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]). He concludes that foundation models changed AI's adoption curve ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 6]]).

## Main Points
- AI began as a research project; expert systems spread in the 1980s and 1990s ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 2]]).
- Machine learning is good at prediction and at spotting outliers, which makes it useful in cybersecurity ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 3]]).
- Deep learning's many layers make it hard to work out why it gives the results it does ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 4]]).
- Generative models also make [[Deepfake|deepfakes]], which have legitimate uses but can be abused ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]).

## Themes
- Each generation of AI builds on the last.
- Accessible simplification over technical precision.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: understand where each technology fits so we can reap its benefits ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 6]]).
- [inferred] The terms are nested, not interchangeable.
