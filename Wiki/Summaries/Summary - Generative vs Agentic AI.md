## Executive Summary
IBM Technology's video contrasts two approaches to AI. It describes [[Generative AI|generative AI]], such as chatbots and image generators, as reactive: it waits for a prompt, generates content from patterns learned in training, and stops ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 1]]). It describes [[Agentic AI|agentic AI]] as proactive, pursuing a goal through a cycle of perceiving, deciding, acting, and learning with minimal human intervention ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 2]]). Both typically rest on [[Large Language Model|large language models]], which also supply agents' reasoning ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 3]]). That reasoning is [[Chain-of-Thought Reasoning|chain-of-thought reasoning]], breaking a task into smaller logical steps ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 5]]). Its thesis: the most powerful future systems will be neither purely generative nor purely agentic, but will know when to generate options and when to act ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 5]]), ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 6]]).

## Main Points
- In generative use, a human reviews and refines each step; the AI generates possibilities and the human curates them ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 4]]).
- Agentic AI suits multi-step, ongoing tasks, such as a shopping agent that tracks prices and handles checkout ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 4]]).
- LLMs power chatbots, while diffusion models typically generate images and audio ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 3]]).

## Themes
- Reactive vs. proactive as the dividing line.
- One foundation, the LLM, under both approaches.
- [inferred] The human's role shrinking from directing every step to giving input only when asked.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: future systems will be "intelligent collaborators" ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 6]]).
- No call to action.
