## Executive Summary
ICD 203 establishes the Intelligence Community's analytic standards, states analysts' responsibility to strive for excellence, integrity, and rigor, and sets out the role of the ODNI Analytic Ombuds ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=1|ICD 203, §B.1]]). Dated 2 January 2015, it supersedes the 2007 version ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=1|ICD 203, §B.2]]). It applies across the IC, though not to purely law enforcement information ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=1|ICD 203, §C]]). The standards serve as criteria for evaluating products, a foundation for training, a common professional ethic, and a safeguard for privacy ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=1|ICD 203, §D.1–5]]). Every analytic product must meet five standards, the last of which contains nine tradecraft standards ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=2|ICD 203, §D.6]]). The directive then assigns duties for evaluating products, hearing analysts' concerns, and training ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=5|ICD 203, §E]]).

## Main Points
- The five standards and nine tradecraft standards: [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards]] ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=2|ICD 203, §D.6]]).
- Fixed terms for likelihood, kept apart from confidence: [[Estimative Language]] ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=3|ICD 203, §D.6.e(2)]]).
- Information, [[Analytic Assumption|assumptions]], and judgments kept distinct ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=3|ICD 203, §D.6.e(3)]]).
- The [[Analytic Ombuds]] hears concerns confidentially ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=5|ICD 203, §E.2]]).

## Themes
- Accountability: the standards double as evaluation criteria, with findings reported to Congress ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=5|ICD 203, §E.1]]).
- Separating what is known from what is assumed or judged.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: products "should not avoid difficult judgments in order to minimize the risk of being wrong" ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=4|ICD 203, §D.6.e(8)]]).
- [inferred] The standards work as much as an evaluation checklist as a writing guide.
