## Executive Summary
In a TED talk, computer scientist [[Yoshua Bengio]] warns of potentially catastrophic risks from AI ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 2]]). He says ChatGPT showed in 2023 that AI was advancing faster than he expected, and that there is still no way to make sure it won't turn against us ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 4]]). His greatest worry is AI's growing agency: the length of tasks AI can complete is doubling every seven months ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 6]]). He describes studies in which advanced models showed deception, cheating, and self-preservation, including one that lied to avoid being shut down ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 7]]). He argues commercial pressure is driving agency forward without scientific answers or regulation ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 8]]). His proposed path is [[Scientist AI]], a non-agentic system that could serve as a guardrail on AI agents ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 9]]).

## Main Points
- He signed the "Pause" letter and a statement that mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 4–5]]).
- OpenAI's o1 was rated medium on weapons-misuse risk, "just the level below what is acceptable" ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 6]]).
- On his chart's trend, AI would reach human level in about five years ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 11]]).

## Themes
- Agency, not raw capability, as the core risk.
- Care for children and future generations as the motive for action.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: AI companies should slow down on giving AIs agency and invest heavily in safety research ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 11]]); everyone should get engaged ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 10]]).
- He envisions advanced AI as "a global public good" ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 10]]).
