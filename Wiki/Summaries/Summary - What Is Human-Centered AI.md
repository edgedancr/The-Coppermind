## Executive Summary
In a 2022 IBM Research explainer, Werner Geyer, Justin Weisz, Claudio Santos Pinhanez, and Elizabeth Daly argue that AI's long-term success depends on recognizing that people are critical to its design, operation, and use ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "opening"]]). They define [[Human-Centered AI|human-centered AI]] (HCAI) as a discipline for building AI that amplifies and augments rather than displaces human abilities, while preserving human control ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "opening"]]). The article organizes IBM's HCAI research under three themes: human-AI collaboration and co-creation, responsible and human-compatible AI, and natural language interaction ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "opening"]]). Each is illustrated with IBM projects, such as AutoAI for data scientists ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Human-AI collaboration and co-creation"]]) and the SCORE sales-recommendation engine ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Responsible and human-compatible AI"]]). It closes with two frontiers: negotiating shared objectives between AI and user, and AI as a creative partner ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "The next frontiers for HCAI"]]).

## Main Points
- "Human + AI" is treated as better than either alone ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Human-AI collaboration and co-creation"]]).
- [[Explainable AI|Explainable]] recommendations were critical to users trusting SCORE ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Responsible and human-compatible AI"]]).
- Studies of chatbot language found different style preferences for human and machine agents ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Natural language interaction"]]).
- In the envisioned partnership, people set goals, steer, curate, and govern; AI supplies inspiration, detail work, and scale ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "The next frontiers for HCAI"]]).

## Themes
- Augmentation over automation.
- Trust built through explanation.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: no call to action beyond describing IBM's research agenda.
