## Executive Summary
Tao Prompts' tutorial walks through making a music video in a single day using only AI tools, one of the creator's most requested client projects ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, intro]]). Part one is the song: lyrics drafted with ChatGPT over a couple of iterations, then music made with a generator the captions call "sunno" [transcript unclear], which the video says gives 10 free "sounds" a day [transcript unclear] ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 2]]). Part two is the visuals: still images made in Midjourney and kept consistent with a style reference ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 3]]), then animated with an image-to-video generator the captions call "clean" [transcript unclear] ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 4]]). The creator lip-syncs the singer to the song, builds a transformation effect from a first and last frame ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 5]]), and edits the clips in CapCut with transitions and lyrics ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 6]]). The thesis: simple AI tools can create visuals and effects that would otherwise be really difficult to make ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, intro]]).

## Main Points
- Making reference images first and then animating them "gives the best results" ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 3]]).
- A consistent style across shots matters, so new images borrow the first image's style ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 3]]).
- Short video prompts work: "don't overdo the prompt" ([[Tao Prompts - How to Make a Music Video with AI (transcript)|Tao Prompts, ch. 4]]).
