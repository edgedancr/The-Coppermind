## Executive Summary
This Intro to Intelligence handout defines intelligence in national security in seven points ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pts. 1–7]]). It opens with Maureen Baginski's definition, "information about those who would do us harm," concerning plans, intentions, and capabilities ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 1]]). Following Mark Lowenthal, it says products must be tailored to scope, mission, and user, and outlines the intelligence process and the community's organization ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pts. 2–4]]). Intelligence serves users from policymakers to tribal and international organizations ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 5]]). Point 6 names persistent themes and the moral principles intelligence requires ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 6]]). It closes with Treverton and Haver: intelligence is now in the information business as well as the secrets business ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 7]]).

## Main Points
- Intelligence aims to reduce uncertainty and estimate "proximate reality" ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 1]]).
- Good intelligence is timely, tailored, digestible, and states what is known and unknown ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 2]]).
- Ethics are needed because of human nature's "inclination… to use power for selfish ends" ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 6]]).

## Themes
- Intelligence as product, process, and organization.
- Old problems facing new actors and technologies.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: the Cold War's leftovers must give way to "new thinking, tools, process, and restructure" ([[SRC - Intelligence in National Security, Definition in 7 Points|7 Points, pt. 7]]).
