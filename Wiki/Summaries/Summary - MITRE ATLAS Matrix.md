## Executive Summary
The source is the main matrix page of [[MITRE ATLAS]], the MITRE Corporation's "ATLAS Matrix for AI Systems" ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]). It states no thesis; it is a reference table [inferred]. The matrix arranges 16 tactics as columns, from Reconnaissance to Impact, with 111 techniques beneath them ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]). Users can filter it by platform (predictive AI, generative AI, agentic AI, and enterprise) and by maturity (feasible, demonstrated, realized) ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]). A marker shows which tactics and techniques are adapted from MITRE ATT&CK ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]).

## Main Points
- Only two tactics, AI Model Access and AI Attack Staging, are not marked as adapted from ATT&CK ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]).
- Defense Evasion has the most techniques (15) and Lateral Movement the fewest (3) ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]).
- Many techniques target agents, retrieval, and prompts, such as AI Agent Context Poisoning, RAG Poisoning, and LLM Prompt Injection ([[MITRE ATLAS Matrix.pdf|MITRE ATLAS, matrix]]).

## Themes
- [inferred] Attacks on AI are organized as an extension of conventional cyber operations.
- [inferred] Generative and agentic AI sit alongside predictive AI as targets.
