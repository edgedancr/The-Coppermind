## Executive Summary
Arena's "About Us" page describes the company as building a platform for AI evaluation that advances AI development by rigorously measuring model performance ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Welcome to the Arena"]]). It states its mission as measuring and advancing the frontier of AI for real-world use ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Our Mission"]]). The page says the platform was created by UC Berkeley researchers, is formerly LMArena and was previously known as Chatbot Arena, and became Arena Intelligence Inc. in 2025 ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Our Story"]]). It reports $250 million in venture funding, led by Felicis and UC Investments, with participation from firms including Andreessen Horowitz and Kleiner Perkins ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Backed by world class investors"]]). It links to coverage in The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Reuters, and TechCrunch, two of whose headlines report a $1.7 billion valuation for LMArena ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Featured Coverage"]]).
