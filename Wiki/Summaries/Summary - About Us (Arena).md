## Executive Summary
Arena's "About Us" page describes the company as building a platform for AI evaluation that advances AI development by rigorously measuring model performance.[^1] It states its mission as measuring and advancing the frontier of AI for real-world use.[^2] The page says the platform was created by UC Berkeley researchers, is formerly LMArena and was previously known as Chatbot Arena, and became Arena Intelligence Inc. in 2025.[^3] It reports $250 million in venture funding, led by Felicis and UC Investments, with participation from firms including Andreessen Horowitz and Kleiner Perkins.[^4] It links to coverage in The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Reuters, and TechCrunch, two of whose headlines report a $1.7 billion valuation for LMArena.[^5] The page states no thesis; it presents the company and its credentials [inferred].

## Main Points
- Arena says Chatbot Arena became known across the AI industry as the gold standard of evaluation.[^3]
- Its navigation lists leaderboards for text, web development, images, video, vision, documents, search, and agents.[^6]
- The page does not describe how its rankings are produced.
