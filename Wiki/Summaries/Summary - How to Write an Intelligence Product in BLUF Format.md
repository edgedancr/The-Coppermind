## Executive Summary
The handout teaches the bottom-line-up-front (BLUF) format using a Justice Department drug intelligence assessment as its model ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=1|BLUF handout, p. 1]]). Intelligence products must be tailored to busy policymakers and commanders, and BLUF lets them find and understand what they need quickly ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=1|BLUF handout, pp. 1–2]]). Each paragraph opens with a sentence that covers everything in it, and the details follow from most to least important ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=2|BLUF handout, p. 2]]). It walks through a product's parts, from background and analysis to context and outlook, and stresses accuracy, brevity, and clarity ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=2|BLUF handout, pp. 2–6]]). The title, executive summary, and introduction are written last ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=7|BLUF handout, p. 7]]).

## Main Points
- The BLUF "umbrella" and the parts of a product: [[Bottom Line Up Front]] ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=2|BLUF handout, pp. 2–7]]).
- The title as "the ultimate BLUF": [[Analytic Title]] ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=7|BLUF handout, pp. 7–8]]).
- Footnotes carry background for novice readers without cluttering paragraphs for expert ones ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=3|BLUF handout, p. 3]]).
- Keep records of sources and methods, even though government products rarely include bibliographies ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=9|BLUF handout, p. 9]]).

## Themes
- Writing for the decision maker's time and needs.
- Accuracy, brevity, and clarity as protection against misreading.

## Key Takeaways
- Explicit: there is no place for the analyst's opinion or editorializing ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=3|BLUF handout, p. 3]]).
- Explicit: editing improves the product at every stage ([[BLUF Writing Format - How to Write an Intelligence Product (Henley-Putnam).pdf#page=6|BLUF handout, p. 6]]).
