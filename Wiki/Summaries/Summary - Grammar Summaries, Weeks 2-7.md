## Executive Summary
Intro to Intelligence's "I2I Grammar Summaries" is a week-by-week glossary of terms for weeks 2 through 7 of the course ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 2–7]]). Most entries define terms from Mark Lowenthal's *Intelligence: From Secrets to Policy*, chapter by chapter, with page numbers ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 2–6]]). Others summarize the 9/11 Commission Report, Kaiser and Pherson's guide to analytic writing, an interview with former House Intelligence Committee chairman Mike Rogers, and, in week 7, readings on cyber, the Internet of Things, and artificial intelligence ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 2–7]]). Most weeks end with discussion questions and answers, on topics such as the [[National Intelligence Strategy]], congressional oversight, and [[Just War Theory|just war theory]] ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 2, 5, 7]]). The document states no thesis; [inferred] it works as the course's study vocabulary.

## Main Points
- Lowenthal treats intelligence as "proximate reality" rather than truth ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]); see [[Intelligence (National Security)]].
- The IC's machinery: budget programs, the [[National Intelligence Priorities Framework|priorities framework]], and directives ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
- Analysis and its pitfalls: [[Analytic Confidence|confidence levels]], [[Competitive Analysis|competitive analysis]], [[Groupthink|groupthink]], and [[Politicized Intelligence|politicization]] ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]).
- Collection, counterintelligence, and oversight: the disciplines' strengths and weaknesses, [[Counterintelligence|counterintelligence]], and the congressional committees ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 4–6]]).
- The [[September 11 Attacks (2001)|9/11 attacks]], with their background and the problems in sharing and warning beforehand ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wks 2–5]]).
