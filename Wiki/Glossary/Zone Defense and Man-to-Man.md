> [!summary]
> **Zone defense and man-to-man:** a CIA official told the 9/11 Commission that the CIA tended to play a "zone defense," concentrating on "where," while the FBI played "man-to-man," following a person from beginning to end. The Commission judged that a combination of the two, had the CIA's information been shared, "might have been productive".[^1]
