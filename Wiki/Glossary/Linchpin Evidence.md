> [!summary]
> **Linchpin evidence:** an evidence item whose rating, if changed, would change the relative ranking of the top hypotheses ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 4]]). For each top-two hypothesis, Step 4 asks for the item's current rating, what it would need to change to, how confident we are in it, whether it is single-source or corroborated, and what further collection could strengthen or weaken it ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 4]]). Step 5 then tests each linchpin by changing its rating and re-ranking ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 5]]).
