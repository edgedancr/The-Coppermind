> [!summary]
> **Predator:** an unmanned aircraft first flown over Afghanistan in fall 2000, when 10 of 15 trial missions were rated successful and a "man in white" at Tarnak Farms was judged probably Bin Ladin.[^1] In 2001, debate centered on an armed version: who would pay, who would authorize a strike, and whether the CIA should "pull the trigger".[^2]
