> [!summary]
> **Chain-of-thought reasoning:** a process in which an AI breaks a complex task into smaller logical steps, much as people tackle difficult problems ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 5]]). SAIL taught it as an advanced prompting technique that forces the model to reason before answering, with instructions such as "Think step-by-step" ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/1/26|SAIL notes, 10/1/26]]). IBM's agentic AI video describes agents using it to talk a task through before acting: to organize a conference, first understand the size, duration, and budget, then research venues, then check their availability ([[IBM Technology - Generative vs Agentic AI (transcript)|IBM, generative vs. agentic, ch. 5]]).
