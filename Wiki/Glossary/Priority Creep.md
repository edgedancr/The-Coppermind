> [!summary]
> **Priority creep:** when analysts push up the priority of their own tasks and push down others'.[^1] Lowenthal describes analysts and policymakers alike pushing their issue higher in the [[National Intelligence Priorities Framework]], where it competes with issues already in that priority bracket.[^2]

**Used in:** [[Intelligence (National Security)]], [[Politicized Intelligence]]
