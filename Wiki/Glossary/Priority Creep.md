> [!summary]
> **Priority creep:** when analysts push up the priority of their own tasks and push down others' ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). Lowenthal describes analysts and policymakers alike pushing their issue higher in the [[National Intelligence Priorities Framework]], where it competes with issues already in that priority bracket (Lowenthal, L: 71/61, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
