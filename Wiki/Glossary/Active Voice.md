> [!summary]
> **Active voice:** a construction in which the subject performs the action; Kaiser and Pherson advise making the cause the subject and the effect the verb and object, as in "The state police obtained the warrant and arrested the suspected terrorist".[^1] Passive voice is grammatically correct but discouraged in analytic writing, used only consciously, as when hiding the actor.[^2] The Williams and Molzon slides also say to use the active voice.[^3]

**Used in:** [[Six Qualities of an Analytic Topic Sentence]]; compare [[Intelligence Writing Style]]
