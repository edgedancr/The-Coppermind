> [!summary]
> **Terrorist entrepreneur:** the 9/11 Commission's term for al-Qaeda's enterprising, strong-willed field commanders, who enjoyed considerable autonomy within its structure; it names [[Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]], Hambali, and Abd al Rahim al Nashiri, and calls KSM the clearest example ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=162|9/11 Report, p. 145]]). KSM "presents himself as an entrepreneur seeking venture capital and people" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=171|9/11 Report, p. 154]]).

**Used in:** [[Al-Qaeda]], [[Khalid Sheikh Mohammed]]
