> [!summary]
> **Algorithmic transparency:** the ability to examine an algorithm's inputs and outputs to understand why it gives certain recommendations ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 8]]). Crash Course AI notes that this is easier said than done for deep-learning methods, whose hidden layers can be hard to interpret ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 8]]).

**Used in:** [[Algorithmic Bias]]; compare [[Explainable AI]]
