> [!summary]
> **Algorithmic transparency:** the ability to examine an algorithm's inputs and outputs to understand why it gives certain recommendations.[^1] Crash Course AI notes that this is easier said than done for deep-learning methods, whose hidden layers can be hard to interpret.[^1]
> IBM's explainer makes a similar point about deep learning: with so many layers, it can be hard to work out why a network gives the results it does.[^2] 3Blue1Brown says a large language model's behavior emerges from training, which makes it very hard to determine why it predicts what it does.[^3]
