> [!summary]
> **Secrecy vs. efficacy:** Rob Johnston's finding that ==secrecy and efficacy conflict==: secrecy limits analysts' access to information, while openness degrades sources and methods and alerts adversaries ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=31|Johnston, p. 11]]). He proposes a notional tradeoff curve between analytic effectiveness and counterintelligence, perceives that the Intelligence Community emphasizes secrecy over effectiveness ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=31|Johnston, pp. 11–12]]), and notes that openness is also a necessary condition for improving performance ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=16|Johnston, p. xvi]]).
