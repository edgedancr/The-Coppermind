> [!summary]
> **Secrecy vs. efficacy:** Rob Johnston's finding that ==secrecy and efficacy conflict==: secrecy limits analysts' access to information, while openness degrades sources and methods and alerts adversaries.[^1] He proposes a notional tradeoff curve between analytic effectiveness and counterintelligence, perceives that the Intelligence Community emphasizes secrecy over effectiveness,[^2] and notes that openness is also a necessary condition for improving performance.[^3]

**Used in:** [[Analytic Culture]], [[Rob Johnston]]; compare [[Need to Share]], [[Security Classification]]
