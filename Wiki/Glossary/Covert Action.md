> [!summary]
> **Covert action:** James Diehl taught that covert action requires a presidential finding ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|Intro notes, 10/20/26]]). Executive Order 12333 bars any covert action intended to influence U.S. political processes, public opinion, policies, or media (Sec. 2.13) ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/10/26|Intro notes, 9/10/26]]).
