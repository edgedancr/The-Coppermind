**Attention head:** in a transformer, a single thing a token is compared against (for example, adjectives); each additional comparison is another attention head. A *query vector* is what goes and finds everything that matches the attention head ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/15/26|SAIL notes, 9/15/26]]).
