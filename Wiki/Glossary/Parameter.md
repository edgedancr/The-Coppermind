> [!summary]
> **Parameter:** one of the continuous values, also called weights, that entirely determine how a model behaves; changing them changes its predictions ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 3]]). In a [[Neural Network|neural network]], each connection has a **weight**, and each neuron a **bias** that sets how high the weighted sum must be before it becomes meaningfully active ([[3Blue1Brown - But What Is a Neural Network (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, neural networks, ch. 6]]). 3Blue1Brown's small digit network has about 13,000 ([[3Blue1Brown - But What Is a Neural Network (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, neural networks, ch. 7]]); large language models have hundreds of billions ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 3]]).
