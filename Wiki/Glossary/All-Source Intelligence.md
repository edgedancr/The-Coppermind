> [!summary]
> **All-source intelligence:** intelligence that draws on all types of collection, as appropriate to the topic ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). Lowenthal: combining sources compensates for each discipline's shortcomings and profits from their combined strengths. The 2004 Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act made the DNI responsible for finished intelligence "based upon all sources of available intelligence" (L: 86–87, 115, [[SRC - Themes in US Intelligence, Summary of Texts|Themes summary, theme 2]]). The 9/11 Commission: "The importance of integrated, all-source analysis cannot be overstated" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=425|9/11 Report, p. 408]]); the biggest impediment to it is resistance to sharing information ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=433|9/11 Report, p. 416]]). See [[Need to Share]].
