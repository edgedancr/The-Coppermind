> [!summary]
> **Structured analytic technique:** a term Intro to Intelligence named as one students need to know ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/8/26|Intro notes, 9/8/26]]). SAIL described [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]] as a structured analytic technique ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]). Joseph Gartin describes structured analytic techniques as intended to counteract the biases that cloud analysts' perceptions and warp their predictions, with Richards Heuer's *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999) the benchmark ([[Gartin - The Future of Analysis (2019).pdf#page=3|Gartin, p. 3]]). Stephen Marrin cites researchers who warn that they still rest on subjective inputs ([[SRC - Analytic Objectivity and Science|Marrin, "Preliminary conclusions"]]). Rob Johnston identified at least 160 analytic methods but no standardized doctrine of which works best for which problem ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=18|Johnston, p. xviii]]).
