> [!summary]
> **Structured analytic technique:** a term Intro to Intelligence named as one students need to know.[^1] SAIL described [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]] as a structured analytic technique.[^2] Joseph Gartin describes structured analytic techniques as intended to counteract the biases that cloud analysts' perceptions and warp their predictions, with Richards Heuer's *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999) the benchmark.[^3] Stephen Marrin cites researchers who warn that they still rest on subjective inputs.[^4] [[Rob Johnston]] identified at least 160 analytic methods but no standardized doctrine of which works best for which problem.[^5]
