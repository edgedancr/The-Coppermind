> [!summary]
> **Diagnostic evidence:** evidence whose consistency rating differs across hypotheses, so it helps tell them apart; evidence consistent with all hypotheses is non-diagnostic and supports none of them ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 0]]). Step 3 rates each evidence row's diagnosticity High, Medium or Low and flags Low rows as ones to deprioritize ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 3]]).

**Used in:** [[ACH GenAI Workflow]], [[Analysis of Competing Hypotheses]]
