> [!summary]
> **Analytic confidence:** how strongly an analytic conclusion is supported ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). Lowenthal ties it to the scope and quality of the information behind a judgment: **high** when the information is high quality or the issue allows a solid judgment; **moderate** when the information can be read several ways, there are alternative views, or it is credible but not corroborated; **low** when it is scant, questionable, or fragmented, or rests on problematic sources (Lowenthal, L: 176–177/143, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]).
