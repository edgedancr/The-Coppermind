> [!summary]
> **Undesired bias:** in the [[ODNI AI Ethics Framework]], ==bias "that could undermine analytic validity and reliability, harm individuals, or impact civil liberties"== such as freedom from undue government intrusion on speech, religion, travel, or privacy.[^1] ODNI separates it from the "biases" the [[United States Intelligence Community|Intelligence Community]] builds into AI on purpose, for legal, policy, and mission reasons: screening out irrelevant information, focusing on specific foreign intelligence targets, and minimizing the collection and use of U.S. person information.[^1] It can enter through data collection, feature extraction, curating and labeling data, model selection and development, and even user training.[^1]

**Used in:** [[ODNI AI Ethics Framework]], [[Algorithmic Bias]]; ODNI ties the goal of mitigating it to the objectivity standard in [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards|ICD 203]].[^1]
