> [!summary]
> **Positive feedback loop:** when an algorithm influences the data it later receives, which Crash Course AI says basically means "amplifying what happened in the past," whether or not that amplification is good ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 5]]). Its examples are [[PredPol]] ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 5]]) and the hypothetical hiring system "HireMe!" ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 7]]).
