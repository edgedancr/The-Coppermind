> [!summary]
> **Hughes-Ryan Amendment:** legislation from after the Watergate era requiring that the President approve, and report to Congress, any covert action. The 9/11 Commission notes that CIA case officers read it as a message that covert action "often leads to trouble and can severely damage one's career" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=107|9/11 Report, p. 90]]).
