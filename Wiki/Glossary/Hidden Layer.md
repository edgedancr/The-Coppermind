> [!summary]
> **Hidden layer:** a layer of a [[Neural Network|neural network]] between the input layer and the output layer ([[3Blue1Brown - But What Is a Neural Network (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, neural networks, ch. 4]]). In 3Blue1Brown's digit example there are two, of 16 neurons each, and the hope is that they pick out edges and then patterns like loops and lines ([[3Blue1Brown - But What Is a Neural Network (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, neural networks, ch. 4–5]]).
