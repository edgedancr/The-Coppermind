> [!summary]
> **Hidden layer:** a layer of a [[Neural Network|neural network]] between the input layer and the output layer.[^1] In 3Blue1Brown's digit example there are two, of 16 neurons each, and the hope is that they pick out edges and then patterns like loops and lines.[^2]

**Used in:** [[Neural Network]], [[Algorithmic Transparency]]
