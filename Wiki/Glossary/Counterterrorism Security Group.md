> [!summary]
> **Counterterrorism Security Group (CSG):** the midlevel interagency committee on counterterrorism chaired by Richard Clarke at the National Security Council.[^1] National Security Advisor Rice called it the "nerve center" for the summer 2001 threat, and her deputy saw Clarke and the CSG as the NSC's bridge between foreign and domestic threats.[^2]

**Used in:** [[Summer of Threat (2001)]]
