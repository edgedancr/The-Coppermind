> [!summary]
> **Caliphate:** the institutionalized leadership of the Muslim community (the Ummah) under a caliph, the Prophet Mohammed's successor; the 9/11 Commission calls it a [[Sunni]] institution that continued until 1924, first under Arab and eventually under Ottoman Turkish control ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=67|9/11 Report, p. 50]]). Bin Ladin draws on fundamentalists who blame its destruction on leaders who abandoned "the pure path of religious devotion," and offers his own "Caliphate" as an imagined alternative to present uncertainty ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=67|9/11 Report, pp. 50–51]]).

**Used in:** [[Sunni]], [[Usama Bin Ladin]]
