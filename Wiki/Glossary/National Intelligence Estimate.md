> [!summary]
> **National Intelligence Estimate (NIE):** in the 9/11 Commission's description, an assessment that can "provoke widespread thought and debate" and reach a wider circle of decisionmakers, including Congress.[^1] The Commission found that none on terrorism was produced between 1997 and [[September 11 Attacks (2001)|9/11]]; the July 1995 estimate had predicted attacks in the United States, and its 1997 update mentioned [[Usama Bin Ladin|bin Ladin]] in only three sentences.[^2]
