> [!summary]
> **Actionable intelligence:** intelligence specific enough to act on. Before 9/11, JCS Chairman Shelton made clear his doubts about any military action that risked U.S. lives "unless the intelligence was 'actionable'" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=368|9/11 Report, p. 351]]); one Special Operations commander's view was "give me the action, I will give you the intelligence" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=154|9/11 Report, p. 137]]).
