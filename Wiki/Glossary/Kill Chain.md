> **Kill chain:** the stages of a lethal engagement: find, fix, track, target, engage, assess. SAIL placed AI in finding through targeting and in assessment, and the human at the engage decision ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/20/26|SAIL notes, 10/20/26]]).
