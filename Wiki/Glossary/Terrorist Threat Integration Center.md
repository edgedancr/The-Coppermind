> [!summary]
> **Terrorist Threat Integration Center (TTIC):** created in 2003 at CIA headquarters, staffed from many agencies and reporting to the Director of Central Intelligence, with primary responsibility for terrorism analysis but "formally proscribed from having any oversight or operational authority".[^1] The 9/11 Commission recommended building the [[National Counterterrorism Center]] on it, with the TTIC as its distinct intelligence function.[^2]
