> [!summary]
> **Economic intelligence:** the Intelligence Community supports the economic domain by conducting foreign economic espionage, monitoring threats to the supply chain, forecasting major economic shifts, and monitoring competition for materials, oil, and natural gas (Lowenthal, L: 378–382, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]). **Financial intelligence (FININT)** is the part of it that follows money flows, especially from illicit or illegal activity; because money now moves mostly digitally, FININT often shades into cyber intelligence (Lowenthal, L: 382, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]).

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> The summary says the IC supports the economic domain by "conducting foreign economic espionage." Check whether Lowenthal means collecting foreign economic intelligence or countering other states' economic espionage.
