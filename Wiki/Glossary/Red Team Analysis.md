> [!summary]
> **Red team analysis:** analysis performed "from the enemy's perspective".[^1] The [[SRC - The 9-11 Commission Report|9/11 Commission]] found that the CIA's [[Counterterrorist Center (CIA)|Counterterrorist Center]] did not do it for the use of aircraft as weapons, and believes it would have spotlighted a critical constraint for the terrorists: finding a suicide operative able to fly large jet aircraft.[^1] Bobby W. argues that efforts to institutionalize alternative analysis, including separate "red cell" product lines, have failed, because separating it from mainline analysis signals that it need not be taken seriously.[^2]

**Used in:** [[Institutionalizing Imagination]], [[Limits of Prediction]]; compare [[Competitive Analysis]]
