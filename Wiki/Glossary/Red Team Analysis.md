> [!summary]
> **Red team analysis:** analysis performed "from the enemy's perspective" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=364|9/11 Report, p. 347]]). The 9/11 Commission found that the CIA's Counterterrorist Center did not do it for the use of aircraft as weapons, and believes it would have spotlighted a critical constraint for the terrorists: finding a suicide operative able to fly large jet aircraft ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=364|9/11 Report, p. 347]]).
