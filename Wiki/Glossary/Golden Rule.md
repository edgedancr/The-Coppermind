> [!summary]
> **Golden Rule:** "Do unto others as you would they do to you," a moral conviction that [[John Lennox]] says appears across "all the philosophies, religions, or even no religion"; he says [[C. S. Lewis]] found it in "30 or 40 different areas."[^1] Natural law would say it is simply there; Christians would say "it's there because God has wired us that way as … moral beings."[^1] Lennox applies it to AI: "So we're building a machine. What could it possibly do to others? And what could it do to you?"[^1]
