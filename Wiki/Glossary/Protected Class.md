> [!summary]
> **Protected class:** a category of people, such as gender, race, or age, that laws protect from discrimination in areas like employment and housing ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, intro]]). Crash Course AI warns that leaving protected classes out of training data doesn't stop an algorithm from discriminating, because they can re-enter as [[Correlated Feature|correlated features]] ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 2]]).
