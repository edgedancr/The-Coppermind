> [!summary]
> **Prediction market:** one of the "structured forecasting tools," like the IC Prediction Market, that Gartin says Philip Tetlock's research showed were needed ([[Gartin - The Future of Analysis (2019).pdf#page=3|Gartin, p. 3]]). In Gartin's 2030 scenario, a prediction market rates a prime minister's chance of dissolving parliament at 63 percent, and the analyst's assistant sets it beside a base rate of 67 percent ([[Gartin - The Future of Analysis (2019).pdf#page=4|Gartin, p. 4]]).
