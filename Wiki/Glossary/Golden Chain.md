> [!summary]
> **Golden Chain:** the financial support network for the anti-Soviet jihad in Afghanistan, put together mainly by financiers in Saudi Arabia and the Persian Gulf states, with donations flowing through charities and other nongovernmental organizations ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=72|9/11 Report, p. 55]]). The 9/11 Commission notes that Bin Ladin later rebuilt a strong financial position in Afghanistan through financiers associated with it ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=83|9/11 Report, p. 66]]).
