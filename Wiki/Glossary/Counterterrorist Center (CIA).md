> [!summary]
> **Counterterrorist Center (CTC):** the CIA's counterterrorism "fusion" center, which recruits liaison officers from across the [[United States Intelligence Community|Intelligence Community]].[^1] Before [[September 11 Attacks (2001)|9/11]] most of the work of its 30- to 40-person analytic group dealt with collection issues; a strategic assessments branch was set up in July 2001, and its new chief reported for duty on September 10, 2001.[^2] Created in the mid-1980s, it reported formally to the DCI but in practice looked to the Directorate of Operations; its analysts focused on support to operations, and a 1994 CIA inspector general's report criticized its capacity to warn.[^3]
