> [!summary]
> **Analytic mission statement:** in Kaiser and Pherson's guide, a precise statement that captures a paper's analytic bottom line; the more precise it is, the more direct the path to the key judgments, and the vaguer it is, the easier it is to go off on a tangent ([[Kaiser and Pherson - Analytic Writing Guide, ch. 4 (scan).pdf#page=3|Kaiser & Pherson, p. 21]]). They introduce it in their chapter 1, which is not among the course's scanned excerpts ([[Kaiser and Pherson - Analytic Writing Guide, ch. 4 (scan).pdf#page=3|Kaiser & Pherson, p. 21]]).
