> [!summary]
> **Analytic mission statement:** in Kaiser and Pherson's guide, a precise statement that captures a paper's analytic bottom line; the more precise it is, the more direct the path to the key judgments, and the vaguer it is, the easier it is to go off on a tangent.[^1] They introduce it in their chapter 1, which is not among the course's scanned excerpts.[^1]
