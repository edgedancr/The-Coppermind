> [!summary]
> **Goldwater-Nichols Act (1986):** the law that created joint commands for operations in the field: the military services organize, train, and equip forces and assign them to joint combatant commanders, and officers must serve outside their own service to be promoted, moving the Defense Department's mindset "from service-specific to 'joint'".[^1] The 9/11 Commission used it as a model for joint work in intelligence.[^2] The Commission also notes it may have lessened the diversity of military advice and options presented to the president.[^3]
