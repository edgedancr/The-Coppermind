> [!summary]
> **Stovepipes:** the organization of intelligence agencies according to their main information sources, with limited sharing across these lanes (Lowenthal, L: 65, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 5]]). The 9/11 Commission wrote that it is hard to "break down stovepipes" when so many agencies are "legally and politically entitled to have cast-iron pipes of their own" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=420|9/11 Report, p. 403]]). Gregory Treverton, introducing Rob Johnston's study, writes that analysts should move much more often across the agency "stovepipes" they inhabit ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=12|Treverton, foreword, p. xii]]).
