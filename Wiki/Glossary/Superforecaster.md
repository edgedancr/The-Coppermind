> [!summary]
> **Superforecaster:** in the research of Barbara Mellers and Philip Tetlock, one of the best forecasters, who combine high cognitive ability, political knowledge, open-mindedness, inductive reasoning and pattern detection, fluid intelligence, nondogmatic beliefs, a balance of competitiveness and collaboration, and a commitment to self-improvement.[^1] Tetlock found superforecasters are partly made, improving with training, and Bobby W. urges the IC to hire for these traits.[^1]
