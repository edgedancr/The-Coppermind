> [!summary]
> **Correlated feature:** a feature that isn't explicitly in the data but may be unintentionally correlated with a specific prediction, which lets a [[Protected Class|protected class]] re-enter an algorithm that never collected it ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 2]]). Crash Course AI's examples: because many U.S. places are still highly segregated, zip code can be strongly correlated with race, and a record of purchases with gender ([[Crash Course AI 18 - Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (transcript)|Crash Course AI, ch. 2]]).
