> [!summary]
> **The Singularity:** the point at which AGI is developing faster than humans can track it or stop it.[^1] [[John Lennox]] says the term "has various definitions, one of which is that eventually we human beings will merge with machines," and names futurist Ray Kurzweil as one who talks about it.[^2] He adds that Martin Rees, a former Astronomer Royal, "quite openly says" that in the far future "it will be the intelligence of machines that dominate."[^2]

**Used in:** [[Artificial General Intelligence]]; compare [[Recursive Self-Improvement]] and [[Transhumanism]]
