> [!summary]
> **Hamburg cell:** Mohamed Atta, Ramzi Binalshibh, Marwan al Shehhi, and Ziad Jarrah, students in Hamburg, Germany, who arrived in Kandahar in late 1999 hoping to fight in Chechnya. Al-Qaeda recruited them for the [[Planes Operation|planes operation]] because they added "fluency in English and familiarity with life in the West".[^1]
