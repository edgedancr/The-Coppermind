> [!summary]
> **Article 3 of the Geneva Conventions:** in the 9/11 Commission's words, an article "specifically designed for those cases in which the usual laws of war did not apply," whose minimum standards are "generally accepted throughout the world as customary international law" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=397|9/11 Report, p. 380]]). The Commission proposed it as a basis for a common coalition approach to the detention and humane treatment of captured terrorists ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=397|9/11 Report, p. 380]]).
