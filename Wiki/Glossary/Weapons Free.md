> [!summary]
> **Weapons free:** a permissive rule of engagement under which "the decision to shoot rests in the cockpit," or in the cockpit of the lead pilot. On [[September 11 Attacks (2001)|9/11]], fighters launched from Andrews Air Force Base flew weapons free, while the [[North American Aerospace Defense Command|NORAD]] fighters over Washington had no engagement order.[^1]

**Used in:** [[Air Defense on September 11 (2001)]]
