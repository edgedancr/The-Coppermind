> [!summary]
> **Weapons free:** a permissive rule of engagement under which "the decision to shoot rests in the cockpit," or in the cockpit of the lead pilot. On 9/11, fighters launched from Andrews Air Force Base flew weapons free, while the NORAD fighters over Washington had no engagement order ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=61|9/11 Report, p. 44]]).

**Used in:** [[Air Defense on September 11 (2001)]]
