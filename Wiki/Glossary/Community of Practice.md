> [!summary]
> **Community of practice:** a network of professionals who exchange methods, post and answer case studies, form ad hoc teams of experts, and mentor novices; Rob Johnston recommends linking such networks to a central lessons-learned repository ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=128|Johnston, p. 112]]). Gregory Treverton's foreword lists communities of practice, with mentoring and online forums on methods, among the book's key proposals ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=12|Treverton, foreword, p. xii]]).
