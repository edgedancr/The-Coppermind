> [!summary]
> **Mirror imaging:** assuming your enemy will think and act the same way you would ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). Lowenthal calls it one of the most frequent flaws of analysis, leaving no room for different norms or motives. Its opposite is "clientism," or going native (L: 8, 129–30). Treverton ties many intelligence failures to it (T: 208–211) ([[SRC - Themes in US Intelligence, Summary of Texts|Themes summary, theme 10]]).
