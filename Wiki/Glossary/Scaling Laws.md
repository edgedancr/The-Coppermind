> [!summary]
> **Scaling laws:** the empirical finding that model performance scales with compute, which leading AI labs rely on as they invest ever-larger sums in training their models ([[RAND - Artificial General Intelligence's Five Hard National Security Problems (2025).pdf|Mitre & Predd, "Endemic Uncertainty"]]). Mitre and Predd note that it is unclear whether performance will keep scaling, and, if it does, where the threshold for AGI would be ([[RAND - Artificial General Intelligence's Five Hard National Security Problems (2025).pdf|Mitre & Predd, "Endemic Uncertainty"]]).

**Used in:** [[Five Hard National Security Problems of Artificial General Intelligence]]; compare the scaling section of [[Machine Learning]] and the [[Bitter Lesson]]
