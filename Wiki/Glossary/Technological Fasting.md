> [!summary]
> **Technological fasting** (or "electronic fasting"): [[John Lennox]]'s name for teaching people to set aside phones, tablets, and other devices for a time, "a concept that we need to push."[^1] His example is parents who put their children's smartphones and tablets in a locker for a week and took them to the country; the children came back "talking to each other instead of just texting each other."[^1] He offers it in response to Jonathan Haidt's account of anxious young people who spend "sometimes five or six hours a day watching screens."[^1]
