> [!summary]
> **Reagan Doctrine:** the policy of giving military assistance to guerrilla fighters opposing the Soviet Union and Soviet-allied countries, to throw the Soviet Union off balance; the course's summary gives leftist governments in South America as an example.[^1]

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> The cases usually cited are Nicaragua (Central America), Afghanistan, and Angola. Check whether "South America" is the summary's slip.
