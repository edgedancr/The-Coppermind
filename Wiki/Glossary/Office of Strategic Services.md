> [!summary]
> **Office of Strategic Services (OSS):** the World War II agency, led by William J. "Wild Bill" Donovan, from which the CIA descends. Its Research and Analysis Branch, where scholars turned spies' reports, intercepts, broadcasts, and publications into reports on foreign conditions, remains part of U.S. intelligence today ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=105|9/11 Report, pp. 88–89]]). President Truman dissolved it after the war ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=106|9/11 Report, p. 89]]).
