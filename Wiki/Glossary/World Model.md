> [!summary]
> **World model:** in [[Richard Sutton]]'s terms, a model that lets an agent predict what will happen, including the consequences of its own actions; he calls it the "transition model of the world," one of an agent's four parts, learned from everything the agent senses ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]), ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "The Era of Experience"]]). Sabine Hossenfelder names world models as one step toward the abstract reasoning she thinks current AI lacks ([[Sabine Hossenfelder - Current AI Models Have 3 Unfixable Problems (transcript)|Hossenfelder, ch. 5]]).

> [!caution] Sources disagree
> Patel: LLMs "seem to have very robust world models," the best AI has made ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]). vs. Sutton: they predict what a person would say, not what will happen, so they mimic things that have world models rather than having one ([[SRC - Richard Sutton, Father of RL Thinks LLMs Are a Dead End|Sutton interview, "Are LLMs a dead end?"]]).
