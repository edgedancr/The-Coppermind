> [!summary]
> **Intelligence Community Directive (ICD):** a directive issued by the Director of National Intelligence that sets policy for the Intelligence Community.[^1] ICD 203, for example, sets the IC's analytic standards; see [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards]].[^2]

**Used in:** [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards]]; compare [[United States Intelligence Community]]
