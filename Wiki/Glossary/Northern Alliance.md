> [!summary]
> **Northern Alliance:** the anti-Taliban forces in northeastern Afghanistan led by Ahmed Shah Massoud, perhaps the CIA's best capture option in 1999, though by December the CIA rated the chance he would act against Bin Ladin at under 15 percent ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=159|9/11 Report, pp. 142–143]]). Massoud was assassinated by al-Qaeda operatives disguised as journalists on September 9, 2001 ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=269|9/11 Report, p. 252]]).
