> [!summary]
> **Explainable AI (XAI):** explaining how AI models operate and why they make a given recommendation, so people understand and can trust them; IBM Research treats it as part of responsible, human-compatible AI and is developing XAI guidelines for practitioners.[^1] In IBM's SCORE sales tool, explainable recommendations that gave clear evidence for why a business partner was recommended were critical to users trusting the system.[^1] The [[ODNI AI Ethics Framework]] asks IC personnel to use explainable methods, to the extent practicable, so users, overseers, and the public understand how and why an AI produced its outputs, because ==AI can reach the expected outcome while using inappropriate criteria==; it ties this to [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards|ICD 203]] and to answering a consumer who asks "How do you know this?"[^2]

**Used in:** [[Human-Centered AI]], [[ODNI AI Ethics Framework]]; compare [[Algorithmic Transparency]]
