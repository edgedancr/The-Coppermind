> [!summary]
> **Explainable AI (XAI):** explaining how AI models operate and why they make a given recommendation, so people understand and can trust them; IBM Research treats it as part of responsible, human-compatible AI and is developing XAI guidelines for practitioners ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Responsible and human-compatible AI"]]). In IBM's SCORE sales tool, explainable recommendations that gave clear evidence for why a business partner was recommended were critical to users trusting the system ([[IBM Research - What Is Human-Centered AI (2022).pdf|IBM Research, "Responsible and human-compatible AI"]]).

**Used in:** [[Human-Centered AI]]; compare [[Algorithmic Transparency]]
