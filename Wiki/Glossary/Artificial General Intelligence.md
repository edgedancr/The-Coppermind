> [!summary]
> **Artificial general intelligence (AGI):** an AI that can do every human task better than every human can do it ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]). RAND's Mitre and Predd describe AGI as producing human-level, or even superhuman-level, intelligence across a wide variety of cognitive tasks ([[RAND - Artificial General Intelligence's Five Hard National Security Problems (2025).pdf|Mitre & Predd, "opening"]]).

> [!caution] Sources disagree
> SAIL: an AI that does every human task better than every human (SAIL notes, 9/1/26) vs. Mitre and Predd: human-level or even superhuman intelligence across a wide variety of cognitive tasks (RAND, opening).
