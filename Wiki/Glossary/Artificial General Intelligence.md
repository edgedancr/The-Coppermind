> [!summary]
> **Artificial general intelligence (AGI):** an AI that can do every human task better than every human can do it.[^1] RAND's Mitre and Predd describe AGI as producing human-level, or even superhuman-level, intelligence across a wide variety of cognitive tasks.[^2] Sabine Hossenfelder defines it as "intelligence comparable to that of humans or above".[^3] [[John Lennox]] notes that "no one has agreed on what they mean by AGI"; as he understands it, AGI means a machine that "can do everything that can be done by a human being, do it much faster, do it much better, and even transcend humanity," so that it is "essentially super intelligent."[^4]
