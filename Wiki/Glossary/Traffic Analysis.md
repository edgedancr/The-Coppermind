> [!summary]
> **Traffic analysis:** analyzing patterns in communications to identify the sender, without analyzing the content of the messages.[^1] Treverton expects cheap, strong encryption to limit SIGINT more and more to traffic analysis, "useful intelligence to be sure, but not as useful as knowing the content of the messages".[^2] The 9/11 Commission describes NSA analysts' traffic analysis as studying technical communications systems and codes, as well as foreign organizational structures, including terrorist organizations'.[^3]
