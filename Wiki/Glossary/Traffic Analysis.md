> [!summary]
> **Traffic analysis:** analyzing patterns in communications to identify the sender, without analyzing the content of the messages ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). Treverton expects cheap, strong encryption to limit SIGINT more and more to traffic analysis, "useful intelligence to be sure, but not as useful as knowing the content of the messages" (T: 88–89, [[SRC - Themes in US Intelligence, Summary of Texts|Themes summary, theme 13]]).
