> [!summary]
> **Standing reserve:** in Heidegger's account of modern technology, as the Then & Now video explains it, things revealed as resources stored and ready for use: coal stockpiled, an airliner waiting on the runway.[^1] The video says data, information, and connections on the internet are standing reserve in the same way.[^1]
