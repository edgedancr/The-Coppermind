> [!summary]
> **Deepfake:** content made with generative audio or video models that, for example, recreates a person's voice so it seems they said things they never said ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]). IBM notes legitimate uses (entertainment, parody, or a voice for someone losing theirs) as well as abuse ([[IBM Technology - AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI Explained (transcript)|IBM Technology video, ch. 5]]). SAIL asked: when video and audio can be forged, what still counts as evidence? ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/15/26|SAIL notes, 10/15/26]])
