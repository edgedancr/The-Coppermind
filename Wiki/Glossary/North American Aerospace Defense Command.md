> [!summary]
> **North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD):** the command responsible for defending U.S. airspace, working with the FAA in the event of a hijacking ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=31|9/11 Report, p. 14]]). The 9/11 Commission found it had construed that mission as facing threats from outside the country, so its scenarios considered hijacked aircraft only if they came from overseas ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=369|9/11 Report, pp. 352, 427]]).
