> [!summary]
> **Pre-training:** the first stage of training a [[Large Language Model|large language model]], in which it learns to autocomplete passages from an enormous amount of text, typically pulled from the internet ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 3]]), ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 5]]). Because autocompleting internet text is a different goal from being a good AI assistant, chatbots get further training afterward ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 6]]).

**Used in:** [[Large Language Model]], [[Fine-Tuning]]
