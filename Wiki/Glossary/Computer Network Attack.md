> [!summary]
> **Computer network attack (CNA):** recorded in my notes as attacking networks "to gather data".[^1] Lowenthal defines it as disrupting or bringing down the relevant network, in contrast to [[Computer Network Exploitation]].[^2]

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> CNA usually means disrupting, degrading, or destroying networks or the information on them; gathering data is [[Computer Network Exploitation]]. Check the slide.
> Possibly resolved by [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7]], week 3.[^3]
