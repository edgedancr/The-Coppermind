> [!summary]
> **Computer network attack (CNA):** defined by Lowenthal as disrupting or bringing down a network as part of an attack, in contrast to [[Computer Network Exploitation]].[^1]

**Used in:** [[Intelligence (National Security)]], [[Attribution (Cyber)]]

## Footnotes
[^1]: Lowenthal, L: 351, 353/275, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]
[^2]: Lowenthal ch. 12, "CNE vs. CNA"
