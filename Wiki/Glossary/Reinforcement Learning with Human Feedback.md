> [!summary]
> **Reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF):** training after [[Pre-Training|pre-training]] in which workers flag unhelpful or problematic predictions, and their corrections further change the model's parameters, making it more likely to give predictions users prefer ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 6]]). 3Blue1Brown calls it just as important as pre-training ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 6]]).
