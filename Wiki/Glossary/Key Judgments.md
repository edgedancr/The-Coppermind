> [!summary]
> **Key judgments:** the 30-second summary of a report's takeaways, the "so what," which can also be called the executive summary.[^1] The Grammar Summaries define them as the central conclusions in a [[National Intelligence Estimate|national intelligence estimate]].[^2] Kaiser and Pherson, as the Grammar Summaries give them, call key judgments a paper's core conclusions, one step deeper than its title but less detailed than its full analysis;[^3] in their message-driven paper, the key judgments come first.[^4]
