> [!summary]
> **Key judgments:** the 30-second summary of a report's takeaways, the "so what," which can also be called the executive summary ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). The Grammar Summaries define them as the central conclusions in a [[National Intelligence Estimate|national intelligence estimate]] ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
