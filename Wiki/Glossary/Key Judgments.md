> [!summary]
> **Key judgments:** the 30-second summary of a report's takeaways, the "so what," which can also be called the executive summary ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#10/13/26|Intro notes, 10/13/26]]). The Grammar Summaries define them as the central conclusions in a [[National Intelligence Estimate|national intelligence estimate]] ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]). Kaiser and Pherson, as the Grammar Summaries give them, call key judgments a paper's core conclusions, one step deeper than its title but less detailed than its full analysis ([[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]); in their message-driven paper, the key judgments come first ([[Kaiser and Pherson - Analytic Writing Guide, ch. 4 (scan).pdf#page=5|Kaiser & Pherson, p. 23]]).
