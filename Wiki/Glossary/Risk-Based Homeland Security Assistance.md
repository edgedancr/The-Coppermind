> [!summary]
> **Risk-based homeland security assistance:** the 9/11 Commission's recommendation that federal homeland security aid "be based strictly on an assessment of risks and vulnerabilities," supplementing state and local resources rather than remaining "a program for general revenue sharing" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=413|9/11 Report, p. 396]]). In 2004 it put Washington, D.C., and New York City at the top of any such list ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=413|9/11 Report, p. 396]]).
