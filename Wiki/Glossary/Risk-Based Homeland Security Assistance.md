> [!summary]
> **Risk-based homeland security assistance:** the 9/11 Commission's recommendation that federal homeland security aid "be based strictly on an assessment of risks and vulnerabilities," supplementing state and local resources rather than remaining "a program for general revenue sharing".[^1] In 2004 it put Washington, D.C., and New York City at the top of any such list.[^1]
