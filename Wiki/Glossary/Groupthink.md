> [!summary]
> **Groupthink:** valuing conformity of opinion over objective analysis (Lowenthal ch. 2, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]); a lack of diverse opinions that comes from failing to challenge and analyze presuppositions, or ==a premature and inappropriate degree of unanimity== (Lowenthal, L: 168/135, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]).
