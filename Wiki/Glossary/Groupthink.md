> [!summary]
> **Groupthink:** valuing conformity of opinion over objective analysis (Lowenthal ch. 2, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]); a lack of diverse opinions that comes from failing to challenge and analyze presuppositions, or ==a premature and inappropriate degree of unanimity== (Lowenthal, L: 168/135, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]). Konya and Johnston list a greater likelihood of groupthink among the consequences of the Intelligence Community's intentionally closed hiring system ([[Johnston - Analytic Culture in the US Intelligence Community (2005).pdf#page=119|Konya & Johnston, p. 103]]).

**Used in:** [[Anticipatory Socialization]]; compare [[Competitive Analysis]], [[Confirmation Bias]]
