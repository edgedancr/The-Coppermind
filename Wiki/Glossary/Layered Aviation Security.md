> [!summary]
> **Layered aviation security:** the FAA's system of rules meant to give "layered" defense, so that the failure of any one layer would not be fatal. The 9/11 Commission found every layer relevant to hijacking (intelligence, passenger prescreening, checkpoint screening, and onboard security) seriously flawed before 9/11; for example, the FAA's no-fly list held just 12 names, while government watchlists held many thousands.[^1]

**Used in:** [[Summer of Threat (2001)]], [[Terrorist Travel]]; compare [[TIPOFF Watchlist]]
