> [!summary]
> **President's Daily Brief (PDB):** the CIA's daily briefing for the President: six to eight short articles chosen by CIA staff, distributed to only a handful of senior officials. The **Senior Executive Intelligence Brief (SEIB)** goes to a broader group and usually contains less information, to protect sources and methods ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=271|9/11 Report, pp. 254–255]]).

**Used in:** [[Summer of Threat (2001)]]
