> [!summary]
> **Analytic Ombuds:** under ICD 203, the chief of ODNI's Analytic Integrity and Standards Group, who handles concerns about "lack of objectivity, bias, politicization, or other issues" in how analytic products apply the standards ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=2|ICD 203, §D.4]]). Analysts outside ODNI first use their own element's procedures, and the Ombuds works confidentially, though confidentiality does not cover significant misconduct or violations of law or ethics ([[ODNI - ICD 203, Analytic Standards (2015).pdf#page=5|ICD 203, §E.2]]).

**Used in:** [[ICD 203 Analytic Standards]]; compare [[Politicized Intelligence]]
