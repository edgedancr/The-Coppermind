> [!summary]
> **Existential risk (x-risk):** in SAIL's framing, the scenario in which AGI decides to kill all humans because they aren't necessary.[^1] SAIL contrasted science fiction with reality: the danger isn't that the AI "wants" something, but that powerful systems pursue goals without sufficient human control.[^1]
