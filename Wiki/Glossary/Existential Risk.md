**Existential risk (x-risk):** in SAIL's framing, the scenario in which AGI decides to kill all humans because they aren't necessary ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]). SAIL contrasted science fiction with reality: the danger isn't that the AI "wants" something, but that powerful systems pursue goals without sufficient human control ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|SAIL notes, 9/1/26]]).
