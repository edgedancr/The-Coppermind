> [!summary]
> **World Islamic Front fatwa:** a February 1998 statement by Bin Ladin, Ayman al Zawahiri, and three others, published in a London Arabic newspaper, calling the murder of any American, anywhere, an "individual duty for every Muslim"; none of its signers were scholars of Islamic law.[^1] Intro to Intelligence's grammar summaries note that it made no distinction between military and civilian targets.[^2]

**Used in:** [[Al-Qaeda]], [[East Africa Embassy Bombings (1998)]], [[Usama Bin Ladin]], [[Struggle of Ideas]]
