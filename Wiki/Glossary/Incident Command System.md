> [!summary]
> **Incident Command System (ICS):** "a formalized management structure for emergency response" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=331|9/11 Report, p. 314]]). The 9/11 Commission credited it with helping the multi-jurisdiction response at the Pentagon succeed ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=331|9/11 Report, p. 314]]), found it did not integrate the agencies responding at the World Trade Center ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=338|9/11 Report, p. 321]]), and recommended that emergency response agencies nationwide adopt it ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=414|9/11 Report, p. 397]]).

**Used in:** [[Emergency Response on September 11 (2001)]], [[Global Strategy Against Islamist Terrorism]], [[Unified Command]]
