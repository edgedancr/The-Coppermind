> [!summary]
> **Four causes:** Aristotle's account, as the Then & Now video explains it, of what makes a physical object: using a silver chalice, its material (the silver), its form (its shape), its end (to drink from), and its maker (the silversmith).[^1] The video applies them to social media: hardware and user content; code and algorithms; the desire for connection; and the platform's owner, along with its users.[^1]

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> The transcript's auto-captions garble the Latin names of the causes ("her cause her material," "calls irfan alice," "causer officiants"). Check the terms against the video before using them.
