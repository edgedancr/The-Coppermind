> [!summary]
> **Metaphors for intelligence:** illustrations of the intelligence process, some better than others, including connecting the dots, assembling a picture piece by piece, assembling a mosaic, and a string of pearls.[^1] The 9/11 Commission called labels like "connecting the dots" too narrow: they "describe the symptoms, not the disease".[^2]

**Used in:** [[National Counterterrorism Center]], [[Intelligence (National Security)]]
