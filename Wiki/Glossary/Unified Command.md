> [!summary]
> **Unified command:** the 9/11 Commission's recommended arrangement when several agencies or jurisdictions respond together, a single command that "comprehensively deploys all dispatched police, fire, and other first responder resources".[^1] It recommended that agencies adopt it alongside the [[Incident Command System]], calling both "proven frameworks for emergency response".[^2]

**Used in:** [[Emergency Response on September 11 (2001)]]
