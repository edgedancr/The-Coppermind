> [!summary]
> **National security letter (NSL):** a type of administrative subpoena: an investigative technique that requires "recipients to turn over records and data pertaining to individuals," with a gag order that bars the recipient from revealing its contents or even that it exists (Lowenthal, L: 223, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 5]]).

**Used in:** [[Counterintelligence]]
