> [!summary]
> **Backpropagation:** the algorithm used in training to tweak all of a model's [[Parameter|parameters]] so it becomes a little more likely to give the right answer and a little less likely to give the others ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 4]]). For a language model, the "right answer" is the true last word of a training example ([[3Blue1Brown - Large Language Models Explained Briefly (transcript)|3Blue1Brown, LLMs, ch. 4]]).

**Used in:** [[Large Language Model]], [[Fine-Tuning]]
