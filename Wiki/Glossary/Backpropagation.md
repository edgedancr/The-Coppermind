> [!summary]
> **Backpropagation:** the algorithm used in training to tweak all of a model's [[Parameter|parameters]] so it becomes a little more likely to give the right answer and a little less likely to give the others.[^1] For a language model, the "right answer" is the true last word of a training example.[^1]

**Used in:** [[Large Language Model]], [[Fine-Tuning]]
