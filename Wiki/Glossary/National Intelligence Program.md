> [!summary]
> **National Intelligence Program (NIP):** the budget program that funds several civilian intelligence agencies and departments under the [[Director of National Intelligence]].[^1] In the course's answer on congressional oversight, the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence oversees the NIP agencies.[^2] In 2004 the 9/11 Commission found most intelligence funding hidden in the Defense Department's budget, and recommended making the top-line amounts public and appropriating the national intelligence program to a National Intelligence Director rather than the secretary of defense.[^3]

**Used in:** [[Congressional Oversight of Intelligence]], [[Military Intelligence Program]], [[Intelligence Oversight Committees]]; compare [[Executive Order 12333]], [[Crosswalks]]
