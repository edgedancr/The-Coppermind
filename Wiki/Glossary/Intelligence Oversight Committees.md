> [!summary]
> **Intelligence oversight committees:** the two congressional committees over U.S. intelligence: the **House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (HPSCI)** and the **Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI)**.[^1] In the course's discussion answer citing Lowenthal, the SSCI has exclusive oversight of the [[Director of National Intelligence|DNI]] and the CIA and assesses U.S. intelligence capabilities to monitor treaties; HPSCI oversees the [[National Intelligence Program]] agencies; and the Senate and House Armed Services Committees oversee the Defense Department's intelligence agencies and the [[Military Intelligence Program]].[^2] Lowenthal describes oversight as committees or groups making sure fair and legal actions are taken by other, lower-level individuals or groups.[^3] The 9/11 Commission notes that Congress created the select committees in 1977, after the Church and Pike investigations.[^4]

**Used in:** [[Congressional Oversight of Intelligence]], [[Church and Pike Committees]], [[United States Intelligence Community]]; compare [[Gang of Eight]], [[Verification (Arms Control)]]
