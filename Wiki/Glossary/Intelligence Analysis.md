> [!summary]
> **Intelligence analysis:** in Rob Johnston's working definition, "the application of individual and collective cognitive methods to weigh data and test hypotheses within a secret socio-cultural context".[^1] He stresses its social side: analysts typically begin by reading up on "the [[Corporate Judgment|analytic line]]" in previous products, then negotiate meaning with peers and supervisors,[^2] so the cognitive side of analysis cannot be separated from its cultural context.[^3] Surveying definitions from [[Sherman Kent]]'s to [[Richards Heuer]]'s ("fundamentally a mental process"), he concludes that analysis is "the socio-cognitive process, occurring within a secret domain, by which a collection of methods is used to reduce a complex issue to a set of simpler issues".[^4]

**Used in:** [[Rob Johnston]], [[Taxonomy of Intelligence Analysis Variables]]; compare [[Intelligence (National Security)]], [[Analytic Culture]]
