> [!summary]
> **SEE-It:** a four-step way to define a term, taught in Intro to Intelligence: **state** it (a clear, formal definition), **elaborate** it (restate it "in other words"), **exemplify** it (give an example), and **illustrate** it (paint a picture) ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|Intro notes, 9/1/26]]). The course's handout calls the last step "Illustrate It" and gives a starter for each step: "The question or topic is…", "In other words…", "For example…", "It is like…". It suggests using SEE-IT to develop each main idea in a paper and to show a grasp of concepts on tests ([[SRC - SEE-IT Checklist for Critical Thinking|SEE-IT Checklist, 'Clarifying with SEE-IT']]).
