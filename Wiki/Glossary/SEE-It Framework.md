> [!summary]
> **SEE-It:** a four-step way to define a term, taught in Intro to Intelligence: **state** it (a clear, formal definition), **elaborate** it (restate it "in other words"), **exemplify** it (give an example), and **illustrate** it (paint a picture) ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/1/26|Intro notes, 9/1/26]]).
