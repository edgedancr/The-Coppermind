> [!summary]
> **Supplementals:** special one-year appropriations by Congress, above the general budget, for agreed-upon priorities. Unlike the general budget, they are not long-term commitments (Lowenthal, L: 64/55, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
