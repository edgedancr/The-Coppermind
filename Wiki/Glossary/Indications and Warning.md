> [!summary]
> **Indications and warning (I&W):** intelligence meant to warn of developments in time to act. Rob Johnston argues that current reporting competes with I&W for time and resources, because I&W requires long-term research, in-depth expertise, scientific methods, and continuous performance improvement.[^1] ==Successful warnings are often taken for granted, while failures cost the Intelligence Community social capital==.[^1]
