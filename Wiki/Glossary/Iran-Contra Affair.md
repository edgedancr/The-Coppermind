> [!summary]
> **Iran-Contra affair:** a mid-1980s scheme by National Security Council staff to trade U.S. arms to Iran for hostages and divert the proceeds to the Nicaraguan Contras, around U.S. law; the 9/11 Commission calls it "a massive abuse of covert action." It notes that the affair made parts of the bureaucracy "reflexively skeptical about any operating directive from the White House" ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=116|9/11 Report, p. 99]]).
