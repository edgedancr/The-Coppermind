> [!summary]
> **Meaningful absence:** evidence that was looked for and not found ("we looked and it wasn't there"), as distinct from an information gap, where nobody has looked ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 0]]). Step 2 labels these `[Absent — meaningful]` and `[Absent — gap]`, and Step 3 says absent items should be rated Inconsistent with hypotheses that would predict their presence ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Steps 2–3]]). Step 4 asks whether a hypothesis is being kept alive only by missing evidence, which is not evidence in its favor ([[SRC - ACH GenAI Workflow|ACH Workflow, Step 4]]).
