> [!summary]
> **Insider threat:** many of the United States' greatest spies or traitors were homegrown, so insider threats are a reality in counterintelligence. A 2011 executive order by President Obama created the Insider Threat Program to deter, detect, and mitigate insider threats while improving security and allowing classified information to be shared.[^1]
