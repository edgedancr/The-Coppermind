> [!summary]
> **AI technology stack:** the layers AI development depends on, which Chan et al. trace "from chips to applications" ([[RAND - Full Stack, China's Evolving Industrial Policy for AI (2025).pdf|Chan et al., "opening"]]). Their survey of China's policy covers energy, chips, computing infrastructure, research and talent, data, and applications ([[RAND - Full Stack, China's Evolving Industrial Policy for AI (2025).pdf|Chan et al., "China's Layered State Support for AI"]]), along with software such as AI frameworks ([[RAND - Full Stack, China's Evolving Industrial Policy for AI (2025).pdf|Chan et al., "U.S. Export Controls to Constrain China's Compute"]]). Mitre and Predd describe U.S. strategy as seeking to keep its lead over China in core components of the AI tech stack ([[RAND - Artificial General Intelligence's Five Hard National Security Problems (2025).pdf|Mitre & Predd, "Toward a Robust Strategy"]]).
