> **DIME:** in an Intro to Intelligence guest lecture, James Diehl presented the options for "defeating a villain": **diplomatic** (slow, boring), **information** (spreading information to ruin their reputation), **military** (conventional and special operations), and **economic** (sanctions and tariffs), with digital warfare as a possible fifth.[^1]
