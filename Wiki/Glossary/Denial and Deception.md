> [!summary]
> **Denial:** trying to prevent people from "seeing" something that is present, as with encryption, refusing to comment, or camouflage. **Deception:** attempting to convey anything besides the whole truth, as with made-up alibis ([[Intro to Intelligence Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/17/26|Intro notes, 9/17/26]]).
