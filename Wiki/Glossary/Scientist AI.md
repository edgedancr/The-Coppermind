> [!summary]
> **Scientist AI:** a non-agentic AI proposed by [[Yoshua Bengio]]'s team, modeled on "a selfless, ideal scientist who's only trying to understand the world," unlike current systems trained to imitate or please us ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 9]]). Bengio says it could serve as a guardrail against an untrusted AI agent's bad actions, because predicting that an action is dangerous takes good, trustworthy predictions, not agency; it could also speed up scientific research ([[TED - Yoshua Bengio, The Catastrophic Risks of AI and a Safer Path (transcript)|Bengio, ch. 9]]).
