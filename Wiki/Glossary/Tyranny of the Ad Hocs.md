> **Tyranny of the ad hocs:** the way surprise issues get placed at a higher priority, diverting attention from work already in progress; a special national intelligence estimate (SNIE) is one example (Lowenthal, L: 72/62, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
