> [!summary]
> **Memorandum of Notification (MON):** a legal document authorizing a covert action, such as the 1998 draft authorizing a capture operation against Bin Ladin ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=130|9/11 Report, p. 113]]); the CIA must bring presidentially authorized covert action findings and memoranda of notification to the congressional intelligence committees ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=120|9/11 Report, p. 103]]).
