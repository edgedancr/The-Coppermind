> [!summary]
> **Plausible deniability:** in the 9/11 Commission's account of the Church and Pike investigations, CIA officials took most of the blame for plots against foreign leaders, "saying they had done so in order to preserve the President's 'plausible deniability'"; the President had not taken plain responsibility for the decisions ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=107|9/11 Report, p. 90]]).

**Used in:** [[Hughes-Ryan Amendment]]; compare [[Church and Pike Committees]]
