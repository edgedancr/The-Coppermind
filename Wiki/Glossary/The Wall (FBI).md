> [!summary]
> **"The Wall":** the FBI's separate rules for domestic law enforcement (court warrants and the like) and for counterterrorism surveillance requests, which required approval from the [[Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court]]. As a result, ==intelligence could not be freely shared between the FBI's law enforcement and counterterrorism elements== (9/11 Commission Report, pp. 78–79, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 3]]). Writing in 2004, the 9/11 Commission said the removal of "the wall" had opened up new opportunities for cooperative action within the FBI ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=441|9/11 Report, p. 424]]). The 9/11 Commission calls the term "misleading," because several factors led to a series of barriers to information sharing ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=96|9/11 Report, p. 79]]). See [[Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act]].
