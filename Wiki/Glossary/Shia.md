> [!summary]
> **Shia:** the branch of Islam whose followers held, after the Prophet Mohammed's death, that any leader of the Muslim community (the Ummah) must be a direct descendant of the Prophet; the Shia are dominant in Iran.[^1] Iran's 1979 revolution "swept a Shia theocracy into power",[^2] and the 9/11 Commission found that al-Qaeda's relationship with Iran showed that [[Sunni]]-Shia divisions "did not necessarily pose an insurmountable barrier" to cooperation in terrorist operations.[^3]

**Used in:** [[Sunni]]; compare [[Al-Qaeda]]
