> [!summary]
> **National technical means:** a nation's technological methods and tools for collecting intelligence, such as satellites, not usually operated by a tactical military organization (Lowenthal ch. 2, as summarized in [[SRC - Grammar Summaries, Weeks 2-7|Grammar, wk 2]]).
