> [!summary]
> **LMSYS Chatbot Arena:** described in class as the current gold standard for comparing chatbots, by anonymously crowdsourcing judgments of which chatbot is better ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/17/26|SAIL notes, 9/17/26]]). It reflects the point that traditional software gives objective data while AI gives subjective data ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/17/26|SAIL notes, 9/17/26]]).

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> My notes say it uses "sentiment analysis." It may instead work by people voting between two anonymous responses, and its name may have changed. Check both before verifying.
