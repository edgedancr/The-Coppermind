> [!summary]
> **LMSYS Chatbot Arena:** described in class as the current gold standard for comparing chatbots, by anonymously crowdsourcing judgments of which chatbot is better.[^1] It reflects the point that traditional software gives objective data while AI gives subjective data.[^1]

**Now called Arena:** Arena's own "About Us" page says the platform was created by researchers from UC Berkeley, is formerly LMArena and was previously known as Chatbot Arena, and that Arena Intelligence Inc. was formed in 2025.[^2] Arena says the platform became known in the AI industry as "the gold standard of evaluation",[^2] and it reports $250 million in venture funding, led by Felicis and UC Investments.[^3]

> [!caution] Accuracy check
> My notes say it uses "sentiment analysis." It may instead work by people voting between two anonymous responses, and its name may have changed. Check both before verifying.
