> [!summary]
> **LMSYS Chatbot Arena (Arena):** described in class as the current gold standard for comparing chatbots, by anonymously crowdsourcing judgments of which chatbot is better.[^1] It reflects the point that traditional software gives objective data while AI gives subjective data.[^1] Arena says the platform became known in the AI industry as "the gold standard of evaluation",[^2] and it reports $250 million in venture funding, led by Felicis and UC Investments.[^3]

**Used in:** [[Artificial Intelligence]]
