> [!summary]
> **LMSYS Chatbot Arena:** described in class as the current gold standard for comparing chatbots, by anonymously crowdsourcing judgments of which chatbot is better ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/17/26|SAIL notes, 9/17/26]]). It reflects the point that traditional software gives objective data while AI gives subjective data ([[SAIL Class Notes (Fall 2026)#9/17/26|SAIL notes, 9/17/26]]).

**Now called Arena:** Arena's own "About Us" page says the platform was created by researchers from UC Berkeley, is formerly LMArena and was previously known as Chatbot Arena, and that Arena Intelligence Inc. was formed in 2025 ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Our Story"]]). Arena says the platform became known in the AI industry as "the gold standard of evaluation" ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Our Story"]]), and it reports $250 million in venture funding, led by Felicis and UC Investments ([[SRC - About Us (Arena)|Arena, About Us, "Backed by world class investors"]]).
