> [!summary]
> **Delenda Plan:** Richard Clarke's 1998 "Political-Military Plan Delenda," named for the Roman vow that Carthage "must be destroyed"; it aimed to "immediately eliminate any significant threat to Americans" from the Bin Ladin network through diplomacy, covert action to capture Bin Ladin, cutting off his money, and follow-on military strikes. It was never formally adopted by the principals ([[National Commission on Terrorist Attacks - The 9-11 Commission Report (2004).pdf#page=137|9/11 Report, p. 120]]).

**Used in:** [[August 1998 Missile Strikes]], [[September 11 Attacks (2001)]]; compare [[The Plan (CIA)]]
